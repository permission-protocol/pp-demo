## 💡 Pro Tips for Text-Based Truth or Dare

### For Truths:
• Ask follow-up questions to dig deeper
• Share your own answer after they respond
• Use their response to learn something new
• Keep the conversation flowing naturally

### For Dares:
• Make sure they're actually doable via text
• Be creative with photo and voice message challenges
• Don't ask for anything you wouldn't do yourself
• Keep it fun and light-hearted

### General Tips:
• Respect boundaries always
• Save the best moments with screenshots (with permission)
• Gradually increase intensity throughout the game
• Have backup questions ready
• Know when to take breaks
