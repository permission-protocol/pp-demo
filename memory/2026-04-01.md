## Pricing Strategy Session (10:19 AM – 12:31 PM MDT)

### Context
Rod opened a #pricing channel in Discord to brainstorm the optimal pricing section for the website redesign. Worked through multiple design iterations with two designers via Stitch.withGoogle.

### Key Decisions
- **4-tier structure finalized:** Developer (Free) → Team ($79/org/mo) → Production ($299/org/mo) → Enterprise (Custom)
- **Annual billing:** 20% off ($59 Team, $239 Production) with "SAVE 25%" toggle
- **Pricing unit:** Per-org, NOT per-seat or per-repo. Repos are packaging caps, not the pricing primitive.
- **No receipt quotas:** Hidden metering contradicts the transparency brand. Handle overuse via sales outreach.
- **Free tier:** 3 protected repos, unlimited signers — generous enough for habit formation.
- **Team tier at $79 (not $49):** $49 is a future experiment if conversion is soft after 60 days.
- **Enterprise as full-width callout** below the 3 main cards, not a 4th equal column.

### Design Process
- Reviewed 3 initial drafts → identified $0→$300 cliff as the main bug
- Spec'd full pricing breakdown from market segmentation (4 buyer segments)
- Sent designer instructions for both light and dark theme versions
- Reviewed two designer iterations, provided specific feedback (missing toggle, price mismatches)
- Compared two full website redesign versions → picked Version 2 (tighter, better conversion for cold LinkedIn traffic)
- Stole Version 1's visceral hero hook ("12,847 user records") and "Execution does not proceed" callout

### Strategic Insights
- **"Price for the buyer, not the company size"** — A 6-person startup and 60-person org both have 10 repos; what changes is who owns the problem.
- **Don't tax the safe action** — Per-receipt pricing penalizes compliance.
- **Unlimited signers on Free** is a killer differentiator vs. competitors that gate on seats immediately.
- **"Request Access" for Production CTA** — At $299/mo, you want a conversation, not self-serve tire-kickers.
- **60-day calibration plan:** Track free→team conversion, repo cap hits, signer additions. Only adjust pricing if data warrants it.
