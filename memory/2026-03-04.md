# 2026-03-04 — PP Strategic Pivot Session

## What Happened
Rod shared 17 strategic documents defining PP's vision as "Authority Infrastructure" — the Stripe of AI authority. Documents cover doctrine, architecture (issuer vs verifier), positioning, competitive analysis, GTM (SDK wedge), distribution (shareable receipts), and roadmap.

Also reviewed Crittora's "Agent Permission Protocol" (APP) whitepaper earlier in the day — they're playing the standards/spec game in the same space. No shipping product yet.

## Key Decisions
- **Positioning shift:** From "deploy gate" / "governance platform" → "Signer of Record for AI systems" / "Authority must exist before execution"
- **Core primitive:** AuthorityReceipt — every approval produces a signed, shareable artifact
- **Fundraising signal:** Developer adoption + receipt volume. Show investors real developers generating real receipts.
- **Cut everything 12+ months out.** No federation, no authority networks, no enterprise platform. Focus on the primitive.

## 30-Day Plan (Agreed Direction)
1. **Receipt page** — `permissionprotocol.com/r/xxx` public URL for every receipt. Beautiful, one screen, signature verified.
2. **Homepage rewrite** — "AI agents shouldn't authorize their own actions" positioning
3. **Python SDK** — `@require_approval` decorator, open source, generates receipts with live URLs

## Strategic Insights
- PP already does the core thing (blocks without approval, signs receipts). Problem is packaging, not capability.
- Every shareable receipt URL = distribution event. The product advertises itself.
- Deploy gate is the first enforcement point, not the whole product. SDK generalizes it.
- Crittora: policies (what's allowed) vs PP: receipts (what was actually authorized). Receipts > policies for compliance/accountability.
- Cloud providers (AWS/GCP/Azure) are the real competitive threat, not startups. PP wins by being cross-platform.
- "AI agents shouldn't authorize their own actions" — the one-liner that opens every conversation.

## Rod's Priorities
- Simplify the message
- Beautiful shareable receipt URLs (viral potential)
- Developer adoption as fundraising metric
- Doesn't care about $50B narratives or 12-month plans — wants the next 90 days to matter
