# Gravitee State of AI Agent Security 2026 — Quotable Stats
**Source:** https://www.gravitee.io/blog/state-of-ai-agent-security-2026-report-when-adoption-outpaces-control  
**Survey:** 900+ executives and technical practitioners  
**Compiled:** 2026-03-04 for issue #252

---

## Top Stats (Use in Outreach + NIST RFI)

### Adoption vs. Governance Gap
- **80.9%** of technical teams have moved past planning into active testing or production AI agents
- **Only 14.4%** of organizations have full security/IT approval for their entire agent fleet
  - *Hook: "6 in 7 orgs are running unapproved agents in production right now."*
- **47.1%** — average share of an organization's AI agents that are actively monitored or secured
  - *Hook: "More than half your agents are invisible to your security team."*
- **>50%** of all AI agents operate without any security oversight or logging

### The Confidence Paradox
- **82%** of executives feel confident their existing policies protect them from unauthorized agent actions
- But only **14.4%** have full approval coverage — a **67-point gap** between executive confidence and actual control
  - *Hook: "Your CEO thinks you're covered. Your agents disagree."*

### Incidents Are Already Happening
- **88%** of organizations reported confirmed or suspected AI agent security incidents in the past year
- Healthcare sector: **92.7%** reported incidents
  - *Hook: "AI agent security incidents aren't coming. They're already in your last 12 months."*

### Identity Is the Weakest Link
- **Only 21.9%** of teams treat AI agents as independent, identity-bearing entities
- **45.6%** of teams still rely on shared API keys for agent-to-agent authentication
- **27.2%** of technical teams have reverted to custom, hardcoded logic to manage agent authorization
- **25.5%** of deployed agents can create and task other agents — with no chain-of-command audit trail

## NIST RFI Integration Suggestion

> A 2026 survey of 900+ executives and practitioners (Gravitee, "State of AI Agent Security 2026") found that while 80.9% of technical teams have deployed AI agents into active testing or production, only 14.4% have full security/IT approval for their agent fleet. More than half of all deployed agents operate without any security oversight or logging. 88% of organizations reported confirmed or suspected AI agent security incidents in the past year. These figures validate that the authorization vacuum is not a theoretical concern — it is an already-operational failure mode affecting the majority of enterprises deploying AI agents today.
