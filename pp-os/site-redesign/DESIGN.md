This document describes the foundational design choices for our product, ensuring consistency and coherence across all user interfaces.

## Theme Overview

Our theme is set to `dark` mode, providing a sophisticated and comfortable viewing experience, especially in low-light environments. The primary color palette is directly defined, offering precise control over the brand's aesthetic.

## Colors

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Color:** `#10B981` (Green) - Used for primary actions, interactive elements, and key brand accents.
- **Secondary Color:** `#44AA99` (Teal) - Supports the primary color for less prominent UI elements and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary Color:** `#1E2B29` (Dark Teal/Green) - An additional accent for highlights, badges, or decorative elements, providing depth to the palette.
- **Neutral Color:** `#0A0A0A` (Deep Black) - Serves as the base for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements, emphasizing the vibrant primary colors in dark mode.

### Named Colors
(No named colors are currently explicitly defined, indicating a reliance on the core palette.)

## Typography

- **Headline Font:** `Space Grotesk` - Chosen for its modern and distinctive character, making headlines impactful and legible.
- **Body Font:** `Inter` - A highly readable and versatile sans-serif font, ideal for long-form content and general UI text.
- **Label Font:** `Space Grotesk` - Consistency with headlines ensures a cohesive typographic hierarchy and brand voice for labels.

## Shape and Spacing

- **Roundedness:** `1` (Subtle Roundedness) - Corners of UI elements have a slight curve, offering a modern yet approachable feel without being overly soft.
- **Spacing:** `2` (Normal) - Provides a balanced amount of whitespace, ensuring readability and comfortable interaction without feeling too dense or overly spacious.
