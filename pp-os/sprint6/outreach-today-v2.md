- "I'm the founder of" → **"I'm building something called"** (Learning voice).
- Removed diagnostic claims about *their* world.
- Casual peer-to-peer phrasing ("a small layer that...", "basically so that...").
- First ask is **"5 minutes to look and react"**, NOT "15 min screen share + install".
- Explicit gratitude at every close.
