### 2) Best Submission Timing Recommendations

- **Day:** Weekday (Tuesday or Wednesday)
- **Time:** Early morning (~7:00 AM EST) or late afternoon (~4:00 PM EST)
- **Rationale:** These times typically see higher engagement on Hacker News as users start their day or take breaks. Avoid weekends when traffic is lower and Monday mornings when the feed is crowded with weekend submissions.

### 3) Should This Be "Show HN" or Regular Post?

- **Recommendation:** Regular post
- **Reasoning:** "Show HN" is typically for showcasing projects, libraries, or tools. Since this is an opinion piece/analysis, a regular submission is more appropriate. It allows for discussion and debate in the comments.

### 4) First Comment Strategy

**Example:**
"I wrote this series after leaving Vercel due to a billing dispute, but the recent security breach brought it all back into focus. The common thread between both issues is the architecture that prioritizes convenience over safety. I hope this serves as a cautionary tale for anyone evaluating cloud platforms."

- **Goal:** Provide context without sounding promotional.
- **Tone:** Neutral and informative.
