### Task 1: Company Categories Ranked by Urgency

1. **Fintech**: High regulatory demands and frequent deployments make security critical.
2. **Healthtech/Medtech**: Compliance with HIPAA and handling sensitive data are paramount.
3. **Cybersecurity Companies**: Already focused on security, adding AI deployment security is logical.
4. **Government Tech Contractors**: Must comply with FedRAMP and other regulations.
5. **E-commerce Platforms**: Protect customer data and ensure secure deployments.
6. **DevOps/Cloud Native Startups**: Focused on efficient CI/CD pipelines.
7. **AI/ML Platform Providers**: Securing their own AI deployments is essential.
8. **Regulated Industries (e.g., Energy)**: Strict compliance needs with security concerns.
9. **Telecom**: Regulated industry with significant security requirements.
10. **EdTech**: Handling student data and needing FERPA compliance.
