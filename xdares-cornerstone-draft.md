## How Paid Dares Work

The concept is simple:

1. **Fan submits a dare** with payment attached
2. **Creator accepts** (or declines — boundaries matter)
3. **Creator completes the dare** and posts proof
4. **Platform verifies** and releases payment
5. **Everyone wins** — fan gets content, creator gets paid

The key innovation is *commitment infrastructure*: escrowed payments, time limits, and verification systems that make the whole thing trustworthy.
