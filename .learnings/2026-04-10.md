## The Lessons

### 1. Parallel Codex is Mature
**Problem:** Need to apply 12 discrete design changes across the entire app. Manual sequential execution would take all day.
**Pattern:** Parallel execution with `git worktree` and multiple Codex agents. 
**Fix:** Adopt the "Parallel Batch Pattern" as a standard for systemic UI upgrades. 12 PRs in 3h is the new benchmark.

### 2. Outreach Drought Requires Narrative Shift
**Problem:** Day 49 of zero installs. Traditional "ask for time" outreach is stalling.
**Pattern:** Stagnation in outreach often means the hook is too technical or low-stakes. 
**Fix:** Shift narrative toward "Safety over Crypto." Focus on the consequence of unauthorized AI actions (The Consequence Engine) rather than the mechanism (receipts).

### 3. Memory & Workspace Hygiene
**Problem:** Memory folder cluttered with 2+ months of daily notes. Workspace getting "heavy."
**Pattern:** LLM context focus degrades when too many unrelated files are present in simple `ls` or `find` searches.
**Fix:** Implement a tiered archive system. Daily notes older than 7 days moved to `memory/archived/`.
