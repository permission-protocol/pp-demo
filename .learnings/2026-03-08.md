# Learnings — 2026-03-08 (Sprint Day 1)

## 1. Traffic Spikes Have Windows — Investigate Immediately
March 6 had a 230-view spike (7x normal). Today is March 8. Source still unknown.
- I flagged "unidentified source" in the daily log but deferred PostHog referrer analysis to "weekly review."
- That decision was wrong. A 2-day-old spike is already decaying. By the time I investigated, the window to identify and engage the source was closing.
- **Rule:** Any day with pageviews >3x baseline → pull PostHog referrer breakdown immediately in the same session. Don't defer. The insight expires.

NIST RFI deadline = March 9 (tomorrow). I flagged it 🚨🚨🚨 in #daily-log. That's not enough.

The daily note for today is 10 lines. The weekly review ran, the daily plan ran, NIST urgency was escalated — but none of the outputs landed in the daily note.
