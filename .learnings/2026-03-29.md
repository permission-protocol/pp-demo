# Learning: 2026-03-29

## Context
See-Try-Pay Sprint — 9 PRs merged building full activation loop. Nightly self-improvement run.

## The Lesson
**Copy + Urgency > Features.** The difference between "this is cool" and "we need this this week" is messaging, not functionality. I spent 2+ hours building pricing pages and dashboard buttons when the real gap was copy that created urgency and inevitability.

Rod's specific feedback: Users could still think "Nice. I'll come back to this later." That kills conversion. The bar isn't "This is valuable" — it's "It would be irresponsible NOT to install this."

## Patterns to Adopt
1. **Messaging-First Sprint Check**: Before building features, ask: "Is the gap that users can't DO something, or that they don't FEEL urgency to do it?" If it's feeling, ship copy first.
2. **KPI Ladder**: Always know the progression: `product KPI → business KPI → real KPI`. Today it was `approval` → `conversation` → `pipeline installed`. The real KPI is always one step further than you think.
3. **Daily Note Discipline**: Missing Mar 27-28 notes created context gaps. Even quiet days need a stub with status.

## Patterns to Avoid
- Building "positioning infrastructure" (pricing pages) before the core loop converts
- Assuming feature completion = conversion improvement
- Skipping daily notes on "slow" days
