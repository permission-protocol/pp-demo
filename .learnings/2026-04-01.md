# Learning: 2026-04-01

## Context
Week 4, Day 3. 3 days to sprint deadline. Massive push on Pricing, Mobile UX, and Demo Polish. Nightly self-improvement run.

## The Lessons

### 1. Visual QA is the Final Mile
**Problem:** Codex implemented the redesigned demo and mobile UX in one pass, but minor alignment/density issues only appeared in the live Vercel preview.
**Pattern:** "Code complete" does not mean "Product complete." The gap is always in the visual density and tonal consistency.
**Fix:** Maintain the `scripts/pre-merge-review.sh` workflow. It's the only way to ensure the "Authority" vibe is maintained on mobile.

### 2. Pricing is Packaging, Not Just Numbers
**Problem:** Initial pricing cliff ($0 to $300) was a strategic bug.
**Pattern:** Buyer segmentation (Developer vs Team vs Production) matters more than feature gating. Gating on "signers" was a mistake; gating on "repos" (packaging unit) aligns better with organizational value.
**Fix:** 4-tier model with per-org pricing. $79 "Team" tier provides a bridge to the $299 "Production" tier.

### 3. Cron Decay is High
**Problem:** 16 crons are failing (rate limits + channel config).
**Pattern:** Autonomous operations decay without proactive maintenance. "Unsupported channel: discord" is a configuration drift issue.
**Fix:** I need a dedicated `scripts/cron-maintenance.sh` or similar to dry-run cron payloads and verify channel connectivity before they fail in isolation.

## Top 3 Improvements
1. **Blocker Aging Escalation**: Added "(X days)" to STATUS.md. Needs automated incrementing.
2. **Cron Health Dashboard**: Create a script to summarize cron failures and channel status in a single view.
3. **Visual Density Baseline**: Define standard vertical rhythm constants (pt-12, pb-10) in `AGENTS.md` to avoid iterative density "fixes."

## Patterns to Adopt
- **Packaging over Gating**: Pricing should reflect the buyer's role, not just usage metrics.
- **Immediate Patching**: The "userTime" cache fix saved thousands in potential burn. Don't wait for "proper" refactors when money is leaking.

## Patterns to Avoid
- **Implicit Blockers**: Listing a blocker without a "last tried" date or age.
- **Manual Pricing**: Trying to "meter" things like receipts which contradicts a transparency brand.
