## Core Principles

- Rules on paper are not enforcement.
- Authority must exist in the execution path — not in a policy doc, not in a contract, not in a prompt.
- Intelligence without explicit authority is dangerous.
- Decision is not authorization.
- Approval after execution is not governance. It is cleanup.
- If an action matters, permission must be explicit.
- If accountability matters, proof must be created before the action — not reconstructed after.
- The system that acts cannot be the system that authorizes itself.
- Promises break the moment a system can act faster than a human can intervene.
- Between decision and execution, there must be authority.
- You can't patch an architecture decision.
- A system prompt that says "be careful" is not security.
- The drone doesn't read contracts.

## Post Archetypes

### 1. Reframe
**Use when:** Public debate is focused on the wrong question.
**Structure:** Everyone's debating X → wrong question → real issue is Y → example → broader implication → doctrine
**Example:** Pentagon-Anthropic post (contracts vs. control)

### 2. Doctrine
**Use when:** Stating a sharp structural principle.
**Structure:** Strong claim → why most people miss it → example → doctrine line
**Best for:** Category definition, authority building

### 3. Breakdown
**Use when:** Explaining a complex failure mode clearly.
**Structure:** What happened → how it worked → why it's worse than it sounds → what it means for everyone
**Example:** MS-Agent RCE post (CVE breakdown)

### 4. Prediction
**Use when:** Naming what happens next.
**Structure:** Current signal → what this leads to → why people underestimate it → implication
**Best for:** Investor signaling, thought leadership

### 5. Founder Lens
**Use when:** Sharing what building the company revealed.
**Structure:** Common assumption → what building taught me → structural insight → implication
**Best for:** Recruiting, founder credibility

### 6. Category Creation
**Use when:** Introducing the missing layer.
**Structure:** What exists today → why it fails → what layer is missing → why it becomes inevitable
**Best for:** Product narrative, market positioning
