## Outreach Angle Per Tier

**Tier 1 (Agent builders):** "Your users' AI agents are making PRs. Who approves the deploy? We add a cryptographic receipt gate so nothing ships without a signer of record."

**Tier 2 (DevTools):** "Your platform accelerates shipping. We add the governance layer — every deploy gets a signed receipt. Integration opportunity."

**Tier 3 (CI/CD users):** "You're shipping AI-written code. Deploy Gate adds a 3-step check: no receipt → no merge. 5 minutes to install."

**Tier 4 (Compliance):** "AI is generating production code now. Your customers need audit trails for who approved each deploy. PP receipts are that artifact."
