## 1. What Xdares Is

**One-liner:** The dare-based creator platform where fans dare, creators prove it on video, and money moves automatically.

**Tagline:** "Prove it. Get paid."

**Category:** 18+ creator economy / dare arena

**How it works:**
1. **Fans dare and fund** — create a dare, set the reward, put real money in escrow
2. **Creators prove it in-app** — accept the dare, record proof inside Xdares (no pre-recorded uploads)
3. **Money moves automatically** — escrow releases when the dare is confirmed

**Wedge:** "Social media is boring because everyone's faking it. Xdares is where people actually do something — and get paid for it."

**18+ positioning:** "18+ only. Real money. Real boundaries." — confident, not apologetic.

## 5. Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:** Almost none. GetDare (now defunct) was the closest — Xdares positions as "the GetDare alternative" and captures that search traffic.

**Adjacent platforms:**
- **OnlyFans** — creator monetization, but passive content. Xdares is action-based.
- **TikTok/YouTube** — video content, but no money mechanics built in. Xdares has escrow.
- **Cameo** — paid interactions, but scripted. Xdares is live proof.

**Positioning vs all:** Xdares is the only platform where the *audience designs the content* (via dares) and *money is escrowed before action*. That's the structural moat.
