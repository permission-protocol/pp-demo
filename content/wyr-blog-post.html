🎉 Bonus: How to Play Would You Rather

Playing would you rather is simple, but here are some tips to make it more fun:

Basic Rules

  1. One person asks a "Would you rather" question with two options
  2. Everyone must choose one option - no skipping or saying "neither"!
  3. Explain your reasoning for even more interesting discussions
  4. Take turns asking questions

Variations to Try

  • Speed Round: Answer in 3 seconds or less - no overthinking!
  • Debate Mode: Two people with different answers must convince the group
  • Dare Integration: The minority answer has to do a dare - check out Xdares for dare ideas!
  • Anonymous Voting: Everyone writes their answer, then reveal simultaneously

When to Play

  • Road trips and long car rides
  • Waiting rooms and lines
  • First dates to break the ice
  • Family dinners
  • Team building at work
  • Sleepovers and parties
