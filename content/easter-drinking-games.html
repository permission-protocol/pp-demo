20+ Easter Egg Hunt Drinking Games for Adults (2026 Edition)

Easter isn't just for kids anymore. Whether you're hosting a backyard bash or a cozy indoor gathering, these adult-themed Easter egg hunt drinking games will take your holiday to the next level. Remember to drink responsibly and stay hydrated!

1. The "Mini Bar" Hunt

Forget the chocolate. Fill plastic eggs with those tiny 50ml airplane bottles of liquor. If you find one, you get to keep it—but you have to take a communal "toast" shot with the group first.

2. Truth or Dare Eggs

Each egg contains a slip of paper with a "Truth" or a "Dare." If you refuse the prompt, you must take a shot. If you complete it, you can assign a drink to someone else.

3. Never Have I Ever: Easter Edition

Fill eggs with "Never Have I Ever" prompts. When an egg is found, the finder reads it aloud. Anyone who has done the deed must take a sip of their drink.

4. The Golden Egg Penalty

Hide one Golden Egg. The person who finds it wins a prize (like a premium bottle), but every other player must finish their current drink in "honor" of the winner.

5. Color-Coded Chaos

Assign each player a specific egg color. If you find someone else's color, you must take a drink and leave the egg where it is. You can only collect your own!

6. Bunny Hop Relay

Players must hop like a bunny to find an egg. If they drop their "basket" or stop hopping, they must take a "penalty sip" before continuing.

7. Boozy Jelly Bean Count

Fill an egg with a specific number of jelly beans. The finder guesses the number. For every number they are off, they take that many seconds of a "waterfall" drink.

8. Reverse Hunt

Hide empty eggs. For every empty egg found, the person who hid them has to drink. If an egg is never found by the end of the timer, the seekers all drink.

9. The "Social" Egg

Hide eggs that simply say "SOCIAL" inside. When found, everyone yells "Social!" and takes a drink together.

10. Riddler's Revenge

Eggs contain riddles. Solve it in 10 seconds or drink. Solve it correctly, and you can pick someone else to drink.
