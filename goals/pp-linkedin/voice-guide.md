# PP LinkedIn Voice Guide

## Tone
- Calm, precise, slightly provocative
- Architect-level thinking, not preaching
- Intellectually uncomfortable, not emotionally loud
- "Damn… that's true" is the target reaction

## Structure (Doctrine Smuggling)
1. **Concrete operational scenario** — make it real, shipping-level
2. **Subtle structural tension** — the uncomfortable question
3. **Light architectural insight** — one sentence of doctrine, not a manifesto
4. **Optional deep link** — only for serious builders

## Do
- Start with relatable shipping scenarios
- Ask questions that expose gaps
- Use terms: "signer of record", "external authority", "separation of powers"
- Keep posts under 200 words
- End with tension, not answers

## Don't
- Lead with abstract governance doctrine
- Use "AI safety" or "alignment" framing
- Sound like a pitch or product announcement
- Chase trending AI hype
- Use hashtags excessively (1-2 max, or none)
- Say "we built" or "check out our product"

## Audience
- Developers shipping AI tools
- Founders building with agents
- VCs evaluating AI companies
- DevOps/infra leads running agents in CI

## Formats
- **Monday:** Operational risk scenario (short, punchy)
- **Wednesday:** Architecture breakdown (slightly longer)
- **Friday:** Founder/VC angle (liability, defensibility)

## Example Hooks
- "Your AI agent just deployed to prod. Who signed it?"
- "If your AI tool can merge code, where does authority live?"
- "Logs are self-reporting. That's not governance."
