## Usage Notes & Personalization Tips

### When to Use Each Template:

**Guest Post Pitch:**
- Target blogs that accept guest content
- Use when you have relevant expertise to share
- Best for building authority and earning quality backlinks

**Resource Page Link Request:**
- Target curated lists, "best of" pages, resource directories
- Use when your content genuinely fits their existing list
- Focus on value to their readers, not your benefit

**Broken Link Outreach:**
- Use tools like Ahrefs or Check My Links to find broken links
- Target pages where your content is genuinely relevant
- Always lead with helping them fix the broken link

**Collaboration Pitch:**
- Target complementary brands, not direct competitors
- Use when you can offer genuine value exchange
- Best for long-term relationship building

### Personalization Best Practices:

1. **Research thoroughly:** Spend 5-10 minutes on their site before writing
2. **Be specific:** Generic compliments are obvious. Reference actual content
3. **Match their tone:** Formal sites get formal emails, casual sites get casual tone
4. **Show your homework:** Mention specific articles, stats, or achievements
5. **Provide options:** Give them multiple ways to say yes
6. **Follow up appropriately:** Wait 1-2 weeks, then send ONE polite follow-up

### Red Flags to Avoid:
- Mass email language ("Dear Webmaster")
- Overly promotional tone
- Demanding or entitled language
- Ignoring their content guidelines
- Following up too aggressively

### Success Metrics to Track:
- Open rates (aim for 25%+)
- Response rates (aim for 5-10%)
- Link acquisition rate (aim for 2-5%)
- Relationship building (qualitative)

**Remember:** Quality relationships beat quantity outreach. Better to send 10 highly personalized emails than 100 generic ones.
