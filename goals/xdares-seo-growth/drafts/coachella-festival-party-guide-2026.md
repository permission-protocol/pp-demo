# The Coachella Playbook: 15 Festival Games for the Desert (or Your Living Room)

Whether you're heading to Indio or hosting a stream-along party from your couch, Coachella 2026 is the ultimate excuse for festival-themed games. Here are 15 ways to keep the energy high between sets.

## In-Indio (Live at the Festival)

### 1. The "Outfit Bingo"
- **The Game:** Create a 5x5 grid of common festival tropes (crochet tops, cowboy boots, celebrity look-alikes, influencers in the wild).
- **How to Win:** First person to spot a row wins a drink (or a snack) back at the camp.

### 2. The "Set List Roulette"
- **The Game:** Before a headliner starts, everyone guesses the first 3 songs.
- **The Rule:** For every wrong guess, you owe a "festival dare" (like complimenting a stranger's outfit or dancing for 30 seconds).

### 3. The "Silent Disco" Mimic
- **The Game:** One person wears headphones. Everyone else has to guess the song they are dancing to.
- **The Reward:** Winner gets to pick the next artist everyone listens to during the drive/walk.

## At-Home (The Couchchella Party)

### 4. The "Drop" Drink
- **The Rule:** Every time the bass drops in an EDM set, everyone takes a sip.

### 5. The "Influencer" Challenge
- **The Game:** Every time the camera pans to someone taking a selfie or looking "bored but pretty," everyone drinks.

### 6. The "VIP" Upgrade
- **The Game:** Use a deck of cards. High card gets "VIP status" and gets to sit on the best chair and have their drinks brought to them for 15 minutes.

## Festival Dares (Adult Edition)

7.  **The "Merch" Swap:** Convince a friend to trade one item of clothing with you for 1 hour.
8.  **The "Front Row" Push:** Mime pushing through a crowd to get to the "bathroom" (the kitchen).
9.  **The "Shout Out":** Shout the name of an artist who *isn't* performing and see if anyone agrees with you.
10. **The "Glow Up":** Apply more glitter to your face every 20 minutes until the headliner starts.
