# Cinco de Mayo 2026: The Ultimate Fiesta Games & Drinking Guide

_Cinco de Mayo is more than just tacos and margaritas—it's about the fiesta! If you’re hosting a Mexican-themed party this year, keep the energy high with these classic and modern games._

---

## 1. Mexican Dice (Dudo / Liar’s Dice)
This is a high-stakes bluffing game that gets everyone involved. 

- **Equipment:** One cup and 5 dice per player.
- **Rules:** Players roll their dice secretly and bid on how many of a certain face value are under all cups combined (e.g., "I bet there are seven 4s"). Each subsequent bid must increase the quantity or the face value. If you think someone is lying, call "Dudo!"
- **Drinking Variant:** If you lose a challenge, you take a shot or a swig of your margarita.

## 2. Toma Todo (Perinola)
A traditional Mexican spinning top game that is deceptively simple and highly addictive.

- **Equipment:** A six-sided spinning top (Perinola) and tokens (or just drinks!).
- **Rules:** Each side of the top has an instruction:
    - **Toma Uno:** Take one (token/drink).
    - **Toma Dos:** Take two.
    - **Toma Todo:** Take everything (the pot).
    - **Pon Uno:** Put one in.
    - **Pon Dos:** Put two in.
    - **Todos Ponen:** Everyone puts one in.
- **The Twist:** Use a "Communal Pitcher" as the pot. If someone hits "Toma Todo," they have to finish the pitcher (or a significant portion).

## 3. Lotería (Mexican Bingo)
Replace standard bingo with this iconic deck of cards featuring classic Mexican imagery like *El Diablito* or *La Sirena*.

- **Equipment:** Lotería deck and tablas (game boards).
- **Drinking Variant:** Assign a specific card as the "Social" card. Every time *El Borracho* or *La Botella* is called, everyone drinks. The first to fill their board and shout "¡Lotería!" gets to assign a "tequila penalty" to the loser.

## 4. "Drink If..." Fiesta Edition
Print out donkey piñata-shaped cards with challenges like:
- Drink if you've ever been to Mexico.
- Drink if you can name the three colors of the Mexican flag.
- Drink if you're currently wearing a sombrero.
- Drink if you can’t handle the spiciest salsa on the table.

## 5. Piñata Dares
Instead of just candy, fill your piñata with small slips of paper containing "Fiesta Dares."

- **The Dares:** "Do a 30-second Grito (Mexican shout)," "Blindfolded taco eating contest," or "Teach everyone a 10-second salsa move."
- **The Catch:** If you can't or won't do the dare, you have to take a penalty drink.

## 6. Taco Bar Game Stations
Set up your food area with mini-games to earn your toppings:
- **Jalapeño Roulette:** Most are mild, but one is a scorcher.
- **Bean Bag Toss (Cornhole):** Score a point to unlock the "Guacamole Premium" topping.

---

**Hosting Tips for 2026:**
- **Go Digital:** Use a Lotería app if you can't find physical cards.
- **Hydration is Key:** For every margarita, have a glass of water.
- **Safety First:** Arrange for Ubers or designated drivers before the first tequila bottle is opened.

_Happy Cinco de Mayo! ¡Salud!_
