# Super Bowl 2027 Party Games: Commercial Bingo, Half-Time Dares & Drinking Games

Get ready for the biggest game of the year! Whether you're a die-hard football fan or just here for the snacks and commercials, these Super Bowl 2027 party games will keep everyone entertained.

## 1. Commercial Bingo
Print out bingo cards with common Super Bowl commercial tropes:
- A beer commercial featuring a dog.
- A celebrity cameo you didn't see coming.
- An emotional car commercial.
- A snack brand using a nostalgic song.
- A movie trailer for a summer blockbuster.

First person to get five in a row wins a prize (or gets to assign a dare!).

## 2. Half-Time Show Dares
During the half-time show, every time there's a costume change or a special guest, someone has to perform a dare:
- **The Backup Dancer:** Mimic the performer's dance moves for 30 seconds.
- **The Hype Man:** Cheer as loudly as possible for the next song.
- **The Lyricist:** Predict the next line of the song. If you're wrong, take a penalty.

## 3. Super Bowl 2027 Drinking Games
- **The Touchdown Toast:** Everyone drinks when a touchdown is scored.
- **The Flag Penalty:** If a flag is thrown, everyone takes a sip.
- **The Replay Ref:** Drink if a play goes under official review.
- **The Quarterback Sack:** If the quarterback is sacked, fans of the opposing team take a drink.

## 4. Final Score Prediction
Have everyone write down their final score predictions before kickoff. The person closest to the actual score at the end of the game wins the "Super Bowl MVP" trophy (or a bag of wings).

Enjoy the game, the food, and the company! Happy Super Bowl 2027!
