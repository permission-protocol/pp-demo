## How to Play Truth or Dare (Adult Edition) {#how-to-play}

The classic rules still apply, but here's how to make it more interesting for grown-ups:

1. **Set boundaries first** — Agree on what's off-limits before you start
2. **Add stakes** — Drinks, forfeits, or even money on the line
3. **Use a timer** — Dares must be completed within 2 minutes
4. **No backing out** — If you refuse, there's a penalty (extra drink, embarrassing photo, etc.)
5. **Escalate gradually** — Start tame, get wilder as the night goes on
