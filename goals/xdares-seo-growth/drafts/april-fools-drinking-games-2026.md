# Draft: 15+ Hilarious April Fool's Day Drinking Games for Adults (2026 Edition)

Looking to spice up your April 1st with more than just basic pranks? These April Fool's Day drinking games combine the chaos of the holiday with your favorite party beverages.

## 1. The "Salt or Sugar" Shot Roulette
A classic prank turned into a high-stakes drinking game.
- **The Setup:** Pour 5 shots of water and 1 shot of heavily salted water.
- **The Game:** Everyone takes a shot at the same time. The person who gets the salt shot has to finish their next drink.

## 2. "Who Am I?" (Prank Edition)
The traditional sticky-note-on-forehead game with an April Fool's twist.
- **The Twist:** Instead of real people or characters, everyone writes down the most embarrassing "past version" of the player (e.g., "Middle School Goth Phase" or "That One Time You Danced on the Table").
- **Drinking Rule:** If you can't guess who you are within 5 questions, you drink. If you guess correctly, everyone else drinks.

## 3. The "Fake Fact" Pub Quiz
Test your friends' ability to spot a lie.
- **How to Play:** The host reads out a mix of obscure real facts and convincing fake ones.
- **The Rule:** If you guess "Fake" and it's real, you drink. If you guess "Real" and it's a prank fact, you drink double.

## 4. The "Mirror" Challenge
A test of coordination and sobriety.
- **The Game:** Two players stand opposite each other. Player A performs a series of increasingly complex movements. Player B must mirror them perfectly.
- **The Prank:** Player A should throw in a "fake-out" move (like a sudden stop or a change in speed). If Player B messes up the mirror, they drink.

## 5. Reverse Beer Pong
Everything is backwards for April Fool's.
- **The Rule:** Instead of the thrower assigning a drink, the thrower must drink if they *make* the shot. If they miss, everyone else drinks.
