# The Ultimate Memorial Day BBQ Guide 2026: 15 Games & Dares for the Backyard

Memorial Day is the unofficial start of summer, and that means one thing: backyards, burgers, and bold dares. Whether you're hosting a neighborhood BBQ or a low-key hangout with friends, these games will take your party from "just another grill-out" to a legendary summer kickoff.

## 1. The "Grill Master" Dare
**The Dare:** The current person at the grill must perform a 30-second interpretive dance about the burger they just flipped.
**The Penalty:** If they refuse, they have to fetch the next round of drinks for everyone.

## 2. Cornhole Roulette
**The Game:** Standard cornhole, but each hole has a specific dare attached to it.
**The Dare:** If you land a bag in the hole, you pick a person from the opposing team to perform a "Hot Sauce Challenge" (tasting the spiciest sauce at the BBQ).

## 3. Water Balloon Truth or Dare
**The Game:** Pass a water balloon around in a circle. When the music stops (or the balloon pops), the person holding it must choose Truth or Dare.
**The Twist:** If they choose Dare, it must be performed while holding a new (and very full) water balloon.

## 4. The "Patriotic" Toast Challenge
**The Dare:** Give a 1-minute toast to a completely random object (e.g., a spatula, a lawn chair, or the cooler) as if it were a national treasure.

## 5. Burger Stack Race
**The Game:** Two teams compete to see who can assemble a "perfect" burger (bun, patty, cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, pickle, bun) the fastest—blindfolded.

## 6. Slip 'n' Slide Dares
**The Dare:** Slide down the slip 'n' slide while trying to catch a flying disc. If you miss, you have to do 10 pushups in the grass.

## 7. The "Condiment" Art Challenge
**The Dare:** Draw a portrait of another guest using only ketchup and mustard on a paper plate. The group votes on the winner; the loser takes a "mystery" drink.

## 8. Yard Dice "I Never"
**The Game:** Roll the giant yard dice. If you roll an even number, you share a "Never Have I Ever" related to summer vacations. If you roll an odd number, you take a "penalty" sip of your lemonade (or something stronger).

## 9. Lawn Chair Musical Chairs
**The Game:** Classic musical chairs, but using lawn chairs and summer hits from 2026.

## 10. The "First Wave" of Summer Dare
**The Dare:** The first person to mention work or school during the BBQ must jump into the pool (or get sprayed with a hose) fully clothed.
