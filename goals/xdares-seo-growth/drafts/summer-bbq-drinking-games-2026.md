# Summer BBQ Drinking Games 2026: 35+ Backyard Grilling Games

*Turn your summer cookout into an epic party with these outdoor drinking games*

Nothing says summer like cold drinks, sizzling burgers, and good friends in the backyard. These BBQ drinking games are perfect for Memorial Day, Labor Day, or any weekend when you fire up the grill.

From lawn games with drinking rules to grill-themed competitions, we've got everything you need to take your backyard barbecue from basic to legendary.

---

## 🍻 Classic Lawn Drinking Games

### 1. Cornhole Drinking Rules
**Players:** 4  
**What you need:** Cornhole set, drinks

Standard cornhole with boozy stakes:
- Opponent scores 1 point = take a sip
- Opponent lands in the hole = finish your drink
- You get skunked (21-0) = shotgun a beer

Pro tip: Play doubles so you have recovery time between throws.

### 2. Beer Pong: Outdoor Edition
**Players:** 4  
**What you need:** Table, cups, ping pong balls

Outdoor twist on the classic:
- Wind interference is fair game
- Landing on grass = re-throw allowed
- Bug lands in a cup = opponent drinks it (cruel but fair)
- Sunset rule: When the sun sets, winning team picks a loser to shotgun

### 3. Horseshoes Drinking Game
**Players:** 2-4  
**What you need:** Horseshoe set, drinks

Classic rules plus:
- Ringer = everyone else drinks
- Leaner = you give out 2 drinks
- Miss the pit completely = take a shot
- Cancel out opponent's ringer = they drink double

### 4. Kan Jam Shots
**Players:** 4  
**What you need:** Kan Jam set, drinks

- Dinger (deflection in) = opponents drink
- Deuce (deflect off barrel) = opponents sip
- Bucket (thrown straight in) = opponents finish drinks and do a lap around the yard
- Instant win slot = losers shotgun

### 5. Ladder Golf Booze Rules
**Players:** 2-4  
**What you need:** Ladder ball set, drinks

Each rung has different rules:
- Top rung (3 pts) = give out 3 drinks
- Middle rung (2 pts) = give out 2 drinks
- Bottom rung (1 pt) = give out 1 drink
- Wrap all three on one rung = opponent finishes drink
- Hit the ground = take a shot yourself

## 🔥 Grill-Themed Drinking Games

### 6. Grill Master Challenge
**Players:** 2+  
**What you need:** Grill, meat, drinks, blindfolds

Blindfolded players must identify what's cooking by smell alone:
- Correct guess = give out 3 drinks
- Wrong guess = take 2 drinks
- Guess "chicken" when it's fish = finish your drink

### 7. Flip Cup: Grill Edition
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Cups, table, drinks

Standard flip cup but with a twist:
- Winning team gets first dibs on food
- Losing team has to flip again before they can eat
- Between rounds, someone yells "FLIP THE MEAT" and everyone has to mime flipping a burger while chugging

### 8. Burger Building Race
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Burger components, timer

Race to build the tallest stable burger:
- Bun, patty, cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, condiments
- Falls over = start drinking until you rebuild
- Tallest standing burger wins
- Losers eat their collapsed creations

### 9. Hot Dog Holding Contest
**Players:** 2+  
**What you need:** Hot dogs, drinks

Hold a hot dog horizontally in your mouth, no hands:
- Every 30 seconds = take a sip (with the hot dog still in mouth)
- Drop it = chug rest of your drink
- Last one standing wins immunity from next round

### 10. Temperature Guessing Game
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Meat thermometer, cooking meat

Guess the internal temp of the meat:
- Closest without going over = give out 5 drinks
- Exactly right = make a rule
- Way off (20+ degrees) = take a shot

## 🌞 Outdoor Party Games with Drinks

### 11. Water Balloon Roulette
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Water balloons, drinks

Form a circle, toss a water balloon:
- Drop it (and it breaks) = take a shot
- Drop it (doesn't break) = pass to next person
- Throw at someone and it breaks = both of you drink
- Survive 5 passes without it breaking = give out 5 drinks

### 12. Frisbee Golf Drinking Edition
**Players:** 2-6  
**What you need:** Frisbees, targets (coolers, trees, chairs)

Set up a 9-hole course in your yard:
- Par = give out drinks equal to par
- Birdie = opponents all drink
- Bogey = take a drink per stroke over
- Double bogey or worse = finish your drink

### 13. Croquet Chaos
**Players:** 4-6  
**What you need:** Croquet set, drinks

Classic croquet with additions:
- Get sent flying by opponent's ball = take a shot
- Hit through a wicket = give out a drink
- Finish first = make a rule for next game
- Come in last = wear a silly hat until next game ends

### 14. Giant Jenga Drinking Tower
**Players:** 2-8  
**What you need:** Giant Jenga, markers, drinks

Write rules on blocks:
- "Give 2" / "Take 3" / "Waterfall" / "Thumb Master"
- "Categories: BBQ Toppings" / "Never Have I Ever"
- "Challenge: Grill impression"
- Tower falls = you drink for 10 seconds

### 15. Bocce Ball Booze
**Players:** 2-8  
**What you need:** Bocce set, drinks

- Closest to pallino = give out 3 drinks
- Hit the pallino = everyone else drinks
- Knock opponent's ball away = they drink
- Dead last = drink until someone finishes

## 🍺 Drinking Game Classics (BBQ Edition)

### 16. Kings: Cookout Version
**Players:** 4-10  
**What you need:** Deck of cards, King's cup

BBQ-themed rules:
- **Ace** - Waterfall (Grill is ON!)
- **2** - You (pick someone)
- **3** - Me (drink up)
- **4** - Floor (touch the grass)
- **5** - Guys
- **6** - Chicks
- **7** - Heaven
- **8** - Mate
- **9** - Rhyme (BBQ words: grill, thrill, chill...)
- **10** - Categories (Types of BBQ sauce)
- **Jack** - Never Have I Ever... at a BBQ
- **Queen** - Question Master
- **King** - Pour into cup, 4th King drinks it

### 17. Flip, Sip, or Strip
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Coin, drinks

Classic game, perfect for hot weather when losing layers isn't so bad:
- Call the flip wrong = sip or strip
- Three wrong in a row = BOTH
- Winner = stays fully clothed and hydrated

### 18. Beer Die: Backyard Edition
**Players:** 4  
**What you need:** Plywood table, dice, cups

Outdoor version with BBQ rules:
- Sink a cup = opponent drinks
- Die hits the grill (safely!) = everyone drinks
- Wind blows die off table = re-throw
- Score 12 first = losers shotgun

### 19. Rage Cage: Cookout Tournament
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Cups, ping pong balls, table

Standard rage cage but:
- Winner gets to announce "THE GRILL IS HOT!"
- Last person bouncing has to flip something on the grill
- Tournament style: bracket it out, loser of finals does dishes

### 20. Civil War: Memorial Day Battle
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** 20+ cups, ping pong balls

Split into two teams:
- Each person has 3 cups in front of them
- Everyone shoots simultaneously
- Get hit = drink and that cup is out
- All your cups gone = you're "dead" (can't shoot but can block)
- Last team with cups wins, losers clean up

## 🌭 Food & Drink Combo Games

### 21. Kebab Challenge
**Players:** 2+  
**What you need:** Kebab skewers, various foods

Build the most creative kebab:
- Best presentation = give out 5 drinks
- Most meat = immune from drinking this round
- Falls apart = drink while picking it up
- Group votes, losers drink

### 22. Sauce Tasting Blind Test
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Various BBQ sauces, blindfolds

Blindfolded players taste and identify:
- Get the brand/type right = give out 3 drinks
- Wrong guess = drink mixed with hot sauce
- Bonus round: Identify the HEAT LEVEL (1-10)

### 23. Chug & Grill Relay
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Drinks, grill

Team relay race:
1. First person chugs
2. Runs to grill, flips one item
3. Runs back, tags next person
- First team to flip all items AND finish drinks wins
- Losing team serves winners

### 24. Condiment Roulette
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Various condiments, chips

Load chips with random condiments, one has hot sauce:
- Pick and eat
- Got the hot one? Drink milk to cool down... just kidding, drink more beer
- Survive all rounds = crowned Condiment Champion

### 25. "First Bite" Drink Sync
**Players:** All

Everyone waits until the grill master says "Meat's ready!"
- First bite must be taken simultaneously
- Late bites = drink
- Drop food = drink
- Compliment the chef = give out a drink

## 🎯 Skill-Based BBQ Drinking Games

### 26. Ring Toss Rampage
**Players:** 2-6  
**What you need:** Ring toss game, drinks

Each stake is worth different drinks:
- Center stake = opponents finish drinks
- Outer stakes = give out 1-3 drinks based on distance
- Complete miss = take a drink
- Ring bounces off and hits someone = THEY drink

### 27. Spike Ball Sippers
**Players:** 4  
**What you need:** Spike Ball set, drinks

After each point:
- Rally longer than 5 hits = losing team drinks
- Amazing play = losers take a shot
- Argument about a call = both teams drink

### 28. Pool Noodle Jousting
**Players:** 2 at a time  
**What you need:** Pool noodles, balance beam (or line)

Stand on chairs or a bench, joust with pool noodles:
- Knocked off = chug
- Both fall = both chug
- Win 3 in a row = immune for 15 minutes

### 29. Blindfold Relay
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Blindfolds, obstacles

Navigate through the yard blindfolded:
- Hit an obstacle = drink when you finish
- Knock over a drink = finish it
- Fastest time = give out 5 drinks

### 30. Dizzy Bat BBQ Run
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Bat/broom, cones

Spin 10 times around a bat, then:
- Navigate to the grill
- Pick up a spatula
- Flip air-meat (mime it)
- Run back

Slowest = drinks until second-to-last finishes

## 🌅 Chill BBQ Games (Lower Key)

### 31. Sunset Sips
**Players:** Any  
**What you need:** View of sunset, drinks

As the sun sets:
- Sun touches horizon = everyone drinks
- Sun halfway down = finish your drink
- Sun disappears = cheers and start a new round
- Beautiful moment

### 32. Music Lyric Drinking Game
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Speaker, drinks

Play summer hits:
- Song says "summer" = drink
- Song says "party" = drink
- Song says "beach" / "sun" / "dance" = sip
- Recognize the song in first 3 seconds = give out 2 drinks

### 33. Two Truths and a Lie: BBQ Edition
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Just drinks

All statements must be BBQ/summer/outdoor related:
- "I've never burned a burger"
- "I once ate 15 hot dogs in one sitting"
- "I've been to a BBQ in every state"

Guess wrong = drink. Stump everyone = give out 5.

### 34. Would You Rather: Summer Edition
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Drinks

Ask summer-themed questions:
- "Would you rather only eat hot dogs or only burgers all summer?"
- Minority answer drinks
- Everyone same answer = question-asker drinks

### 35. Photo Hunt
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Phones, drinks

Create a scavenger hunt list:
- Photo with the grill master
- Someone mid-bite

- A bug on food (gross but funny)

Last to complete list = finishes their drink

## Pro BBQ Drinking Tips

**Stay Hydrated**
- 1 water for every 2 drinks
- Especially important in summer heat
- Signs you need water: headache, dizziness, dry mouth

**Pace Yourself**
- You've got all afternoon/evening
- No need to sprint when it's a marathon
- The grill isn't going anywhere

**Eat First**
- Never drink on an empty stomach at a BBQ
- The food is literally right there
- Use it

**Designated Grillers**
- One person stays sober enough to work the grill
- Switch off halfway through
- Fire + too many drinks = bad

**Know When to Stop**
- The goal is fun, not blackout
- Tomorrow-you will thank today-you
- Better to remember the good times
