## 🎃 Classic Halloween Party Games (With Adult Twists)

### 1. Costume Contest Categories

**The Setup:** Go beyond "Best Costume" with these categories:
- **Best DIY/Low Budget** — creativity beats spending
- **Best Couples/Group Theme** — coordination is key
- **Most Terrifying** — actually scary, not just gory
- **Best Pop Culture Reference** — what everyone's talking about in 2026
- **Best "Sexy [Random Object]"** — the inevitable category
- **Most Likely to Go Viral** — for the TikTok era

**Drinking Rule:** Losers in each category take a shot.

### 2. Witch's Brew Taste Test

**What You Need:** 5-6 mystery cocktails in identical cups

**How to Play:**
1. Create themed cocktails (Blood Orange Margarita, Swamp Water, etc.)
2. Blindfold players one at a time
3. They must identify ingredients or at least name the drink
4. Wrong guesses = drink the whole thing

### 3. Mummy Wrap Race

**The Classic Party Game:** Teams race to wrap one member in toilet paper.

**Adult Version:** 
- Use glow-in-the-dark tape instead
- Add drinking checkpoints ("pause and drink")
- The mummy must escape and drink their beer before time's up
- Fastest team wins; slowest team drinks a penalty round

### 4. Pumpkin Carving Contest

**Standard Rules:** Everyone carves a pumpkin, best one wins.

**Drinking Edition:**
- Every time you cut yourself (lightly!), take a sip
- If your design looks nothing like your vision, finish your drink
- Winner gets to assign drinks to others

## 🍻 Halloween Drinking Games

### 5. Horror Movie Drinking Game

**Pick a Classic:** *Scream*, *Halloween*, *A Nightmare on Elm Street*, or whatever's trending in 2026.

**Universal Rules:**
- Someone says "Don't go in there" — drink
- Jump scare — everyone drinks
- Someone trips while running — 2 drinks
- The killer appears — drink
- Obvious foreshadowing — drink
- Someone investigates a noise alone — drink
- "I'll be right back" — finish your drink (they won't be)

### 6. Trick or Drink

**The Setup:** Get a bowl of Halloween candy and a separate bowl of "trick" cards.

**How to Play:**
1. Each turn, choose: Trick or Treat?
2. **Treat:** Take a piece of candy, but the person to your right picks how many drinks you take based on candy type (chocolate = 1, sour = 2, full-size bar = 3)
3. **Trick:** Draw a card with a challenge (do a scary impression, tell a spooky story, etc.) — fail and drink

### 7. Monster Mash Flip Cup

**Team Setup:** Divide into classic monster teams — Vampires vs. Werewolves, Zombies vs. Ghosts.

**Rules:**
- Standard flip cup rules apply
- Losing team must do a monster impression before next round
- After 3 losses, that team has to do the "Monster Mash" dance

### 8. Bobbing for... Shots

**The Modern Twist:** Float shot glasses in a bucket of water (or use floating shot glass holders).

**How to Play:**
- Hands behind your back
- Grab a shot with your mouth
- Whatever you grab, you drink
- Mix up the shots: some tame (apple cider), some not (fireball)

### 9. Ouija Board Drinking Game

**How to Play:**
1. Actually use an Ouija board (real or app)
2. Every time the planchette moves, person touching it drinks
3. If it spells something spooky, everyone drinks
4. If someone gets scared and quits, they finish their drink

*Note: For entertainment purposes only. Or is it? 👻*

### 10. Graveyard Dice

**What You Need:** 6 dice, drinks, a "graveyard" cup in the center

**Rules:**
- Roll all 6 dice
- 1s = add a shot to the graveyard cup
- 6s = you're safe
- 2-5 = drink that many sips
- Three of a kind = assign drinks to others equal to the number
- If you roll all 1s, you drink the graveyard

## 🎭 Murder Mystery & Interactive Games

### 11. Werewolf (Mafia) — Halloween Edition

**The Classic Social Deduction Game:**
- Werewolves eliminate villagers each night
- Villagers vote to eliminate suspected werewolves during the day
- Special roles: Seer, Witch, Hunter, Bodyguard

**Drinking Rules:**
- Dead players drink while watching
- Falsely accused villagers drink before being eliminated
- Werewolves drink if caught
- Last surviving faction assigns drinks to the losers

### 12. Halloween "Who Am I?"

**Setup:** Everyone gets a sticky note on their forehead with a Halloween character.

**Characters:** Dracula, Frankenstein, The Headless Horseman, Michael Myers, Pennywise, etc.

**Drinking Twist:**
- Only yes/no questions allowed
- Wrong guesses = drink
- If someone gives you a too-obvious hint, they drink
- Guess correctly and assign 3 drinks

### 13. Murder Mystery Party

**Commit to the Bit:** Host a full murder mystery dinner party.

**2026 Trending Themes:**
- "Social Media Influencer Found Dead" — suspects are different platform stars
- "Reality TV Reunion Gone Wrong"
- "Haunted Mansion Inheritance Drama"
- "The Last Podcast Recording"

**Drinking Integration:**
- Drink when you lie
- Drink when you're caught lying
- Drink when you accuse the wrong person

## 🔮 Spooky Challenges & Dares

### 14. Séance Circle (Truth or Dare: Haunted Edition)

**How to Play:**
1. Sit in a circle with candles (battery ones work)
2. Spin a bottle (or skull)
3. The person it points to chooses: Confession or Curse
4. **Confession:** Tell a spooky truth (biggest fear, creepiest experience, etc.)
5. **Curse:** Do a dare chosen by the group

**Sample Curses:**
- Go into a dark room alone for 2 minutes
- Let someone do your makeup blindfolded
- Speak only in a creepy voice for 10 minutes
- Text your ex "I see dead people"
- Do a dramatic reading of a cheesy ghost story

### 15. Fear Factor: Halloween Edition

**Challenges (Rate by Scariness):**
- Eat something blindfolded (could be candy, could be... interesting)
- Watch 5 minutes of a horror movie alone
- Let someone hide and jump-scare you
- Touch mystery items in a box ("brains," "eyeballs," etc.)
- Do karaoke to "Thriller" — full choreography attempt

**Drinking Rule:** Refuse a challenge = take 3 drinks

### 16. Haunted Scavenger Hunt

**How It Works:**
1. Hide creepy items around the party space
2. Teams race to find everything
3. First team done wins
4. Losing team drinks for each item they missed

**Scavenger Hunt Items:**
- A fake spider
- Something that glows
- A pumpkin seed
- A black cat (picture counts)
- Something "dead" (fake, please)
- A potion ingredient

## 👻 Low-Key Games (For Smaller Gatherings)

### 17. Two Truths and a Lie: Horror Edition

**The Twist:** All statements must be spooky-themed.

**Examples:**
- "I've seen a ghost" / "I've been to a haunted house" / "I once found a human bone"
- "I can say Bloody Mary in a mirror" / "I've used a Ouija board" / "I've slept in a graveyard"

**Drinking Rule:** If the group guesses wrong, they all drink. If they guess right, you drink.

### 18. Spooky Story Circle

**How to Play:**
1. Someone starts a horror story with one sentence
2. Go around the circle, each person adding one sentence
3. Try to make it as scary (or ridiculous) as possible
4. If someone laughs, they drink
5. If someone can't continue the story, they drink

### 19. Horror Movie Trivia

**Categories:**
- Classic slashers
- Modern horror (2020s)
- International horror
- Horror comedies
- "Based on true events"

**Drinking Rules:**
- Wrong answer = drink
- Last place after each round = take a shot
- Get a question about a movie you haven't seen = "social" (everyone drinks)

### 20. Candy Corn Roulette

**What You Need:** A bowl of candy corn, some coated in hot sauce or wasabi

**Rules:**
- Take turns eating candy corn
- No reactions allowed
- First person to react loses
- Losers drink

## Pro Tips for the Perfect Halloween Party

1. **Set the Mood:** Dim lights, candles, and fog machines transform any space
2. **Sound Design:** Use a Bluetooth speaker in a hidden spot for random creepy sounds
3. **Commit to Theme:** Even the snacks should be spooky (deviled egg "eyeballs," "finger" hot dogs, etc.)
4. **Have Alternatives:** Keep non-alcoholic "potions" available
5. **Safety First:** Uber codes, crash space, or a designated driver
6. **Capture Everything:** The costumes and chaos are too good not to document
