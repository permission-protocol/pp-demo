## How to Play Truth or Dare

### Basic Rules

1. **Gather your group** — Truth or dare works best with 4-10 people
2. **Take turns** — Go around the circle or spin a bottle
3. **Choose wisely** — When it's your turn, pick truth or dare
4. **No backing out** — Once you choose, you commit (or face a penalty)
5. **Keep it fun** — The goal is laughter, not trauma

### House Rules to Consider

- **Veto rule:** Each player gets 1-2 vetoes per game
- **Shot rule:** If you refuse, you take a shot
- **Escalation:** Dares get progressively wilder
- **Time limits:** Set time limits for longer dares
- **Safe words:** For when things get too uncomfortable

### Playing Over Text

- Use a random number generator to pick questions
- Set response time limits (24 hours works well)
- Send photo/video proof for dares
- Keep a running chat thread for the game
