## What is Truth or Dare Drinking Game?

The truth or dare drinking game combines the classic childhood favorite with adult themes and alcohol. Players take turns choosing between answering revealing questions (truth) or completing fun challenges (dare), with drinks as penalties for backing out or as rewards for participation.

**Why It's Perfect for Parties:**
- Breaks the ice quickly
- Gets people laughing and bonding
- Creates unforgettable memories
- Works with groups of any size
- No special equipment needed

## How to Play Truth or Dare Drinking Game

### Basic Rules

1. **Sit in a circle** with drinks ready
2. **Choose who goes first** (youngest player, birthday person, etc.)
3. **Spin a bottle** or go clockwise to select the next player
4. **Ask "Truth or Dare?"** to the selected player
5. **Complete the challenge** or take a drink penalty
6. **Pass the turn** to the next person

### Drinking Rules

**Standard Version:**
- Refuse a truth: Take 2 drinks
- Refuse a dare: Take 3 drinks
- Complete a dare: Everyone else drinks
- Answer a truth: Questioner drinks

**Party Version:**
- Failed dare: Finish your drink
- Embarrassing truth: Everyone drinks
- Chicken out: Take 5 sips + skip next turn

## Party Setup & Safety Tips

### What You'll Need

**Essential Supplies:**
- Variety of alcoholic beverages
- Shot glasses and regular cups
- Bottle for spinning (or cards)
- Comfortable seating arrangement
- Good music playlist
- Snacks and water

**Optional Extras:**
- Truth or dare app for backup questions
- Polaroid camera for memories
- Party decorations
- Dance floor space

### Safety Guidelines

**Drink Responsibly:**
- Set limits before starting
- Have designated drivers or rideshares ready
- Keep water and food available
- Never pressure anyone to drink excessively

**Consent & Boundaries:**
- Establish boundaries before starting
- Allow players to pass with penalty
- Respect comfort levels
- No means no - always

**Create Safe Space:**
- Keep the group trusted friends
- What happens at the party, stays at the party
- Have a signal for when someone's uncomfortable
- Include non-drinkers with alternative penalties

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How many people can play truth or dare drinking game?
**A:** The game works with 3-15 people ideally. Smaller groups (3-6) allow for more personal interaction, while larger groups (8-15) create more variety and energy.

### Q: What if someone refuses both truth and dare?
**A:** Traditional penalty is finishing their drink and skipping their next turn. Some groups also add embarrassing penalties like wearing a silly hat or doing dishes.

### Q: Can you play without alcohol?
**A:** Absolutely! Substitute drinks with embarrassing punishments, funny tasks, or non-alcoholic drinks. The social interaction is what makes it fun.

### Q: How do you handle inappropriate questions or dares?
**A:** Establish group boundaries before starting. Players can always pass with a penalty, and the group should respect everyone's comfort levels.

### Q: What's the best way to choose questions?
**A:** Start light and build intensity. Read the room - keep it fun and consensual. Having a mix of funny, revealing, and spicy options keeps everyone engaged.

### Q: How long should a game last?
**A:** Most parties play for 1-3 hours. End before people get too drunk to enjoy it safely. Quality time is better than marathon sessions.
