# Summer Camp Drinking Games 2026: 35+ Nostalgic Games for Adults

*Relive your childhood summers with boozy twists on classic camp activities*

Remember capture the flag, color wars, and campfire songs? Now imagine all of that with alcohol. Welcome to Summer Camp Drinking Games—where nostalgia meets your adult beverage of choice.

These games are perfect for cabin trips, backyard camping, summer reunions, or any time you want to channel those carefree summer camp vibes from your youth (but with beer).

---

## 🏕️ Classic Camp Games with Drinking Rules

### 1. Capture the Flag: Booze Edition
**Players:** 10+  
**What you need:** Two flags (or towels), drinks at base

The classic camp game with stakes:
- Get tagged in enemy territory = drink at jail until freed
- Free prisoners = captors drink
- Capture the flag = entire opposing team does a shot
- Win the game = losers serve drinks for 30 minutes

**Pro tip:** Play at dusk for maximum camp vibes.

### 2. Color Wars Drinking Tournament
**Players:** 10+  
**What you need:** Team bandanas, multiple games

Divide into teams (classic red vs. blue):
- Compete in 5+ mini-games
- Losing team drinks after each event
- Events: Flip cup, relay race, trivia, tug of war, scavenger hunt
- Overall losing team cleans up

### 3. Red Light, Green Light (21+ Version)
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Open space, drinks

Childhood classic with consequences:
- Move on green, freeze on red
- Caught moving = back to start + drink
- First across = gives out 5 drinks
- Last across = chugs

### 4. Freeze Dance: Party Edition
**Players:** 5+  
**What you need:** Music, drinks

Dance when music plays, freeze when it stops:
- Caught moving = drink
- Last one dancing before freeze = drink
- Best dance move = give out 3 drinks
- Worst freeze pose = finish drink

### 5. Duck Duck Goose: Drink Drink Chug
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Circle seating, drinks

Updated for adults:
- "Duck" = tap head, person sips
- "Goose" = they chase you around the circle
- Get caught = chug
- Make it back = goose chugs
- Bonus: If goose doesn't catch you in one lap, they drink AND become the next tapper

## 🔥 Campfire Drinking Games

### 6. Campfire Story Circle
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Fire pit, drinks

Collaborative storytelling:
- One person starts a story with one sentence
- Next person continues
- Pause too long (5 seconds) = drink + story continues without you
- Say "um" or "uh" = drink
- End the story = everyone toasts

### 7. Ghost Story Shot Game
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Darkness, flashlight, drinks

Tell scary stories around the fire:
- Someone gets genuinely scared = they drink
- Story includes a jump scare = storyteller drinks if no one jumps
- Someone says "that's not scary" = they drink
- Best story = storyteller gives out 5 drinks

### 8. Campfire Song Karaoke
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Fire, song knowledge, drinks

Sing classic camp songs:
- Forget the lyrics = drink
- Others join your song = they drink WITH you (solidarity)
- Song request you don't know = take a shot
- Nail "Kumbaya" = give out 3 drinks

### 9. S'mores Roulette
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** S'mores supplies, one spicy ingredient

Make s'mores, but one has a surprise:
- One marshmallow has hot sauce inside
- Everyone makes and eats simultaneously
- Got the spicy one = chug to cool down
- Caught checking your marshmallow = drink anyway

### 10. Truth or Dare: Camp Edition
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Flashlight, drinks

All truths/dares must be camp-themed:
- "Truth: What's your most embarrassing camp memory?"
- "Dare: Howl at the moon for 10 seconds"
- Refuse a truth/dare = drink twice
- Complete = give out drinks

## 🏃 Field Day Drinking Games

### 11. Relay Race Drinking Edition
**Players:** 8+  
**What you need:** Cones, drinks at checkpoints

Add drinks to the classic:
- Station 1: Sip and tag
- Station 2: Spin 3 times, sip, run
- Station 3: Answer trivia, wrong = drink, run
- Station 4: Finish line chug
- Losing team does another round

### 12. Three-Legged Beer Run
**Players:** 6+ (pairs)  
**What you need:** Bandanas, drinks at finish

Tied together at the ankle:
- Race to the finish line
- Chug a drink together (tied legs stay tied)
- Race back
- Last pair = both do a shot

### 13. Egg and Spoon (Beer in Cup)
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Spoons, cups with beer

Balance a cup of beer on a spoon:
- Spill = refill and restart
- Drop cup = drink a full replacement
- First across = gives out 5 drinks
- Most spilled = cleans up

### 14. Sack Race Shots
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Sacks/bags, shot glasses

Hop to shots set at 10-yard intervals:
- Take a shot at each checkpoint
- Fall over = shot penalty when you finish
- First across = champion
- Last across = extra shot

### 15. Water Balloon Toss: Drink the Splash
**Players:** 4+ (pairs)  
**What you need:** Water balloons, drinks

Step back after each catch:
- Drop balloon = both drink
- Balloon pops ON you = opponent drinks
- Last pair standing = gives out 5 each
- Fill some balloons with beer for surprises

## 🎨 Arts & Crafts Drinking Games

### 16. Friendship Bracelet Race
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Bracelet-making supplies, drinks

First to complete a bracelet wins:
- Every 5 minutes without finishing = drink
- Tangled thread = drink while untangling
- Give your bracelet to winner = they drink (appreciation shot)
- Winner picks next activity

### 17. Tie-Dye Predictions
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Tie-dye supplies, shirts

Before unfolding:
- Predict what pattern you'll get
- Way off = drink
- Better than predicted = give out 2 drinks
- Best design = gives out 5 drinks

### 18. Nature Art Competition
**Players:** 3+  
**What you need:** Found natural items, timer

15 minutes to create art from nature:
- Rocks, sticks, leaves, etc.
- Group votes on best
- Winner gives out 5 drinks
- "What IS that?" = creator drinks

### 19. Camp Craft Show-and-Tell
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Crafts, drinks

Share your questionable creations:
- Most creative = give out 3
- Most likely to fall apart = drink
- "I made this at summer camp" story = take a drink
- Best childhood craft story = immune for a round

## 🌲 Outdoor Adventure Games

### 20. Flashlight Seek
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Flashlights, darkness

Hide and seek with flashlights:
- Beam hits you = you're found, drink at base
- Last one found = gives out 5 drinks
- First one found = seeker next round
- Found three games in a row = shot

### 21. Nature Scavenger Hunt
**Players:** 4+ (teams)  
**What you need:** Lists, phones for photos

Find and photograph items:
- Pine cone, mushroom, specific leaf type
- Animal sighting (bonus drinks!)
- Team finishes last = drinks for every item they missed
- First team = picks a dare for losers

### 22. Manhunt: Counselor vs. Campers
**Players:** 8+  
**What you need:** Outdoor area, glowsticks

One person is the "counselor":
- Counselor hunts campers in the dark
- Tagged = go to "jail" (designated drinking area)
- Last camper standing wins
- Counselor tags everyone = becomes ultimate winner

### 23. Orienteering Drinks
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Map, compass (or phone), hidden drinks

Hide drinks at compass coordinates:
- Teams navigate to find hidden cans
- Wrong location = penalty drink
- Find all drinks first = winning team drinks them
- Get lost = designated driver picks you up (maybe)

### 24. Night Hike Drink Stops
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Trail, flashlights, drinks at waypoints

Planned hike with stations:
- Every 10 minutes = drink stop
- First to spot wildlife = gives out 2 drinks
- Someone trips = they drink
- Reach the end = toast to adventure

## 🎮 Cabin Games for Rainy Days

### 25. Card Games Marathon
**Players:** 4-8  
**What you need:** Cards, drinks

Camp card game classics:
- Bullshit: Called correctly = caller drinks, called wrong = liar drinks
- Spoons: Lose a round = drink, first out = shot
- War: Lose a war = finish drink
- Play until someone taps out

### 26. Board Game Drinking Rules
**Players:** 3-6  
**What you need:** Classic board games, drinks

Add drinks to camp favorites:
- **Candy Land:** Land on a color twice = drink that color (assign colors to drinks)
- **Chutes and Ladders:** Hit a chute = drink going down
- **Monopoly:** Pay rent = drink proportional to amount
- **Sorry!:** Send someone home = they drink

### 27. Charades: Camp Edition
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Camp-themed prompts, drinks

Act out camp activities:
- Canoeing, archery, swimming, crafts
- Team doesn't guess in time = all drink
- Nailed it fast = other team drinks
- Actor breaks = they drink

### 28. Name That Tune: Summer Camp Songs
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Music/humming ability, drinks

Hum or play classic camp songs:
- First to guess = give out 2 drinks
- Stumped the group = give out 5 drinks
- You don't know ANY camp songs = finish your drink in shame

### 29. Bunk Bed Trivia
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Camp trivia, drinks

Test camp knowledge:
- "What merit badge requires knot-tying?"
- "How do you treat poison ivy?"
- "Name 5 camp songs"
- Wrong = drink, way wrong = double

## 🏊 Lake & Water Games

### 30. Canoe Races
**Players:** 4+ (pairs)  
**What you need:** Canoes/kayaks, drinks at shore

Race across the water:
- First team to far shore and back wins
- Losers drink when they finally arrive
- Flip your canoe = finish drink when you get back
- Winners get first dibs at dinner

### 31. Dock Diving Contest
**Players:** 4+  
**What you need:** Dock, water, judges

Best dive competition:
- Cannonball = classic, give 2 drinks
- Belly flop = drink for your bravery
- Most creative = give 5 drinks
- Chickens out = do a shot

### 32. Marco Polo: Shots Edition
**Players:** 5+  
**What you need:** Pool/lake safe area, drinks

Classic with stakes:
- Tagged = out, drink on shore
- "Marco!" with no response = everyone drinks
- Tag three in a row = become Marco Master
- Last one swimming = gives out 5

### 33. Water Balloon Volleyball
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Net, water balloon, towels

Teams catch and toss with towels:
- Drop balloon = team drinks
- Pop balloon = everyone on that side drinks
- Win the point = other team sips
- Win the game = losers do a water challenge

### 35. Talent Show Drinking Game
**Players:** 6+  
**What you need:** Talent (debatable), drinks

Perform for the group:
- Standing ovation = give out 5 drinks

### 36. Taps (Drinking Nightcap)
**Players:** Any  
**What you need:** Drinks, music

End of night ritual:
- Play "Taps" (or any chill camp song)
- Take a slow sip with each line

## Pro Tips for Adult Camp Games

**Set the Scene**
- String lights, campfire, outdoor setting
- Camp-style dress code (flannel, bandanas)
- Play the camp soundtrack

**Safety First**
- Water activities = have sober spotters
- Flashlight games = clear the area of hazards
- Hiking = buddy system always

**Pace the Night**
- Start with high energy field games
- Move to crafts/cabin games as it gets dark
- End with campfire activities

**Food Matters**
- Serve camp food: hot dogs, s'mores, bug juice
- Eat before heavy drinking
- Have plenty of water

**Nostalgia Boost**
- Encourage sharing real camp stories
- Dig out old camp photos
- Make friendship bracelets for real
