- **Target:** 3-5 posts per week (18 total posts over 30 days)
- **Monday, Wednesday, Friday:** Primary publishing days
- **Tuesday, Thursday:** Bonus posts for high-priority content
- **Weekends:** Reserved for seasonal/timely content

1. **Cornerstone Content (30%)** - High-volume keywords, comprehensive guides
2. **Supporting Content (40%)** - Long-tail variations, niche topics
3. **Seasonal/Timely (15%)** - Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day themes
4. **Alternative/Competitor (15%)** - Capturing competitor traffic

### Keyword Targeting Strategy
- **Week 1:** Publish ready-drafted content (6 posts ready)
- **Week 2:** High-volume adjacent niches (icebreakers, party games)
- **Week 3:** Drinking games + Valentine's seasonal content
- **Week 4:** Couples activities + group games
- **Week 5:** Final push with remaining opportunities

### Internal Linking Strategy
Each new post will link to 3-5 existing posts, creating content clusters:
- **Truth/Dare Cluster:** Core truth or dare posts link to each other
- **Party Games Cluster:** All party-themed content interconnected
- **Couples Cluster:** Dating, couples, romantic content linked
- **Drinking Games Cluster:** All alcohol-related content connected

## SEO Optimization Standards

### On-Page SEO Checklist (Every Post)
- ✅ Target keyword in title (within first 60 characters)
- ✅ Target keyword in first paragraph
- ✅ Target keyword in at least one H2 heading
- ✅ Meta description (150-160 characters) with target keyword
- ✅ Alt text for all images with relevant keywords
- ✅ Internal links to 3-5 related posts
- ✅ External links to 1-2 authority sources
- ✅ FAQ schema markup where appropriate
- ✅ Article schema markup
- ✅ Social sharing buttons
- ✅ Related posts section

### Content Structure Template
1. **Hook/Problem** (100-150 words)
2. **Table of Contents** (for 2000+ word posts)
3. **Main Content** (structured with H2/H3 headings)
4. **Key Takeaways** (bulleted list)
5. **FAQ Section** (3-5 common questions)
6. **Call to Action** (visit Xdares platform)
7. **Related Posts** (internal linking)
