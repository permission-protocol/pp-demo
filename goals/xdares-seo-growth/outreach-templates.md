# Usage Notes

## Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Replace [PLACEHOLDERS] with specific details
- [ ] Reference specific recent content from their site
- [ ] Mention their name/brand naturally
- [ ] Include relevant stats/results where applicable
- [ ] Adjust tone to match their brand voice

## Follow-up Schedule
1. **Initial outreach:** Send template
2. **First follow-up:** 1 week later (if no response)
3. **Second follow-up:** 2 weeks after first follow-up
4. **Final follow-up:** 1 month later, then archive

## Success Metrics to Track
- Open rates by subject line type
- Response rates by template
- Conversion rates (response → backlink)
- Quality of resulting backlinks
- Traffic driven from each placement

## Template Performance Notes
- Template 1 (Guest Post): Best for established bloggers
- Template 4 (Resource Link): Highest success rate for quick wins
- Template 5 (Broken Link): Great conversation starter
- Template 6 (Social Media): Best for visual content creators
