The Coachella Playbook: 15 Festival Games for the Desert (or Your Living Room)

Whether you’re heading to Indio or hosting a stream-along party from your couch, Coachella 2026 is the ultimate excuse for festival-themed games. Here are 15 ways to keep the energy high between sets.

In-Indio (Live at the Festival)

1. The “Outfit Bingo”

  • The Game: Create a 5x5 grid of common festival tropes (crochet tops, cowboy boots, celebrity look-alikes, influencers in the wild).
  • How to Win: First person to spot a row wins a drink (or a snack) back at the camp.

2. The “Set List Roulette”

  • The Game: Before a headliner starts, everyone guesses the first 3 songs.
  • The Rule: For every wrong guess, you owe a “festival dare” (like complimenting a stranger’s outfit or dancing for 30 seconds).

3. The “Silent Disco” Mimic

  • The Game: One person wears headphones. Everyone else has to guess the song they are dancing to.
  • The Reward: Winner gets to pick the next artist everyone listens to during the drive/walk.

At-Home (The Couchchella Party)

4. The “Drop” Drink

  • The Rule: Every time the bass drops in an EDM set, everyone takes a sip.

5. The “Influencer” Challenge

  • The Game: Every time the camera pans to someone taking a selfie or looking “bored but pretty,” everyone drinks.

6. The “VIP” Upgrade

  • The Game: Use a deck of cards. High card gets “VIP status” and gets to sit on the best chair and have their drinks brought to them for 15 minutes.

Festival Dares (Adult Edition)

  1. The “Merch” Swap: Convince a friend to trade one item of clothing with you for 1 hour.

  2. The “Front Row” Push: Mime pushing through a crowd to get to the “bathroom” (the kitchen).

  3. The “Shout Out”: Shout the name of an artist who isn’t performing and see if anyone agrees with you.

  4. The “Glow Up”: Apply more glitter to your face every 20 minutes until the headliner starts.

  5. The “Secret Set” Guess: Everyone puts a dollar in a jar (or a virtual bet) and guesses who the unannounced “secret guest” will be. Closest guess (even if nobody is right, whoever was most plausible) takes the pot or gets a free drink.

  6. The “Desert Heat” Hydration Challenge: Festival safety is no joke. For every 30 minutes of dancing, the group must collectively take a 2-minute “hydration break” with water. Anyone who skips it is “disqualified” from the next fun round.

  7. The “Influencer Pose” Charades: One person mimes a classic “Influencer in the Wild” pose (the “over-the-shoulder look,” the “fake laugh,” the “hair flip,” or the “looking at the ground for no reason”). First person to guess the pose gets to “upgrade” their drink.

  8. The “Headliner” Trivia: While waiting for the main act to take the stage, the host (or designated expert) asks 3 trivia questions about the headliner’s career. Whoever gets the most right chooses the next round’s drinking rules.

  9. The “Couchchella” Crowd Surf: At home only! One person gets “crowd surfed” (lifted by the group or just celebrated on the couch) while their favorite anthem plays. If they touch the ground, they have to finish their drink.
