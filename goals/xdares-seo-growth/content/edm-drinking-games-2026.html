EDM Drinking Games: Beats, Drops & Neon Dares (2026 Guide)

The bass is pumping, the lights are flashing, and the energy is electric. Music festivals and EDM shows are the ultimate social experience, but sometimes you want to bring that rave energy home. Whether you’re pregaming for Coachella or hosting a “Couchchella” stream party, these EDM drinking games will keep the vibes high.

Core Rules: The “Beat Drop” Basics

These rules apply to any EDM set or playlist:

  1. The Drop Sip: Every time the bass drops, everyone takes a sip.
  2. The Build-Up Gulp: During a build-up (the rising synth or drum roll), everyone must keep drinking until the drop hits.
  3. The DJ Shoutout: Every time the DJ shouts “1, 2, 3, JUMP!” or “How you feeling [City Name]?”, everyone takes a sip.
  4. The Remix Rule: If you recognize the original song being remixed, you get to assign a sip to someone else.

The Rave Games

1. Kandi Swap Challenge

  • The Game: Every time a new artist starts their set, everyone swaps a piece of Kandi (beaded bracelets).
  • The Twist: If you don’t have any Kandi left to swap, you owe a “neon dare” (see below).

2. The Visualizer Drink

  • The Game: Pick a color in the stage visuals (e.g., Pink). Every time that color becomes the dominant screen color, your team drinks.

3. PLUR Dares (Adult Edition)

  • Peace: Find the person you’ve known the shortest amount of time and give them a genuine compliment.
  • Love: Group hug for 10 seconds.
  • Unity: Everyone must dance in perfect sync for one full minute.
  • Respect: Toast to the DJ for a legendary set.

Neon Dares (The Consequences)

If you lose a challenge or miss a drop, pick a dare:

  • The Glow Up: Apply a fresh layer of neon face paint or glitter.
  • The Shuffle: Perform your best 15-second shuffle (or attempt to).
  • The LED Mime: Mime your favorite instrument (drums, synth, or decks) for 30 seconds.
  • The Fan Clack: (If you have a fan) Perform a dramatic “clack” and a pose.
