1. Sandy Competition Games 🏖️

Get competitive in the sand with these high-energy beach games that'll have everyone laughing and diving.

Sandcastle Showdown

Players: 4+ (teams of 2-3) | Vibe: Creative & competitive

Teams have 30 minutes to build the most impressive sandcastle. Judge categories: height, creativity, structural integrity, and "most likely to survive a wave." Losers drink.

Pro tip: Bring spray bottles to keep sand moist. Add a "sabotage round" where teams can splash opponents' castles from 10 feet away.

Beach Bocce Drinking

Players: 2-8 | Vibe: Chill competitive

Classic bocce with a drinking twist. Closest ball to the pallino wins. Everyone else drinks based on distance — one sip per foot away. If you hit the pallino directly, everyone else finishes their drink.

Sandy Flip Cup

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: High energy

Set up a driftwood plank or bring a folding table. Standard Flip Cup rules but in the sand — uneven surfaces make flipping WAY harder. Sand in your cup? That's the beach tax.

Bury the Body

Players: 4+ (teams of 2+) | Vibe: Hilarious

Each team picks a volunteer to be buried. First team to completely bury their person (head visible obviously) wins. The buried people from losing teams have to take a shot before being dug out.

Sand Pong

Players: 2-4 | Vibe: Classic beach

Dig cup-sized holes in the sand in a triangle formation. Use a tennis ball instead of a ping pong ball. Standard Beer Pong rules — the wind and sand make every shot an adventure.

Limbo on the Beach

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Party classic

Use a pool noodle or stick for the limbo bar. Everyone who fails takes a drink. Sand makes it slippery — expect wipeouts. Play tropical music for the full effect.

Frisbee Golf Drinking

Players: 2-8 | Vibe: Active & social

Set up "holes" using towels, coolers, umbrellas as targets along the beach. Par is 3 throws per hole. Over par = drink per extra throw. Hole in one = everyone else drinks.

2. Tropical Cocktail Drinking Games 🍹

Pair these games with margaritas, piña coladas, and ice-cold beers for the ultimate beach drinking experience.

Coconut Roulette

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Suspenseful

Fill several coconut cups (or regular cups) with drinks — most with juice, a few with straight liquor. Players pick randomly. Get the strong one? That's your fate. No switching!

Tropical Trivia Sips

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Chill brain games

Beach and ocean themed trivia. Wrong answers = drink. Categories: marine life, famous beaches, tropical cocktails, surf culture, beach movies. Get 3 in a row right? Pick someone to waterfall.

Margarita Madness

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Taste challenge

Blindfolded taste test of different margarita flavors (mango, strawberry, classic, spicy). Guess wrong = finish it. Guess right = assign a drink. The sommelier of slush wins bragging rights.

Drink or Dare: Beach Edition

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Wild & fun

Draw from a deck of beach-themed dares: run into the ocean fully clothed, do a cartwheel in the sand, serenade a stranger, build a sand angel. Refuse the dare? Drink double. Check out our full Truth or Dare guide for more ideas.

Sunset Toast Challenge

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Chill & creative

Everyone prepares a toast — best beach memory, funniest moment of the day, most dramatic sunset quote. Group votes on best toast. Winner assigns drinks. Worst toast chugs.

Shell Shot Roulette

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Quick rounds

Place shells in a circle, one hiding a "marker" underneath. Players pick shells — find the marker and you're drinking. Simple, fast, and perfect between swims.

Beach Bar Bingo

Players: 3+ | Vibe: All-day game

Create bingo cards with beach sightings: jet ski, dog in sunglasses, someone getting hit by a wave, sunburn victim, metal detector person. First bingo = everyone else drinks. Play all day!

3. Ocean & Wave Games 🌊

Take the party into the water with these ocean-side games. Safety first: stay in shallow water and know your limits!

Wave Chicken

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Adrenaline

Stand in a line facing the ocean. Last person to run from an incoming big wave wins the round. Everyone who ran early drinks. Play safe — ankle-to-knee deep water only!

Splash Attack

Players: 4+ (two teams) | Vibe: All-out war

Shallow water splash battle. Team that retreats to dry sand first loses and drinks. Best of 3 rounds. Alliances, betrayals, and soaking guaranteed.

Tide Pool Treasure

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Scavenger hunt

Create a list of things to find near the water's edge: specific shell types, smooth stones, sea glass, interesting driftwood. First to complete the list picks the drinking punishment for last place.

Surfboard Balance Challenge

Players: 2+ | Vibe: Skill test

Try to stand on a surfboard or boogie board in shallow water. Time how long you balance. Shortest time drinks. If you can do a pose (yoga, dab, flex), bonus points and you assign a drink.

Ocean Relay Race

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: High energy

Teams relay race: run to the water, swim/wade to a marker, come back. But here's the twist — you're carrying a cup of beer. Whatever's left in your cup when you return counts. Least beer remaining team drinks extra.

Seaweed Tag

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Silly fun

Tag in ankle-deep water. Person who's "it" has to tag someone while everyone moves in slow-mo through the water. Last person tagged in each round assigns drinks.

4. Beach Volleyball & Sports Variations 🏐

Beach sports with a drinking twist — because regular volleyball just isn't enough.

Drink & Volley

Players: 4+ (two teams) | Vibe: Active & competitive

Standard beach volleyball but: every point scored against your team = everyone on your side takes a sip. Every ace = losing team takes a shot. Game to 15 — things get interesting around point 10.

Spikeball Drinking Tournament

Players: 4-8 | Vibe: Tournament style

Bracket-style Spikeball tournament. Losing team drinks between rounds. Championship losers do a "walk of shame" to the water and back. Bring Spikeball — it's the ultimate beach game.

KanJam Beach Edition

Players: 4 (two teams) | Vibe: Chill competitive

KanJam with drinking rules: hit the can = opponents sip, slot it = opponents finish drinks, instant win = opponents do a waterfall. The wind makes this SO much harder on the beach.

Beach Football Drinking

Players: 6+ | Vibe: All-American

Touch football on the sand. Scoring team picks someone on the losing team to drink. Interceptions = entire opposing team drinks. Running in sand is exhausting — pace your drinks!

Paddleball Rally

Players: 2-4 | Vibe: Quick & easy

Use beach paddleball sets. Count consecutive rallies. Drop the ball before 10? Drink. Break the group record? Assign 3 drinks to anyone. The wind is your enemy.

Beach Soccer Shootout

Players: 4+ | Vibe: International flair

Set up goal markers with towels. Penalty shootout style — 5 shots each. Miss = drink. Score all 5 = you're the "beach Messi" and assign drinks to everyone. Playing in sand is humbling.

Ultimate Frisbee: Drink Edition

Players: 6+ | Vibe: High energy

Standard ultimate rules but turnovers = losing team drinks. Scoring = opponents drink. End zone celebrations are mandatory and judged — lamest celebration means extra drinks.

5. Tiki Bar & Luau Games 🌺

Give your beach party a tropical theme with these luau-inspired games.

Lei Exchange Game

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Social icebreaker

Everyone starts with a lei. If someone catches you saying a banned word (like "beach," "sun," or "drink"), they take your lei. Most leis at the end of the party = winner picks a group punishment drink. Great icebreaker for mixed groups.

Limbo Luau

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Classic party

Tropical music, grass skirts optional, drinks mandatory. Each limbo round, the bar goes lower AND you take a sip. The combination of alcohol and flexibility makes this exponentially harder and funnier.

Coconut Bowling

Players: 2+ | Vibe: Easy fun

Set up water bottles as pins, roll a coconut (or any ball) to knock them down. Strikes = assign 3 drinks. Gutter ball = drink yourself. Spare = assign 1. Play a full 10 frames for maximum chaos.

Hula Hoop Challenge

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Hilarious

Hula hoop contest — longest spin wins. But you have to hold your drink while hooping. Spill your drink = you're out AND you finish what's left. Sand makes the footing tricky.

Tiki Torch Trivia

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Evening game

As the tiki torches come out, so does the trivia. Topics: tropical islands, famous beaches, surf movies, ocean facts, cocktail ingredients. Each wrong answer = a tropical sip. Play under the stars.

Pineapple Pass

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Silly relay

Pass a pineapple down the line — but only using your neck/chin. Drop it = your section of the line drinks. Make it to the end? Reverse direction. It's harder than it sounds, especially with sunscreen-slippery skin.

6. Beach Team Relays & Races 🏃

Get moving with these relay races designed for sandy terrain.

Cooler Carry Relay

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: Exhausting & hilarious

Teams relay race carrying a loaded cooler through the sand. Drop anything = go back to start. Losing team serves drinks to the winners for the next hour.

Three-Legged Beach Race

Players: 4+ (pairs) | Vibe: Classic chaos

Three-legged race through soft sand. Falling is guaranteed. Last place pair drinks. Bonus: race into knee-deep water for extra difficulty.

Bucket Brigade

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: Wet & wild

Teams form a line from ocean to a bucket on shore. Pass water using only cups (with holes poked in them). First team to fill their bucket wins. Losers drink the "beach cocktail" (just regular drinks, don't worry).

Sand Sprint & Chug

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Race mode

Sprint 50 yards through sand, chug a drink at the turnaround, sprint back. First back wins. Running in sand after chugging = pure comedy. Not for the faint of stomach.

Towel Toss Relay

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: Coordination test

Teams use a beach towel to launch a water balloon to the next pair. Drop it = start over. First team to get the balloon to the end wins. Losing team gets the remaining balloons thrown at them.

Crab Walk Race

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Silly & tiring

Race crab-walk style across the sand. It's way harder than you remember from gym class, especially on uneven sand. Last place drinks. Bonus: carry a cup of beer on your stomach while crab walking.

Beach Obstacle Course

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Epic challenge

Design a course: army crawl under a towel, jump over a cooler, spin around an umbrella 5 times, sprint to the water and back. Time everyone. Slowest time = drinks. Dizziest person = extra drink.

7. Water Balloon & Splash Games 💦

Bring water balloons to the beach for maximum splash potential.

Water Balloon Toss

Players: 4+ (pairs) | Vibe: Classic

Partners toss a water balloon back and forth, stepping back after each successful catch. Pop it = both drink. Last pair standing assigns drinks to everyone else. Fill balloons with ocean water to save freshwater.

Water Balloon Dodgeball

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: All-out war

Beach dodgeball with water balloons. Get hit = you're out AND you drink. Last person standing wins immunity. Losing team finishes their drinks.

Splash Cup

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Refreshing

Like Flip Cup but after chugging, you fill your cup from the ocean and splash the next person in line before they can drink. It's chaotic, wet, and perfect for hot days.

Balloon Pop Dare

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Surprise element

Write dares on paper slips, put them inside balloons before filling with water. Pop a balloon (by sitting on it, throwing it, etc.) and do the dare inside. Refuse = drink triple.

Sponge Relay

Players: 6+ (two teams) | Vibe: Wet relay

Soak a sponge in the ocean, run to a bucket, squeeze it out. First team to fill their bucket wins. But here's the twist: opponents can throw water balloons at runners. Hit = go back and re-soak.

Super Soaker Showdown

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Battle royale

Everyone gets a water gun. Set up "bases" with towels. Capture-the-flag rules with water guns. Getting soaked is part of the fun. Losing team buys the next round of drinks.

8. Beach Treasure Hunts & Scavenger Games 🗺️

Turn the beach into your playground with these hunt-style games.

Buried Treasure Hunt

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Adventure

One person buries small items (sealed mini bottles, toys, coins) in a marked area. Everyone else digs to find them. Each item has a different "power" — assign drinks, skip a round, make a rule. Most treasures = pirate king.

Beach Bingo Scavenger Hunt

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Exploratory

Bingo cards with beach items: specific shell types, driftwood, sea glass, feather, smooth rock, crab, specific colored towel on another group's setup. Photo evidence required. First bingo = everyone drinks.

Drunk Treasure Map

Players: 4+ (teams) | Vibe: Creative chaos

Teams draw treasure maps for each other with clues leading to a buried "treasure" (mini liquor bottles work great). Maps must be drawn in the sand. Following drunk-drawn sand maps = hilarious.

Message in a Bottle

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Creative

Everyone writes a dare or drinking challenge on paper, puts it in a bottle (not in the ocean — we're not littering!). Shuffle bottles. Each person picks one and must complete what's inside or drink double.

Nature Scavenger Race

Players: 4+ (teams) | Vibe: Active

List of 15 beach items to find. First team to photograph all items wins. Losers do a group chug. Items can include: something that floats, 3 different shells, a piece of sea glass, animal tracks, something blue.

GPS Coordinate Hunt

Players: 4+ (teams) | Vibe: Tech meets beach

Pre-set GPS coordinates on the beach using your phone. Each coordinate has a challenge or prize. First team to complete all waypoints wins. This takes some setup but it's EPIC for larger beach parties.

9. Sunset & Bonfire Beach Games 🌅

As the sun goes down, the party shifts vibes. These games are perfect for golden hour and beyond.

Sunset Never Have I Ever

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Revelatory

Play as the sun sets — the golden light makes confessions feel cinematic. Beach-themed additions: "Never have I ever skinny dipped," "Never have I ever been stung by a jellyfish," "Never have I ever peed in the ocean" (spoiler: everyone drinks).

Bonfire Most Likely To

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Social & funny

Beach-themed questions: "Most likely to get the worst sunburn," "Most likely to befriend a random beach dog," "Most likely to lose their swimsuit in the waves." Person with most votes drinks.

Beach Campfire Stories

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Spooky & creative

Take turns telling scary stories around the bonfire. Group votes on scariest — winner assigns drinks. Best twist ending? Double assignment. Told a boring story? You drink.

Stars & Sips

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Romantic & chill

Lie back and stargaze. Spot a shooting star = assign a drink. First to identify a constellation = everyone else drinks. See a satellite = social drink (everyone sips together). Perfect for clear beach nights.

Sunset Predictions

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Chill betting

Everyone predicts the exact time the sun will fully set (check weather apps for actual sunset time but don't tell). Closest guess assigns drinks. Furthest off finishes their drink. Simple but surprisingly competitive.

Acoustic Sing-Along Drinking

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Musical

If anyone brought a guitar (or just use a speaker), sing along to beach/summer songs. Forget the lyrics = drink. Nail a harmony = assign drinks. Check out our karaoke drinking games for more musical fun.

Night Swim Dare Chain

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Adventurous

Take turns daring each other to wade into the dark water (ankle deep only for safety!). Each dare escalates: stand in waves for 10 seconds, do a handstand in the shallows, etc. Refuse = drink. Safety: designated sober spotter required.

10. Chill Beach Vibes Games 😎

Not everything has to be high-energy. These games are perfect for lounging on towels with drinks in hand.

Beach Read Roulette

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Bookish fun

Everyone opens their phone to a random Wikipedia article. Take turns reading the most absurd sentence you can find. Group votes on most interesting/funniest. Loser drinks.

Cloud Watching Game

Players: 2+ | Vibe: Ultra chill

Take turns pointing at clouds and describing what they look like. Group agrees = spotter assigns a drink. Nobody sees it = spotter drinks. Bonus: if it actually looks like what they said, everyone drinks in appreciation.

Beach Playlist DJ Battle

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Musical

Take turns playing songs. Group votes: beach vibe or not? Play a song that doesn't fit the beach mood = drink. Get everyone vibing = assign drinks. Create the ultimate beach playlist in the process.

Would You Rather: Beach Edition

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Conversation starter

Beach-themed Would You Rather: "Would you rather be attacked by 1 shark or 100 jellyfish?" "Would you rather only swim in freezing water or boiling sand?" Minority answer drinks.

Tan Line Contest

Players: 3+ | Vibe: End-of-day fun

At the end of the beach day, compare tan lines. Most ridiculous tan line wins and assigns drinks. Bonus category: worst sunburn (loser gets the aloe AND a drink).

Name That Tune: Summer Hits

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Competitive chill

Play 5-second clips of summer hits (Spotify makes this easy). First to name song AND artist gets a point. Most points after 10 rounds = DJ privileges and drink assignments.

Two Truths and a Beach Lie

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Icebreaker

Classic two truths and a lie but all statements must be beach/vacation related. "I've surfed in Hawaii," "I've been bitten by a crab," "I've swum with dolphins." Wrong guessers drink.

Seashell Storytelling

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Creative chill

Pass a beautiful shell around the circle. Whoever holds it tells a one-minute story (real or fake) about their best beach memory. Group votes real or fake. Wrong voters drink.

Beach Bucket List

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Deep conversation

Go around sharing beach/travel bucket list items. If someone else has already done your bucket list item, you drink. If NO ONE has done it, they all drink. Great way to learn about your friends' adventure goals.

Slow Motion Replay

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Hilarious

Pick the funniest moment from the beach day and everyone acts it out in slow motion — the wipeout, the seagull stealing food, the wave surprise. Best reenactment wins. Worst drinks.

Alphabet Beach Game

Players: 3+ | Vibe: Quick rounds

Go around naming beach-related things alphabetically: A-anchor, B-boogie board, C-coral... Hesitate for more than 5 seconds or repeat something = drink. Gets surprisingly hard after a few rounds and a few drinks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best drinking games to play at the beach?

The best beach drinking games include Sandy Flip Cup, Beach Bocce Drinking, Drink or Dare Beach Edition, and Sunset Never Have I Ever. For active groups, try Beach Football Drinking or Water Balloon Dodgeball. For chill vibes, Beach Bingo or Cloud Watching Game are perfect.

How do you set up beer pong at the beach?

For beach beer pong (Sand Pong), dig cup-sized holes in wet sand in a triangle formation instead of using cups on a table. Use a tennis ball instead of a ping pong ball since it won't blow away in the wind. Standard beer pong rules apply — the sand adds a fun unpredictable element to every shot.

What games can you play at a beach party without equipment?

No-equipment beach games include: Wave Chicken, Splash Attack, Never Have I Ever Beach Edition, Most Likely To, Beach Campfire Stories, Cloud Watching Game, Two Truths and a Beach Lie, Would You Rather Beach Edition, and the Alphabet Beach Game. All you need is friends and drinks!

Are beach drinking games safe?

Beach drinking games can be safe with proper precautions: stay hydrated with water between alcoholic drinks, apply sunscreen regularly (alcohol increases sun sensitivity), avoid deep water when drinking, designate a sober spotter for any water activities, know your limits, and never swim drunk. Pack plenty of water, snacks, and shade.

What should I bring to a beach drinking party?

Essential beach party supplies: a quality cooler with ice, plastic cups (no glass on beaches!), water bottles for hydration, sunscreen, beach towels, a portable speaker, water balloons, a frisbee, beach paddle ball set, and your favorite beverages. Optional upgrades: pop-up tent for shade, folding table for Flip Cup, tiki torches for evening vibes, and a portable grill.

Beach Party Safety Tips 🏥

  • Hydrate constantly — alternate water with alcoholic drinks (sun + alcohol = dehydration fast)
  • Sunscreen is mandatory — reapply every 2 hours, more if swimming. Alcohol increases sun sensitivity
  • No glass on the beach — use plastic cups, cans, or koozies only
  • Designated sober spotter for any water activities
  • Never swim drunk — ocean currents are no joke
  • Pack out everything — leave the beach cleaner than you found it
  • Check local laws — many beaches prohibit alcohol. Use koozies and be discreet
  • Know the tides — set up above the high tide line
