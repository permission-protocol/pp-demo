70+ Super Bowl Party Games & Football Drinking Games for Adults (2027)

The Big Game is more than just football - it's the ultimate excuse for day drinking, eating ridiculous amounts of food, and hanging with friends. Whether you're there for the touchdowns, the commercials, or just the seven-layer dip, we've got Super Bowl party games to make your game day legendary.

From drinking games that sync with every play to halftime entertainment and competitions for non-football fans, these 70+ games ensure everyone has a blast. Let's kick off! 🏈

🏈 Football Drinking Games

Games that follow the action on the field. Pace yourself - it's a long game!

1. Touchdown Toast

Players: Any | Vibe: Classic

The simplest game: drink every time either team scores a touchdown. Safety = finish your drink (rare but devastating). Field goal = sip. Extra point = nothing. Two-point conversion = two sips.

2. Penalty Box

Players: Any | Vibe: Flag-focused

Assign drink values to penalties. Holding = 2 sips. Pass interference = 3 sips. False start = 1 sip. Personal foul = finish your drink. Roughing the passer = the whole room drinks!

3. Turnover Takedown

Players: 4+ | Vibe: High stakes

Split into two teams supporting each Super Bowl team. When your team throws an interception or fumbles, your whole team drinks. Turnover on downs = half a drink.

4. The Announcer Game

Players: Any | Vibe: Commentary fun

Drink when announcers say: "Let's take a look at that again" (1 sip), mention a player's college (1 sip), say "what a play!" (2 sips), or compare someone to a retired legend (finish drink).

5. Timeout Trivia

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Educational

During every timeout, someone asks a football trivia question. Wrong answers drink. Right answers get to assign drinks. Keeps everyone engaged during breaks!

6. Flag Football

Players: Any | Vibe: Watchful

Everyone watches for penalty flags. First person to yell "FLAG!" when a flag is thrown gets to assign drinks. Wrong calls = you drink double.

7. Replay Review

Players: Any | Vibe: Judgmental

During coach's challenges or official reviews, everyone predicts the outcome. Wrong predictions drink when the call is announced. Overturned calls = everyone drinks!

📺 Commercial Break Games

Super Bowl commercials are half the fun. Make them interactive!

8. Commercial Bingo

Players: Any | Vibe: Classic

Create bingo cards with commercial tropes: celebrity cameo, talking animal, nostalgic reference, car ad, beer commercial, movie trailer, etc. First bingo = others finish their drinks!

9. Ad Draft

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Competitive

Before the game, draft which companies will have the best commercials. Rate each commercial 1-10 as a group. Highest total score at the end wins!

10. Celebrity Spotting

Players: Any | Vibe: Quick reactions

First person to correctly identify a celebrity in a commercial gets to assign drinks. Wrong guesses = you drink. Some celebrities appear multiple times - stay sharp!

11. Emotional Ad Challenge

Players: Any | Vibe: Sappy

Every time someone gets genuinely emotional during a heartwarming commercial, they have to admit it and drink. No judgment - those puppy ads hit different.

12. Commercial Drinking Rules

Players: Any | Vibe: Steady pace

  • Beer commercial = everyone with a beer drinks
  • Car commercial = last to pretend to drive drinks
  • Tech commercial = youngest person drinks
  • Insurance commercial = homeowners drink
  • Movie trailer = everyone who's seen the original drinks

13. Jingle Karaoke

Players: Any | Vibe: Musical

When a commercial has a memorable jingle or song, anyone who sings along gets a pass. Anyone who doesn't know it drinks!

14. Ad Recall Challenge

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Memory test

After each commercial break, someone quizzes the group on what brands were advertised. Each one you miss = 1 drink. Tests who's actually watching!

🎤 Halftime Show Games

The halftime show is its own event. Make it interactive!

15. Halftime Bingo

Players: Any | Vibe: Musical

Create cards with halftime show predictions: pyrotechnics, guest appearance, costume change, crowd shot, drone show, etc. Play during the performance!

16. Setlist Predictions

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Musical trivia

Everyone predicts the halftime performer's setlist. One drink for each song you miss. If someone gets the exact order, everyone else finishes their drinks!

17. Lip Sync Battle

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Performative

During the halftime show, compete to lip sync most enthusiastically. Worst performer (voted by group) drinks. Best performer assigns drinks!

18. Guest Star Gamble

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Predictive

Before halftime, everyone bets on whether there'll be a surprise guest performer and who it might be. Correct guesses = assign 5 drinks. Wrong = drink 2.

19. Costume Change Counter

Players: Any | Vibe: Observant

Predict how many costume changes the performer will have. Closest guess wins. Everyone else drinks the difference between their guess and actual number.

20. Halftime Dance-Off

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Active

During the halftime show, everyone must dance. Person voted "worst dancer" by the group drinks. Best dancer picks the next game!

🍗 Food & Eating Games

Super Bowl is about the spread. Make eating competitive!

21. Wing Championship

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Spicy

Who can eat the most wings during the game? Track throughout. Winner gets bragging rights and first dibs on leftovers. Optional hot sauce bonus rounds!

22. Dip Roulette

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Tasty

Blindfolded taste test of different dips. Guess the dip correctly or drink. Add one "trick" dip that's unexpectedly spicy for chaos!

23. Chip Shot

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Skillful

Try to throw chips into a dip bowl from increasing distances. Miss = drink. Make it = choose someone else to drink. Championship round at halftime!

24. Food Pairing Challenge

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Creative

Create weird food combinations from the spread. Others rate 1-10. Lowest rated creation drinks. Highest rated gets immunity from next drinking round.

25. Nacho Average Game

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Cheesy

Build the tallest nacho tower possible. If it falls while you're building, drink. Successfully completed tower = you're safe for a quarter.

26. The Clean Plate Club

Players: Any | Vibe: Sustainable

First person to finish everything on their plate without getting more food gets to make a rule for the next quarter. Encourages moderate portions!

💰 Prop Bet Games

Make everything about the game bettable (for drinks, not money!).

27. Over/Under Everything

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Predictive

Set over/under lines on everything: total points, number of commercials featuring dogs, times announcers say "dynasty," etc. Wrong predictions drink!

28. Coin Toss Chaos

Players: Any | Vibe: 50/50

Everyone predicts the coin toss. Wrong guesses drink. Also predict: will the winner choose to kick or receive? Double or nothing!

29. First Score Bet

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Strategic

Everyone predicts: which team scores first AND how (touchdown, field goal, safety). Both right = assign 5 drinks. One right = safe. Both wrong = drink 3.

30. Anthem Length

Players: Any | Vibe: Musical

Predict the national anthem length (over/under a set time). Time it together. Losing side drinks for every 5 seconds they were off.

31. Gatorade Shower Color

Players: Any | Vibe: End game

Everyone predicts the Gatorade color that will be dumped on the winning coach. Correct guess = assign drinks. Wrong = drink that color (find a close match in your drinks!).

32. MVP Prediction

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Football knowledge

Before the game, everyone writes their MVP prediction. Closest correct (even position if not exact player) wins. Others drink!

33. Final Score Prediction

Players: Any | Vibe: Classic

Everyone writes down their predicted final score. Closest to actual score wins and assigns drinks equal to the point difference between predictions.

🔲 Football Squares & Number Games

The classic party game with drinking twists!

34. Classic Football Squares Drinking Edition

Players: 10-100 | Vibe: Traditional

Standard football squares, but instead of (or in addition to) money, losers drink. Winner of each quarter assigns drinks to everyone who had "cursed" numbers like 2 or 5.

35. Reverse Squares

Players: 10+ | Vibe: Underdog

The worst numbers each quarter (furthest from winning) have to drink. Makes even bad squares exciting!

36. Quarter-by-Quarter Elimination

Players: 4+ | Vibe: High stakes

Everyone picks a score differential. If either team's score ends in your number, you're eliminated and drink. Last person standing wins!

37. Point Total Countdown

Players: Any | Vibe: Ongoing

Pick a total combined point number before the game. When that number is hit exactly, everyone drinks. Pick "69" for maximum party energy.

🏆 Team Competition Games

Split into teams for head-to-head battles!

38. Tailgate Olympics

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Active

Events: cornhole tournament, paper football, mini basketball shooting, chip toss accuracy. Losing team each event drinks!

39. Team Trivia

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Knowledgeable

Super Bowl history trivia in team format. Questions about past games, famous plays, records, and halftime shows. Losing team drinks each round!

40. Endzone Relay

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Active

Teams race to complete challenges: balance a beer on a football and walk across room, throw chips into a bowl, name 5 NFL teams, chug, repeat!

41. Draft Your Squad

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Fantasy-style

Draft real players from both teams. Score points when your player does something notable. Lowest score at end drinks!

42. Play Calling Challenge

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Strategic

Before each play, teams guess: run or pass? Correct guesses get points. Team with fewer points at end of each drive drinks!

43. Commercial Team Battle

Players: 6+ | Vibe: Reactive

Teams assigned alternating commercial breaks. Your team rates each ad during your break (1-10). Lowest scoring team at end drinks!

🤷 Games for Non-Football Fans

Include everyone, even those who can't tell a touchdown from a field goal!

44. Fashion Police

Players: Any | Vibe: Judgy

Rate player uniforms, sideline fashion, commercial outfits on style. Vote on best and worst dressed. Fun for fashion fans who dgaf about sports!

45. Storyline Bingo

Players: Any | Vibe: Narrative

Cards with announcer storylines: underdog narrative, comeback story, "playing for something bigger," injury update, family in stands. Mark when mentioned!

46. People Watching

Players: Any | Vibe: Observant

Spot specific fan types in crowd shots: painted face, crying child, celebrity, sleeping person, costume. First to spot each = assigns drinks!

47. Ad Rating Battle

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Critical

Rate every commercial. Compare scores at the end of each break. Person whose ratings differ most from group average drinks!

48. Snack Bracket

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Foodie

Create a bracket of all party snacks. Vote head-to-head during commercial breaks. Crown the ultimate Super Bowl snack! Losers of final round drink.

49. Text Prediction

Players: Any | Vibe: Social

Before each quarter, text your predictions for what will happen to a group chat. Most wrong predictions at end drinks!

50. Phone-Free Challenge

Players: Any | Vibe: Present

Stack phones in center of table. First person to reach for their phone during action drinks. Creates actual engagement!

58. Commercial Commentary

61. Photo Challenge

64. Highlight Predictions

Predict what will be the "play of the game" before it starts. Closest prediction wins bragging rights. Review highlights at end!

65. Couch Coach

Track when people yell advice at the TV that the team actually does next. Most "correct" coaching calls = honorary coach title!

66. Fantasy Recap

Players: 4+ | Vibe: Season wrap

For fantasy football players: share your season highlights and lowlights. Toast to the winner (and roast the last place)!

67. Prediction Envelope

Players: Any | Vibe: Time capsule

Write down score prediction, MVP, and one wild prediction in an envelope before the game. Open after the game. Save for next year's party!

68. Commercial Power Rankings

69. Thank You Notes

Players: Any | Vibe: Grateful

Write quick thank you notes during halftime: to the host, to whoever brought the best snack, to the MVP of your party. Spread appreciation!

70. Next Year's Host Vote

Players: Any | Vibe: Planning ahead

At the end of the night, nominate and vote for next year's host. Winner has a whole year to plan the ultimate Super Bowl party!

Pro Tips for Super Bowl Party Success

🏈 Setup Tips

  • Multiple screens = multiple viewing angles (or one for game, one for group chat reactions)
  • Clear sightlines from food area to TV
  • Print football squares and betting sheets in advance
  • Team-colored decorations and cups

🍺 Drink Pacing

  • Super Bowl is 3-4 hours - pace your drinking games accordingly
  • Have light drinking options (beer, seltzer) for sustained play
  • Non-alcoholic options for designated drivers and moderators
  • Water and snacks between drinking rounds

🍕 Food Strategy

  • Finger foods that don't require looking away from TV
  • Refillable snack stations vs. one-time dishes
  • Different heat levels for wings (mild to wild)
  • Save desserts for post-game celebration

📱 Digital Tips

  • Create a group chat for predictions and reactions
  • Share betting squares via app or document
  • Live poll for best commercial during breaks
  • Photo sharing folder for memories
