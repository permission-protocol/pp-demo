50+ Virtual Drinking Games & Online Party Ideas for Adults

Virtual parties have become a staple of modern social life. Whether you're celebrating a long-distance birthday, catching up with college friends, or hosting a remote team-building event, virtual drinking games are the perfect way to break the ice and keep the energy high.

In this guide, we've compiled over 50 of the best virtual drinking games and online party ideas. From simple verbal games to interactive web platforms, there's something here for every group size and vibe.

Best Platforms for Virtual Drinking Games

  • Zoom: Great for large groups (up to 100+ on free plans) and reliable screen sharing.
  • Discord: The gold standard for low-latency voice and video. Perfect for playing interactive web games.
  • FaceTime: Ideal for smaller groups of Apple users. Now supports SharePlay for watching videos together.
  • Google Meet: A solid, free alternative with easy-to-join links.

1. Classic Drinking Games (Virtual Edition)

These games require zero equipment (other than your drink) and work perfectly over a video call.

  • Never Have I Ever: The classic "confession" game. Take a sip every time you've done the act mentioned.
  • Most Likely To: On the count of three, everyone points to the person on screen they think is most likely to do something. Drink for every person pointing at you.
  • Truth or Drink: One person asks a question. The target either answers truthfully or takes a shot (or a big gulp).
  • Kings Cup (Online): Use a virtual card generator or have the host draw physical cards and hold them to the camera. Each card has a specific rule (e.g., Waterfall, Floor, Heaven).
  • Power Hour: Everyone takes a shot of beer (or a sip of their drink) every minute for 60 minutes. Use a YouTube "Power Hour" playlist to keep time.
  • Medusa: Everyone starts by looking down. On three, everyone looks at another person on screen. If you're looking at someone and they're looking back at you, both drink!
  • Straight Face: Everyone writes down a ridiculous or funny sentence in the chat. One person reads them out loud while trying to keep a straight face. If you laugh, you drink.

2. Interactive Online Games & Apps

Take advantage of digital platforms designed for social interaction.

  • The Jackbox Games: The undisputed king of virtual parties. Games like Quiplash, Drawful, and Trivia Murder Party are legendary. The host shares their screen, and everyone plays on their phones.
  • Skribbl.io: A free online drawing and guessing game. It's simple, hilarious, and competitive.
  • Gartic Phone: Like the game of "Telephone" but with drawings. The results are always chaotic and funny.
  • Codenames.game: The digital version of the popular board game. Perfect for groups that enjoy strategy.
  • Among Us: While technically a social deduction game, it's easily turned into a drinking game (drink when you're voted off, or when a body is found).

3. Virtual Drinking Competitions

For groups that want to get active and competitive from their own living rooms.

  • Virtual Beer Pong: Set up a table with 6 cups in front of your camera. Take turns "throwing" (throwing a ball at your own cups) when it's your turn. If you make it, your opponent drinks.
  • Solo Flip Cup: Race against your friends! Everyone starts at the same time. Drink your cup, flip it, and the first one to finish a set of three wins.
  • Quarters (Zoom Edition): Bounce a quarter off your desk into a shot glass. It's harder than it looks on camera!
  • Scavenger Hunt: The host calls out an item (e.g., "A yellow sock" or "A souvenir from a trip"). The first person to bring it to the camera wins. Last one back drinks!

4. Creative Virtual Party Themes

Mix things up with a unique theme or structured activity.

  • PowerPoint Night: Everyone prepares a 3-minute presentation on a ridiculous topic (e.g., "Ranking my friends as Taylor Swift eras"). Drink during every slide transition.
  • Virtual Trivia: Use a tool like Kahoot! or Mentimeter to host a custom trivia night. Winner picks someone to finish their drink.
  • Murder Mystery: Purchase a digital "Murder Mystery" kit and assign characters to your friends before the call.
  • Watch Party: Use a browser extension like Teleparty to watch a bad movie together and play a movie-specific drinking game.

Hosting Tips for a Seamless Virtual Party

  1. Test Your Tech: Ensure your internet connection is stable and your microphone works.
  2. Send a Menu: Suggest a specific cocktail or drink theme for the night so everyone feels connected.
  3. Appoint a "Game Master": Have one person in charge of explaining rules and keeping the flow moving.
  4. Respect Limits: Always remember that drinking is optional. Have fun, but drink responsibly.

Conclusion

Virtual drinking games prove that you don't need to be in the same room to have a legendary night. With the right platform and a bit of creativity, you can create memories that are just as fun as an in-person party.
