How to Play Would You Rather (Couples Edition)

Basic Rules

  1. Take turns asking "Would you rather...?" questions
  2. The other person MUST choose — no "neither" or "both"
  3. After choosing, explain your reasoning
  4. No judgment (okay, a little judgment is allowed)

Making It More Fun

  • Prediction Game: Before your partner answers, guess what they'll choose. Keep score!
  • Consequences: If you can't decide within 10 seconds, you have to do a dare
  • Wine Pairing: Add a glass of wine. Questions get more interesting as the bottle empties.

🎯 Pro Tips for Couples

When to Play

  • Road trips: Miles fly by when you're debating impossible choices
  • Date nights: Better than staring at phones in silence
  • Before sleep: End the day learning something new
  • Long distance: Makes video calls more engaging

Ground Rules for Couples

  • No "I don't know": Force a choice, that's where the magic is
  • Actually listen: Their reasoning reveals more than the choice
  • Follow up: "Wait, really? Tell me more about that..."
  • Remember answers: Bonus points for recalling their weird choices later

Red Flag Alert 🚩

If your partner consistently chooses the opposite of everything you'd want... maybe that's worth a deeper conversation. Or maybe opposites attract. You decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Would You Rather questions good for couples?

Yes! Would You Rather helps couples discover preferences, values, and deal-breakers in a playful way. You'll learn things about your partner that might never come up in normal conversation — like whether they'd rather lose all photos or all future plans.

What are good Would You Rather questions for date night?

Start with romantic or funny questions to warm up, then gradually move to deeper or flirtier territory. Questions about the future ("Would you rather retire early or travel full-time?") can reveal important compatibility insights.

How do you play Would You Rather with your partner?

Take turns asking questions. The key rule: you MUST choose one option — no "neither" or "both." After choosing, explain your reasoning. The conversation that follows is often more interesting than the choice itself.

What's a flirty Would You Rather question?

Try: "Would you rather I kiss you softly or grab you passionately?" or "Would you rather be surprised in bed or know exactly what's coming?" These questions build anticipation without being explicit.

Can Would You Rather questions lead to serious conversations?

Absolutely. Questions about future plans, money, family, and values can reveal compatibility — or areas that need more discussion. It's a gentle way to approach topics that might feel too heavy to bring up directly.
