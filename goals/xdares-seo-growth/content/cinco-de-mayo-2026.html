Cinco de Mayo 2026 is almost here! If you’re looking to host the ultimate fiesta, we’ve got you covered with the best games and drinking rules for your Mexican-themed party.

1. Mexican Dice (Dudo / Liar’s Dice)

This is a high-stakes bluffing game that gets everyone involved. It's perfect for a group sitting around a table with a round of margaritas.

  • Equipment: One cup and 5 dice per player.
  • Rules: Players roll their dice secretly and bid on how many of a certain face value are under all cups combined (e.g., "I bet there are seven 4s"). Each subsequent bid must increase the quantity or the face value. If you think someone is lying, call "Dudo!"
  • Drinking Variant: If you lose a challenge, you take a swig of your drink.

2. Toma Todo (Perinola)

A traditional Mexican spinning top game that is deceptively simple and highly addictive. It's the ultimate game of chance.

  • Equipment: A six-sided spinning top (Perinola) and tokens (or just drinks!).
  • Rules: Each side of the top has an instruction:
    • Toma Uno: Take one drink.
    • Toma Dos: Take two.
    • Toma Todo: Take everything (the communal pitcher!).
    • Pon Uno: Put one in.
    • Pon Dos: Put two in.
    • Todos Ponen: Everyone drinks one.

3. Lotería (Mexican Bingo)

Replace standard bingo with this iconic deck of cards featuring classic Mexican imagery. It's a staple at every fiesta.

  • Equipment: Lotería deck and boards (tablas).
  • Drinking Variant: Assign a specific card as the "Social" card. Every time El Borracho (The Drunkard) or La Botella (The Bottle) is called, everyone drinks!

4. "Drink If..." Fiesta Edition

A fast-paced icebreaker to get the party started. Go around the room and everyone drinks if the statement applies to them:

  • Drink if you’ve ever been to Mexico.
  • Drink if you can name the three colors of the Mexican flag.
  • Drink if you’re currently wearing a sombrero or a poncho.
  • Drink if you can’t handle spicy salsa.
  • Drink if you've already had a shot of tequila tonight.

5. Piñata Dares

Take the classic piñata to the adult level. Instead of just candy, fill it with small slips of paper containing dares!

  • The Dares: "Do a 30-second Grito (Mexican shout)," "Blindfolded taco eating contest," or "Teach everyone a 10-second salsa move."
  • The Catch: If you can't or won't do the dare, you have to take a penalty drink.

Fiesta Pro-Tip: Always ensure your guests have plenty of water and food between games. Happy Cinco de Mayo 2026! ¡Salud!
