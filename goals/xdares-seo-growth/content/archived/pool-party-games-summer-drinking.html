70+ Pool Party Games & Summer Drinking Games for Adults (2026)

Nothing beats a pool party with cold drinks, good music, and games that keep everyone entertained — whether they're in the water, lounging poolside, or somewhere in between getting progressively more sunburned.

We've put together 70+ pool party games across 10 categories — from in-the-water competitions to poolside drinking games, floatie races, and chill sunset activities. Summer is about to get a whole lot more fun.

☀️ How to use this guide: Pick 4-6 games based on your pool setup and crowd size. Alternate between active water games and relaxed poolside games to manage energy. And for the love of summer — wear sunscreen.

🍹 Pool Drinking Games (8 Games)

The whole point of a pool party is drinks + water + sun. These games combine all three perfectly.

1. Waterproof Flip Cup

Players: Teams of 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Set up a table at the pool's edge. Standard flip cup rules, but losers jump in the pool. Or play IN the shallow end with a floating table. The wet hands make flipping infinitely harder and funnier.

Pro tip: Use plastic cups that won't shatter. Seems obvious, but you'd be surprised.

2. Pool Pong

Players: 2v2 | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Float two triangle racks of cups on pool noodle platforms (or buy inflatable beer pong sets). Shoot from opposite ends of the pool. The water current adds chaos. Wind is your enemy. Splash shots count double.

3. Drink or Dive

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Draw a card from the deck. Red = take a drink. Black = do a challenge dive (belly flop, backflip attempt, cannonball). Face cards = you choose someone else. Ace = everyone drinks AND jumps in.

4. Shark Attack

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

One person is the "shark" underwater. Everyone else sits on the pool edge with feet in the water, holding their drinks. Shark taps a foot = that person chugs and becomes the new shark. Get pulled in = finish your drink.

5. Survivor Island

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Everyone starts on floaties (their "islands"). Each round, vote someone off their island into the water. Voted off = finish your drink. Last person still floating wins immunity and assigns drinks to everyone.

6. Marco Polo Drinking Rules

Players: 5+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Classic Marco Polo but add drinking: get tagged = take a drink and you're it. Tag someone within 30 seconds = they drink double. Nobody caught after 2 minutes = Marco drinks and everyone else is safe.

7. Frozen T-Shirt Race

Players: 2+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Freeze t-shirts in blocks of ice the night before. Race to thaw and put yours on first — use pool water, body heat, whatever. First person wearing their shirt wins. Losers chug. Watching people wrestle frozen fabric is pure gold.

8. Tipsy Toss

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Set up floating targets in the pool (hula hoops, inflatable rings). Toss balls/rings from poolside. Score zones: center = opponents drink 3 sips, middle ring = 2 sips, outer = 1 sip. Miss everything = you drink.

🏊 In-the-Water Competitions (7 Games)

Get everyone in the pool with these active, competitive water games.

9. Underwater Treasure Hunt

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Throw coins, weighted rings, or dive sticks into the pool. On "go," everyone dives to collect as many as possible. Most treasure wins. Add "golden items" worth bonus points. Play elimination rounds for intensity.

10. Chicken Fight Tournament

Players: Pairs | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Classic shoulders-mounted wrestling. Bracket-style tournament. Losing pair buys the next round of drinks. Championship match gets full crowd attention. Establish rules: no hair pulling, no scratching, pushing only.

11. Noodle Jousting

Players: 2 at a time | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Each player sits on a floatie holding a pool noodle. Joust until someone falls off. Double elimination bracket. Add foam swords for extra drama. Losers drink. Champions get a ridiculous pool noodle crown.

12. Sharks and Minnows

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

One shark in the middle of the pool. Minnows swim from one side to the other. Get tagged = become a shark. Last minnow standing wins and picks someone to chug. The childhood classic, upgraded with stakes.

13. Handstand Contest

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

Who can hold an underwater handstand the longest? Judge by how long legs stay above water. Worst attempt each round drinks. Bonus category: most creative handstand (splits, one-handed, etc.).

14. Breath Holding Challenge

Players: 2+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Simple: who can hold their breath underwater the longest? Spotter required for safety. Time each attempt. Loser drinks. Multiple rounds with increasing stakes. Set a reasonable time cap for safety.

⚠️ Always have a sober spotter. Never do breath-holding games alone or while intoxicated.

15. Whirlpool Challenge

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Everyone walks/runs in a circle to create a whirlpool current. Once it's going strong, yell "SWITCH!" Everyone tries to walk against the current. Last person to fall/give up wins. The physics are hilarious.

🦩 Floatie & Inflatable Games (7 Games)

You invested in those ridiculous inflatables — time to actually use them for more than Instagram photos.

16. Floatie Musical Chairs

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

One fewer floatie than players. Music plays, everyone swims around. Music stops, scramble for a floatie. No floatie = you're out and you drink. Remove a floatie each round. The desperation is real and entertaining.

17. King of the Float

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

One person on a large floatie. Everyone else tries to knock them off. Stay on for 60 seconds = you're the king and assign drinks. Get knocked off = drink and the person who knocked you off takes the throne.

18. Floatie Relay Race

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Paddle across the pool on a floatie, tag your teammate, they go back. Catch: you must stay on the floatie — fall off and start over. Losing team does a group cannonball while holding their drinks.

19. Inflatable Obstacle Course

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Line up floaties, pool noodles, and inflatables across the pool. Time each person navigating the course: climb over the unicorn, swim under the noodle bridge, flip onto the pizza slice. Slowest time drinks.

20. Floatie Sumo

Players: 2 at a time | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Both players stand/kneel on separate large floaties. Use pool noodles to knock the other person off. Best of 3. It's nearly impossible to stay balanced, which is exactly the point.

21. Duck Duck Splash

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Everyone sits on the pool edge. One person walks behind, tapping heads: "duck, duck, duck..." Instead of "goose," they splash someone. That person chases them — first one to jump in the pool and touch the wall loses. Loser drinks.

22. Synchronized Swimming Contest

Players: Teams of 3-4 | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Teams get 5 minutes to choreograph a synchronized swimming routine (with or without floaties). Perform for judges. Scoring: creativity, synchronization, splash factor. Worst team drinks. Best team gets immunity next round.

🎯 Poolside Classics (7 Games)

For the people who want to stay dry (or dry-ish) while still having a blast near the pool.

23. Poolside Beer Pong

Players: 2v2 | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Standard beer pong on a table near the pool. New rule: lose = jump in the pool. Alternatively, set up on the pool deck with the loser getting pushed in. The threat of water adds intensity to every shot.

24. Cornhole by the Pool

Players: 2v2 | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Set up cornhole boards on the pool deck. Standard rules to 21. Drinking addition: get a bag in the hole = opponents drink. Get skunked = jump in the pool with your clothes on. Perfect for a tournament.

25. Kan Jam Splash

Players: 2v2 | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Play Kan Jam near the pool. Any throw that goes in the pool = automatic point for the other team AND the thrower retrieves it. Adds a hilarious risk/reward to aggressive throws.

26. Limbo

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Use a pool noodle as the limbo bar. Set up near the pool edge. Fall or touch the bar = jump in the pool. Lower the noodle each round. Play tropical music. Give the champion a ridiculous lei and crown.

27. Water Balloon Toss

Players: Pairs | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Partners face each other, tossing a water balloon back and forth. Step back after each successful catch. Drop or pop = you're out (and wet). Last dry pair wins. Winning pair assigns drinks to everyone.

28. Slip and Slide Races

Players: 2+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Set up a slip and slide on the lawn near the pool. Race format — first to the end wins. Add soap for extra speed. Finish line is the pool itself for maximum entertainment. Losers drink.

29. Water Gun Fight

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Stock up on Super Soakers. Team elimination — get hit in the chest 3 times and you're out. Last team standing wins. Refill stations at the pool. Add rules: headshots = the shooter drinks as penalty.

💦 Diving & Splash Contests (6 Games)

For pools with enough depth and groups with enough audacity. Always check depth first.

30. Cannonball Contest

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Biggest splash wins. Judges sit poolside with scorecards (1-10). Categories: height, splash radius, form, and "wow factor." Smallest splash drinks. Winner gets the pool noodle scepter of power.

31. Belly Flop Competition

Players: Brave ones only | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

The reddest belly wins. Score on sound, splash, and visible redness. This is a glory-or-pain situation. Provide ice for the aftermath. Champion gets legendary status and a cold beer for their belly.

⚠️ Only in pools with proper depth. Not responsible for regret.

32. Dive Competition

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Judge on form, splash (less is better for dives), and creativity. Categories: most graceful, most ridiculous, most creative entry. Non-divers can do "pencil dives" or "starfish drops" for the comedy category.

33. Trick Jump Showdown

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Each round, someone calls a trick: "Do a spin," "Enter backwards," "Make no splash," "Hold a pose." Worst execution drinks. Escalate difficulty each round. Phone recording is mandatory for replays.

34. Splash the DJ

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Place the speaker (waterproof, obviously) at a safe distance from the pool. Take turns jumping — whoever's splash reaches closest to the speaker without hitting it wins. Hit the speaker = you're the DJ for the next hour (punishment or reward depending on taste).

35. Synchronized Jumping

Players: Pairs/groups | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Partners must jump in simultaneously doing the same move. Judges score on synchronization. Mismatched = both drink. Perfect sync = they assign drinks. Build to group jumps of 3-4 for maximum difficulty.

🏅 Team Relays & Races (7 Games)

Split into teams and race. Pool relays combine swimming, drinking, and chaos.

36. Swim and Chug Relay

Players: Teams of 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Swim one lap, get out, chug a drink, tag the next person. First team through all members wins. Spillage adds 10-second penalties. The combination of swimming effort and chugging is surprisingly brutal.

37. Noodle Race

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Ride a pool noodle like a horse across the pool. Tag your teammate. First team done wins. Fall off = restart from your side. Watching adults try to ride pool noodles at speed is peak comedy.

38. Bucket Brigade

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Line up on the pool deck. Pass water in cups over your head to fill a bucket at the end. First team to fill their bucket wins. You WILL get soaked. Losing team jumps in fully clothed.

39. Ping Pong Rescue

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Scatter ping pong balls across the pool (color-coded per team). Race to collect all your team's balls. Catch: you can only carry them in a cup held in your mouth. Drop one = it goes back in the pool.

40. Underwater Relay

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Swim one pool length entirely underwater. Surface = you restart. Each teammate goes once. Fastest total time wins. Losing team buys the next round of drinks. Test your lung capacity.

41. T-Shirt Relay

Players: Teams of 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

First person puts on an oversized wet t-shirt, swims a lap, takes it off, passes it to the next person who puts it on and swims. Wet t-shirts are impossible to put on quickly. First team done wins.

42. Ice Cube Melt Race

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥

Each team gets a large block of ice. First to completely melt it wins — use body heat, pool water, friction, whatever. No tools allowed. It's a weird, hilarious, oddly engaging competition.

🏐 Beach Ball & Ball Games (7 Games)

Every pool party needs beach balls. Here's how to make them competitive.

43. Pool Volleyball

Players: 4v4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

String a net (or pool noodle) across the middle. Standard volleyball rules adapted for water — 3 touches per side, rotate servers. Play to 15. Losing team does a chug relay. The water adds a hilarious degree of difficulty.

44. Keepaway

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Two teams, one ball. Pass to your teammates — other team tries to intercept. Score by touching the ball to the opposing pool wall. First to 5 wins. No holding someone underwater (house rule, but necessary).

45. Hot Potato (Pool Edition)

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Toss a beach ball around the pool while music plays. Music stops — whoever's touching it drinks. Refuse to catch it = automatic drink. The ball gets slippery, catches get frantic. Simple but effective.

46. Pool Basketball

Players: 2v2 or 3v3 | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Floating or poolside basketball hoop. Play to 11, make-it-take-it. Fouls = free throw (make it = they drink, miss = you drink). Dunks allowed if you can manage them in water. Gets surprisingly competitive.

47. Watermelon Ball

Players: Teams | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Grease a watermelon with petroleum jelly. Two teams try to get it to the opposing pool wall. It's like underwater rugby with a slippery melon. Impossible to grip. Absolute chaos. One of the best pool party games ever invented.

48. Target Toss

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

Float hula hoops or inflatable rings in the pool. Throw balls from the deck — score points for landing in each ring. Different distances = different points. Losers drink. Tournament bracket for serious competition.

49. Catch or Splash

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Stand in the pool. Someone throws a ball. Catch it cleanly = you're safe. Drop it = everyone splashes you AND you drink. Clean catch = assign a drink to someone. One-handed catches = assign two drinks.

🎶 Music & Dance Games (6 Games)

Good music is non-negotiable at a pool party. Make it interactive.

50. Pool Party Playlist War

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

Everyone takes turns playing their best summer song. Group rates 1-10. Lowest rated each round = that person does a penalty jump. Build the ultimate pool party playlist by the end of the day.

51. Freeze Dance (Pool Version)

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Dance in the water while music plays. Music stops = freeze. Last person to freeze or anyone who moves is out and drinks. Judging movement in water is hilariously subjective and causes arguments.

52. Lip Sync Battle Poolside

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Full performance lip sync battles on the pool deck. Use the pool as your stage prop — dramatic jumps, splash effects, poolside choreography. Audience scores each performance. Lowest score = cannonball punishment.

53. Name That Tune (Summer Edition)

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

Play 2-3 seconds of summer hits. First to name the song gets a point. Categories: summer anthems, one-hit wonders, throwback hits, songs with "summer/sun/beach" in the title. Wrong guess = sip.

54. Karaoke Cannonball

Players: Brave ones | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Sing a karaoke song from the diving board. Audience rates you. Score below 5 = you're pushed in mid-song. Score above 8 = you choose who gets pushed in. The audience has too much power and they know it.

55. Dance Battle in the Shallow End

Players: 2 at a time | Vibe: 🔥🔥

30-second dance-offs in waist-deep water. Water makes every move dramatic and every fall hilarious. Crowd picks the winner by splash volume of applause. Loser buys winner a drink from the poolside bar.

🌙 After Dark Pool Games (7 Games)

When the sun goes down, the pool party evolves. Add glow sticks, LED lights, and a whole different vibe.

56. Glow Stick Dive

Players: Any | Vibe: ✨✨✨

Throw glow sticks and glow bracelets into the pool. Turn off all lights. Dive to collect as many as possible in 60 seconds. Most glow sticks wins. The pool looks incredible and it's surprisingly fun for adults.

57. Night Swim Truth or Dare

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

Floating in the pool under the stars, play truth or dare. The ambiance makes people way more willing to share truths and accept dares. Water-based dares only: "Do a handstand for 30 seconds," "Swim a lap underwater."

58. LED Beach Ball Hot Potato

Players: 6+ | Vibe: ✨✨

Use an LED-lit beach ball. Music plays, pass it around in the dark pool. Music stops = the glowing person drinks. The visual is stunning and the game is more fun when you can't see where it's going.

59. Flashlight Tag

Players: 6+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

One person has a waterproof flashlight. Others hide/swim in the dark pool. Shine the light on someone = they're tagged and drink. Switch taggers every 2 minutes. Pool lights OFF for maximum stealth mode.

60. Glow Ring Toss

Players: Any | Vibe: ✨✨

Set up glow-in-the-dark rings on floating targets. Toss from poolside in the dark. Hits = opponents drink. Misses = you drink. The neon glow on the water creates an incredible atmosphere.

61. Stargazing Trivia

Players: Any | Vibe: Chill ✨

Float on your backs, look at the stars (or pretend to in the city). Ask space/astronomy trivia: "How far is the moon?" "What planet has the most moons?" Wrong answers = sip. Right answer = assign a sip. Incredibly peaceful game.

62. Midnight Chicken Fight Championship

Players: Pairs | Vibe: 🔥🔥🔥

The championship round of chicken fights happens after dark. Add glow necklaces so you can see who's fighting. The reduced visibility makes everything more dramatic. Final match gets full ceremony treatment.

😎 Chill Poolside Games (9 Games)

For when the energy dips or people want to relax with a drink in hand. Low-key fun, maximum enjoyment.

63. Never Have I Ever (Pool Edition)

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Classic rules, poolside setting. Add a pool twist: "Never have I ever peed in a pool" — watch the honest ones squirm. Summer-themed: "...gotten a horrible sunburn," "...lost a swimsuit to a wave," "...been stung by a jellyfish."

64. Would You Rather: Summer Edition

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

"Would you rather have eternal summer or eternal fall?" "Live at the beach or in the mountains?" "Only swim in pools or only in the ocean?" Minority vote drinks. Summer-specific questions hit different while poolside.

65. Categories

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Someone names a category (sunscreen brands, beach movies, tropical fruits). Go around the circle — first person who can't name one or repeats = drinks and starts the next category. Faster pace = more fun.

66. Story Builder

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥

One person starts a story with one sentence. Next person adds a sentence. Keep going around. The story must include: a pool, a celebrity, and something that went wrong. Most random contribution wins. Boring addition = drink.

67. Rate the Tan

Players: Any | Vibe: Silly

At the end of the day, everyone shows their tan lines (or sunburn lines). Group votes on: best tan, worst tan line, most creative burn pattern, most SPF-responsible. Worst sunburn gets aloe as their "prize."

68. Floatie Lounge Trivia

Players: Any | Vibe: 🔥

Everyone on floaties in the pool. One person reads trivia questions. Wrong answer = you get splashed by everyone. Right answer = splash someone of your choice. Lazy, fun, and you never have to leave the water.

69. Two Truths and a Swim

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥🔥

Standard two truths and a lie, but if the group correctly identifies your lie, you have to swim a full lap. If they guess wrong, THEY swim. Adds physical stakes to a classic game.

70. Sunset Cheers

Players: Any | Vibe: ✨ Perfect

As the sun sets, everyone raises their drink and shares one highlight from the day. Go around the group. No drinking penalty — just gratitude, good vibes, and a beautiful sky. The perfect way to transition from day party to night party.

71. Celebrity Float

Players: 4+ | Vibe: 🔥

While floating, each person picks a celebrity and answers questions "as" that person. Group guesses who you are. Bad impressions get splashed. If nobody guesses in 5 questions, everyone else drinks. A chill, funny closer.

Pool Party Planning Tips

  • Safety first: Designate a sober lifeguard/spotter, especially after dark. No diving in shallow pools. Seriously.
  • Sunscreen station: Set up a communal sunscreen station. Your guests will thank you tomorrow.
  • Waterproof everything: Waterproof speaker, waterproof phone cases, waterproof cups. Plan for splashing.
  • Hydration: Water bottles everywhere. Sun + alcohol + swimming = dehydration city. Alternate drinks with water.
  • Food timing: Eat BEFORE water games. Burgers + cannonballs = regret. Plan meals around activity breaks.
  • Towel surplus: Way more towels than you think. People go through 2-3 each. Seriously over-prepare.
  • Music is key: A good waterproof Bluetooth speaker makes or breaks the vibe. Make the playlist before the party.
  • Shade zones: Umbrellas, canopies, or trees for people who need sun breaks. Not everyone can be in direct sun all day.
  • Night transition: If the party goes past sunset, have pool lights, glow sticks, and LED floaties ready. The vibe shift is magical.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best pool party games for adults?

The best pool party games for adults include Pool Pong, Watermelon Ball (greased watermelon rugby), Chicken Fight tournaments, Pool Volleyball, and Floatie Musical Chairs. For drinking games, try Drink or Dive, Shark Attack, and Waterproof Flip Cup. Mix active water games with chill poolside games for the best flow.

How do you play pool beer pong?

Float two triangle formations of cups on inflatable beer pong rafts (or DIY with pool noodles). Players shoot from opposite ends of the pool. Standard beer pong rules apply — sink a cup, opponent drinks and removes it. The water current adds an extra challenge since the cups are constantly moving.

What do you need for a pool party?

Essentials: sunscreen, waterproof speaker, plenty of towels, cooler with drinks and water, food/snacks, floaties and inflatables, plastic cups (no glass near pools!), trash bags, first aid kit, and a sober lifeguard/spotter. For games: pool noodles, beach balls, ping pong balls, glow sticks (for night), and a watermelon for the best game ever.

Are pool party games safe with alcohol?

Pool safety with alcohol requires extra precautions: always have a designated sober spotter/lifeguard, avoid breath-holding games while intoxicated, alternate alcoholic drinks with water, never swim alone, skip diving in shallow pools, use plastic containers (never glass), and know everyone's swimming ability. When in doubt, keep the drinking games poolside rather than in the water.
