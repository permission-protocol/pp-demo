25+ Hilarious April Fool's Day Dares & Drinking Games for Adults (2026 Edition)

Looking to spice up your April 1st with more than just basic pranks? These April Fool's Day drinking games and dares combine the chaos of the holiday with your favorite party beverages.

Top 10 April Fool's Drinking Games

1. The "Salt or Sugar" Shot Roulette

A classic prank turned into a high-stakes drinking game.

  • The Setup: Pour 5 shots of water and 1 shot of heavily salted water.
  • The Game: Everyone takes a shot at the same time. The person who gets the salt shot has to finish their next drink.

2. "Who Am I?" (Prank Edition)

The traditional sticky-note-on-forehead game with an April Fool's twist.

  • The Twist: Instead of real people or characters, everyone writes down the most embarrassing "past version" of the player (e.g., "Middle School Goth Phase" or "That One Time You Danced on the Table").
  • Drinking Rule: If you can't guess who you are within 5 questions, you drink. If you guess correctly, everyone else drinks.

3. The "Fake Fact" Pub Quiz

Test your friends' ability to spot a lie.

  • How to Play: The host reads out a mix of obscure real facts and convincing fake ones.
  • The Rule: If you guess "Fake" and it's real, you drink. If you guess "Real" and it's a prank fact, you drink double.

4. The "Mirror" Challenge

A test of coordination and sobriety.

  • The Game: Two players stand opposite each other. Player A performs a series of increasingly complex movements. Player B must mirror them perfectly.
  • The Prank: Player A should throw in a "fake-out" move (like a sudden stop or a change in speed). If Player B messes up the mirror, they drink.

5. Reverse Beer Pong

Everything is backwards for April Fool's.

  • The Rule: Instead of the thrower assigning a drink, the thrower must drink if they make the shot. If they miss, everyone else drinks.

6. The "Frozen" Game

Someone shouts "Freeze!" and the last person to stop moving drinks.

7. The "Swap" Game

Swap your drink with the person to your left every 15 minutes.

8. The "Question Only" Game

Only speak in questions. If you make a statement, you drink.

9. The "Thumb" Game

The last person to put their thumb on the table drinks.

10. The "Animal Kingdom" Game

Assign an animal to each player. When their animal is mentioned (even accidentally), they drink.

Top 15 April Fool's Dares for Adults

  1. The "Invisible Friend" Dare: Pretend to have an invisible person with you all night. Introduce them to everyone.
  2. The "Random Accent" Dare: Speak in a different accent every 10 minutes (British, Australian, Pirate—go wild!).
  3. The "Reverse Walk" Dare: Walk backwards to the bar or bathroom every time you need to go.
  4. The "Compliment Sandwich" Dare: Tell someone something nice, something weird, then something nice again.
  5. The "Wrong Name" Dare: Call everyone by the wrong name for 30 minutes and act confused when corrected.
  6. The "Slow Motion" Dare: Move in slow motion for 2 minutes every time someone says your name.
  7. The "Mime" Dare: Mime the act of drinking instead of actually drinking for 1 full round.
  8. The "Sing-a-Long" Dare: Sing everything you want to say for 5 minutes.
  9. The "Shadow" Dare: Follow someone's movements for 3 minutes without telling them what you're doing.
  10. The "Confession" Dare: Admit to a fake, ridiculous crime you "committed" (like stealing a park bench).
  11. The "Accent Swap": Everyone must speak in the accent of the person to their right for one round.
  12. The "Fake Phone Call": Take a fake phone call and make it as awkward as possible for 2 minutes.
  13. The "New Rule": Every 30 minutes, a new rule is added (e.g., "no using the word 'drink'").
  14. The "Lava" Game: The floor is lava. The last person to get off the floor drinks.
  15. The "Meme" Challenge: Show a meme. The person who laughs first drinks.
