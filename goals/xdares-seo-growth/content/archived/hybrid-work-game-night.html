50+ Truth or Dare Questions for Hybrid Work & Remote Team Building

Remote work shouldn't mean remote relationships. In the era of hybrid work, building a cohesive team culture requires more than just "checking in" on Slack. You need interactive, low-pressure ways to break the ice and build trust across time zones.

Truth or Dare is a classic for a reason, but the standard version doesn't always translate to a professional Zoom call. That's why we've curated the ultimate list of hybrid work truth or dare questions specifically designed for remote teams.

Remote Work Truths

These "Truth" questions are designed to be office-appropriate while still revealing the fun, human side of your colleagues.

  • What is the weirdest thing you've ever done while on a conference call with your camera off?
  • Have you ever worked from your bed for an entire day?
  • What’s the most embarrassing thing that’s happened in the background of a Zoom call?
  • Do you actually have pants on right now, or just pajamas/shorts?
  • What is your go-to "I'm busy" Slack status when you're actually taking a nap?
  • Which colleague would you most want to be stuck in a real-life elevator with?
  • What’s the biggest lie you’ve told to get out of a non-mandatory meeting?
  • If you could replace the CEO with a fictional character, who would it be?
  • What is your most controversial "hot take" about our industry?
  • Which Slack channel do you mute immediately?

Zoom-Friendly Dares

Dares that work within the confines of a 720p rectangle.

  • Show us the most cluttered part of your room/office that is currently off-camera.
  • Change your Zoom background to the most embarrassing photo on your phone (within reason!).
  • Give a 30-second passionate speech about why [insert mundane office supply] is the most important invention in history.
  • Let the person to your "left" (the next person in the gallery view) choose a new Slack avatar for you for the next hour.
  • Perform a dramatic reading of the last email you received.
  • Spend the next 5 minutes only communicating through the Zoom chat using emojis.
  • Show us your favorite mug and tell the "origin story" behind it.
  • Do your best impression of the boss (keep it lighthearted!).
  • Balance a pen on your nose for 30 seconds without dropping it.
  • Find the closest object to your left and try to sell it to the team like a QVC host.

Slack & Async Dares

Perfect for hybrid teams that aren't always online at the same time.

  • Post a picture of your "desk lunch" in the #general channel.
  • Change your Slack name to a pun based on your job title for the rest of the day.
  • Share a "Work From Home" hack that sounds fake but actually works.
  • Start a thread using only GIFs to describe your current workload.
  • Nominate a "hidden talent" video or photo of yourself to the team.

Tips for a Successful Hybrid Game Night

To make sure your remote team building doesn't feel like "forced fun," follow these guidelines:

  • Keep it Voluntary: Never force someone to participate in a dare they find uncomfortable.
  • Use Breakout Rooms: For large teams, break into groups of 4-6 to ensure everyone gets a turn.
  • Establish Boundaries: Set clear "Off Limits" topics (e.g., salary, HR issues, personal family matters).
  • Reward Participation: Offer small digital prizes like Starbucks gift cards or "No Meeting Friday" passes.

FAQ

Is Truth or Dare appropriate for work?

Yes, if you use curated, professional-appropriate questions. Focus on workplace quirks, remote habits, and lighthearted challenges rather than personal secrets.

How do you play Truth or Dare on Zoom?

Use the "Gallery View" to see everyone. You can use a random name generator or the "popcorn" method where the last person to go picks the next participant.
