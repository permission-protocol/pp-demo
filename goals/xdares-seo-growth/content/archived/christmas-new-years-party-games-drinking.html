🍷 Christmas Drinking Games

Get festive with these holiday-themed drinking games. Responsible drinking, maximum merriment.

1. Christmas Movie Drinking Game

Players: 3+ | Difficulty: Easy

Pick a holiday classic and set rules. Elf: drink when Buddy says "Santa." Home Alone: drink when Kevin screams. Die Hard: drink whenever someone argues if it's a Christmas movie.

2. Santa's Naughty List

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Everyone writes down something "naughty" they did this year on a card. Read anonymously—group votes if it's naughty or nice. Majority wrong? Everyone drinks.

3. Twelve Drinks of Christmas

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Hard

Like the song, but drinking. Each round adds a drink and repeats all previous. "On the first drink of Christmas..." Gets progressively challenging.

4. Holiday Carol Karaoke

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Karaoke Christmas carols. Mess up the lyrics? Drink. Refuse to sing when it's your turn? Double drink. Nail it perfectly? Everyone else drinks.

5. Reindeer Games

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Name all Santa's reindeer in order. Get one wrong or hesitate? Drink. Add made-up reindeer for bonus rounds. Yes, "Olive the Other Reindeer" counts.

6. Christmas Story Time

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

One person starts a Christmas story with one sentence. Each person adds a sentence. Say "um" or hesitate? Drink. Story gets gloriously weird.

7. Ugly Sweater Drink

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

At an ugly sweater party, whenever someone compliments a sweater, both parties drink. Whoever has the worst sweater by vote becomes the drink distributor.

8. Gift Wrap Race

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Race to wrap a gift while drinking. Must finish drink AND wrap the gift. Judges rate wrapping quality. Worst wrapper drinks again.

🎁 White Elephant & Gift Games

The best part of holiday parties—the gift exchange chaos.

9. Classic White Elephant

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone brings a wrapped gift (usually funny or regiftable). Draw numbers, pick or steal gifts. Item can be stolen 3 times before it's "dead." Drama guaranteed.

10. Dirty Santa

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Similar to White Elephant, but with more aggressive stealing. No steal limits until your turn ends. Alliances form. Feuds begin. Perfect holiday chaos.

11. Left Right Christmas Game

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone holds a gift. Read a Christmas story with "left" and "right" throughout. Pass gifts in that direction when you hear the word. Keep what you're holding at the end.

12. Dice Gift Exchange

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Roll dice to determine actions: 1 = unwrap, 2 = steal, 3 = swap with someone, 4 = pass left, 5 = pass right, 6 = keep. Timer adds pressure.

13. Secret Santa Reveal

Players: 5+ | Difficulty: Easy

After Secret Santa gifts are opened, everyone guesses who gave what. Points for correct guesses. Reveals often more entertaining than the gifts.

14. Gift Auction

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Everyone gets play money. Auctioneer reveals gifts one by one. Bid on what you want. Strategic and competitive. Big spenders often regret it.

15. Speed Unwrap

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Gifts are wrapped in multiple layers. Set a timer, pass the gift. When timer stops, that person removes one layer. Keep passing until someone reaches the prize.

16. Mystery Gift Pictionary

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Without looking inside, artists draw what they THINK their wrapped gift is based on weight, shape, and sound. Then reveal. Most accurate guess wins.

🎆 New Year's Eve Countdown Games

Build excitement as midnight approaches with these countdown activities.

17. Resolution Roulette

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Write resolutions on slips of paper. Mix them up. Each person draws and reads one—everyone guesses who wrote it. Wrong guesses = drink at midnight.

18. Year in Review Quiz

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Trivia about events from the past year. News, pop culture, personal group moments. Test how well everyone paid attention to the year.

19. Predictions Party

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone writes predictions for the new year (personal and world events). Seal in envelopes. Open at next year's party. See who was most accurate.

20. Hourly Challenges

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Various

Each hour before midnight has a different mini-challenge: 9 PM trivia, 10 PM dance-off, 11 PM toast competition. Builds momentum to midnight.

21. New Year's Eve Bingo

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Create bingo cards with NYE clichés (someone says "new year, new me," champagne cork pops, drunk text gets sent). First to bingo wins.

22. Countdown Shots

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Paced

Plan specific shots for each hour leading to midnight. Different themes or colors. Midnight = champagne toast. Pace yourselves—it's a marathon.

23. The Time Capsule

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone contributes something to a group time capsule: notes, photos, predictions, small objects. Seal it. Open same time next year.

24. Best/Worst of the Year

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Go around sharing: best moment, worst moment, most unexpected moment, thing you're most grateful for. Reflection game that sparks real conversation.

📚 Holiday Trivia & Knowledge Games

Test holiday knowledge and settle debates once and for all.

25. Name That Carol

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Play 5 seconds of a Christmas song. First to correctly name it gets a point. Obscure carols = bonus points. Mariah Carey = obvious but still valid.

26. Holiday Movie Quote Challenge

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

"You'll shoot your eye out!" Name that movie. Classic holiday movie quotes, teams compete to identify them first. Deep cuts separate the fans from the casuals.

27. Christmas Around the World

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Hard

Trivia about how different countries celebrate Christmas. Match traditions to countries. Learn something new while competing.

28. Holiday History

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Questions about the history of Christmas traditions: Why do we have Christmas trees? When did Santa's red suit become standard? Origins of traditions.

29. Finish the Lyric

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Read partial Christmas song lyrics. First to correctly finish gets the point. "Last Christmas, I gave you my..." Everyone knows, but under pressure?

30. Two Truths and a Lie: Holiday Edition

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Share two true holiday facts/memories and one fake. Others guess the lie. Works for historical facts or personal stories.

31. Guess the Gift

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

One person thinks of a gift. Others ask yes/no questions to figure out what it is. Limit questions to 20. Get specific: "Is it something you'd buy at Target?"

32. Holiday Jeopardy

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Create a Jeopardy board with categories: Classic Movies, Holiday Songs, Traditions, Santa Lore, NYE History. Teams compete. Final Jeopardy for the win.

🎬 Christmas Movie Games

For movie marathons with a competitive edge.

33. Christmas Movie Draft

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Draft Christmas movies like fantasy sports. Take turns picking. Then vote on which collection is the best overall lineup. Trades allowed.

34. Die Hard Debate

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Formal debate: Is Die Hard a Christmas movie? Two teams, timed arguments. Audience votes. Loser accepts the verdict (until next year).

35. Movie Scene Charades

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Medium

Act out famous Christmas movie scenes. Teams guess the movie. "Leg lamp" scene from A Christmas Story. "KEVIN!" from Home Alone.

36. Hallmark Movie Bingo

Players: 3+ | Difficulty: Easy

Watch any Hallmark Christmas movie. Bingo cards with tropes: big city girl returns to small town, saved by Christmas miracle, kiss in snow. Mark as they happen.

37. Christmas Movie Bracket

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Create a tournament bracket of Christmas movies. Vote head-to-head. Crown the ultimate champion. Arguments will be passionate.

38. Scene It: Holiday Edition

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Play clips from holiday movies. Identify the movie, character, or next line. Points for speed. Board game versions exist, or DIY with YouTube.

39. Movie Quote Telephone

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Fun

Whisper a movie quote down the line. See how mangled it gets. "Every time a bell rings an angel gets its wings" becomes... something else.

40. Christmas Movie Couples

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Match characters to their movie. Kevin McCallister? Home Alone. Buddy the Elf? Elf. Speed round for bonus points.

⚔️ Team Competitions

Split into teams for bigger challenges and bragging rights.

41. Ornament Decorating Contest

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Crafty

Teams decorate ornaments in a time limit. Judge on creativity, theme adherence, and overall aesthetic. Winners' ornaments go on the tree.

42. Gingerbread House Build-Off

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Crafty

Teams build gingerbread houses with the same materials. Timer adds pressure. Judge on structural integrity, creativity, and most candy used.

43. Holiday Relay Races

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Active

Relay challenges: wrap a gift while wearing mittens, string lights on a mini tree, decorate a cookie and eat it. First team done wins.

44. Snowball Fight (Indoor)

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Active

Crumpled white paper or cotton balls as snowballs. Two teams, divide the room. Most "snowballs" on opponent's side when timer ends = they lose.

45. Christmas Charades

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Act out holiday-themed words and phrases: wrapping presents, decorating the tree, sitting on Santa's lap. Teams race to guess correctly.

46. Holiday Lip Sync Battle

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Teams perform lip sync routines to Christmas songs. Judged on performance, enthusiasm, and crowd reaction. Mariah Carey = high difficulty, high reward.

47. Christmas Minute to Win It

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Various

One-minute challenges: stack ornaments, blow cotton balls across a table, candy cane fishing. Teams accumulate points across challenges.

48. Snowman Building Contest

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

If you have real snow, classic competition. Indoors: build "snowmen" from craft supplies or toilet paper. Most creative design wins.

🧊 Holiday Icebreakers

Get the party started and mix people who don't know each other well.

49. Holiday Would You Rather

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Would you rather only listen to Christmas music forever or never hear it again? Work Christmas Eve or New Year's Day? Quick rounds, revealing answers.

50. Naughty or Nice List

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Host reads scenarios. Everyone votes if it's naughty or nice. "Regifting a present" — naughty or nice? Debates welcome.

51. Find Your Holiday Twin

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Mingle and find someone who shares: favorite Christmas movie, holiday food, or NYE tradition. Report back to the group what you have in common.

52. Holiday Emoji Guess

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Show emoji combinations that represent holiday movies or songs. 🏠🎄🔫 = Home Alone. Teams guess. Fast-paced and modern.

53. Most Likely To: Holiday Edition

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Read "most likely to" prompts. Everyone points to who fits best. "Most likely to start singing carols unprompted." See who gets the most votes.

54. Holiday Human Bingo

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

Bingo cards with traits: "Has a real tree," "Has seen Elf 10+ times," "Born in December." Find people who match each square. First to bingo wins.

55. Gift Giving Styles

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone describes their gift-giving style: last-minute shopper, handmade gifter, gift card defaulter. Find your tribe.

56. New Year's Kiss History

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Optional sharing: best midnight kiss story, worst midnight kiss story, most memorable. Keep it PG or don't—know your audience.

🎵 Holiday Music Games

For groups that love to sing, compete, or just enjoy holiday tunes.

57. Carol Karaoke Battle

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Karaoke showdown with Christmas songs. Judges rate performances. Duets allowed. Winner gets a festive prize.

58. Name That Tune: Christmas Edition

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Play the first few notes of Christmas songs. First to buzz in with correct title wins the point. Shorter clips = harder.

59. Jingle Bell Rock Off

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Dance competition to Christmas music. Judges score on moves, enthusiasm, and festive flair. Group voting for the champion.

60. Lyric Writing Challenge

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Give everyone the same Christmas tune. 10 minutes to write new, funny lyrics. Perform and vote on the best.

61. Song Association: Holiday

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Host says a word. First person to sing a Christmas song containing that word gets a point. "Snow" — "Let it snow, let it snow..."

62. Holiday Playlist Battle

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone queues 5 songs for the ultimate holiday playlist. Play snippets and vote. Winner controls the party music.

63. Finish the Carol

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Start singing a carol and stop mid-line. Next person must continue correctly. Stumble? You're out. Last singer standing wins.

64. Silent Night Challenge

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Hard

Lip sync Christmas songs while others guess. No sounds allowed. Exaggerated mouth movements help. Harder than it seems.

🕛 Midnight & After Party Games

Games for when the clock strikes twelve and the real celebration begins.

65. Midnight Toast Competition

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone gives a short midnight toast (30 seconds max). Vote on most heartfelt, funniest, and most likely to make someone cry.

66. First Steps Challenge

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

First thing you do in the new year matters. Everyone declares their "first act of the new year" at midnight. Silly or serious.

67. Resolution Roast

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Fun

Share last year's resolutions. Group roasts everyone (lovingly) for what didn't happen. Then share new ones for more future roasting.

68. Spin the Bottle: New Year's Edition

Players: 6+ | Difficulty: Fun

Instead of kissing, spin determines who gives a compliment, shares a memory together, or makes a prediction about the other person's year.

69. Confession Time

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Medium

Start fresh: admit something from the past year. Could be small ("I ate your leftovers") or meaningful. Clean slate for the new year.

70. Dance Marathon

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Endurance

After midnight, hit the dance floor. Last person dancing wins. No sitting, no stopping. Energy comes in waves.

71. First Photo of the Year

Players: Any | Difficulty: Easy

Group photo immediately after midnight. Make it silly, make it memorable. This photo lives forever on social media.

72. New Year Superlatives

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Vote on the past year's superlatives: best party moment, most improved, most surprising news, best outfit of the year.

🌙 Chill Holiday Games

For winding down, late nights, and cozy vibes.

73. Holiday 20 Questions

Players: 3+ | Difficulty: Easy

Think of a Christmas item. Others ask yes/no questions. Is it edible? Is it a decoration? Simple but engaging.

74. Cozy Story Time

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Take turns sharing favorite holiday memories. Could be childhood, recent years, or made-up "what if" scenarios.

75. Letter to Next Year

Players: Any | Difficulty: Reflective

Everyone writes a letter to their future self. Seal in envelopes. Open same time next year. See what changed.

76. Chill Trivia

Players: 3+ | Difficulty: Easy

Low-stakes trivia about the year. No competition, just conversation starters. "Remember when...?"

77. Holiday This or That

Players: 3+ | Difficulty: Easy

Quick choices: Real tree or fake? Open presents Christmas Eve or morning? Champagne or cocktails at midnight?

78. Gratitude Circle

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

End the night saying something you're grateful for about each person present. Heartfelt way to close out the celebration.

79. Favorite Memory Share

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

Everyone shares their favorite moment from this specific party. Real-time reflection makes moments more meaningful.

80. Planning Next Year

Players: 4+ | Difficulty: Easy

While you're together, plan next year's party. Who hosts? Theme ideas? Date? Make commitments while the energy is high.

Tips for Epic Holiday Parties

  • Plan the flow: Start with icebreakers, build to active games, end with chill activities
  • Mix game types: Balance competitive, creative, and reflective games
  • Prepare supplies: Cards, dice, paper, pens, and prizes ready to go
  • Time your drinks: Pace drinking games throughout the night, especially before midnight
  • Capture moments: Designate a photographer or set up a photo booth
  • Have prizes: Small prizes make games more competitive (and fun)

Frequently Asked Questions

What are good Christmas party games for adults?

Popular Christmas party games for adults include White Elephant gift exchanges, Christmas Movie Drinking Games, Holiday Trivia, Ugly Sweater contests, and Gingerbread House Build-Offs. Mix competitive and social games for the best party experience.

What games do you play at New Year's Eve parties?

New Year's Eve party games include Resolution Roulette, Year in Review Quiz, Hourly Challenges leading to midnight, Countdown Bingo, Predictions Party, and Midnight Toast Competition. Build anticipation with games that intensify as midnight approaches.

What are fun drinking games for Christmas parties?

Christmas drinking games include Christmas Movie Drinking Game (set rules for holiday films), Santa's Naughty List, Twelve Drinks of Christmas, Holiday Carol Karaoke with drinking penalties, and Ugly Sweater Drink. Always drink responsibly and pace yourselves.

How do you make a Christmas party more fun for adults?

Make Christmas parties more fun with structured activities: gift exchange games (White Elephant, Dirty Santa), team competitions (decorating contests, relay races), holiday trivia, and themed drinking games. Having planned activities prevents awkward mingling and keeps energy high.

What do adults do at New Year's Eve parties?

Adults at NYE parties typically do midnight countdowns with champagne, make resolutions, play party games, dance, reflect on the past year, and plan for the new one. Adding structured games like predictions, toasts, and challenges makes the wait for midnight more engaging.
