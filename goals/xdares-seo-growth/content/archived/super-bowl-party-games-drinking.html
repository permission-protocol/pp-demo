Super Bowl Party Games & Drinking Games for Adults: 50+ Ideas for the Big Game

This guide has 50+ Super Bowl party games for every type of guest: drinking games for the rowdy crowd, betting games that don't require football knowledge, commercial activities for the ad enthusiasts, and halftime games to fill that 20-minute break. 🍺 Super Bowl Drinking Games

Turn every play into a party. These games keep everyone engaged with the action—just remember it's a 3+ hour event, so pace accordingly!

1. Touchdown Drinks

Simple and effective: when either team scores a touchdown, everyone takes a drink. Extra point = sip. Two-point conversion = extra sip. Field goal = half drink. Safety = finish your drink (they're rare, so celebrate). Pick a team for bonus rules: your team scores, opponents drink.

Players: Any | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺 (depends on the game)

2. Penalty Shot

Every time there's a penalty flag, the person who last spoke takes a drink. If the penalty is declined, they take another. Challenged play? Everyone drinks while waiting for the review. Overturned call? Social drink.

Players: 3+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺

3. Announcer Bingo Drinking

Create bingo cards with things announcers always say: "He's a real team player," "They came to play today," "That's a football move," player name mentions, stat references. Get five in a row? Make someone else finish their drink.

Players: Any | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

4. The Quarterback Game

Everyone picks a quarterback at the start. Every time YOUR quarterback:

  • Gets sacked → You drink
  • Throws an incompletion → Sip
  • Throws an interception → Finish your drink
  • Throws a touchdown → Everyone else drinks
  • Gets the camera close-up → Social drink

Players: 2+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

5. Flip Cup: AFC vs. NFC

Teams represent the two conferences. Play during commercial breaks. Winning team's conference gets bragging points. Running tournament throughout the game—final championship during the two-minute warning.

Players: 6+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺🍺

6. The "Drink When" Classic

Drink when:

  • Camera shows celebrities in the crowd
  • Coaches challenge a play
  • Someone mentions "the big game" (copyright dodge)
  • A player does a celebration dance
  • The camera shows someone's family in the stands
  • Announcers mention a player's college
  • There's a controversial call

Players: Any | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

7. Kings Cup: Football Edition

During timeouts and commercial breaks, play Kings with football rules. Ace = "Audible" (you call a rule), King = pour into the championship cup, etc. Losing team at the end of the game has their designated person drink the cup.

Players: 4+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

💰 Betting & Prediction Games

You don't need Vegas to make things interesting. These games work with money, chores, or just bragging rights.

8. Prop Bet Sheets

Create sheets with fun prop bets everyone fills out before the game:

  • Coin toss result (heads/tails)
  • First team to score
  • First scoring play (TD, FG, safety)
  • Total combined points (over/under 50)
  • Will there be a pick-six?
  • Color of Gatorade shower on winning coach
  • Length of national anthem (over/under 2 minutes)
  • First commercial category (beer, car, tech, food)

Most correct answers wins the pot.

Players: Any | Stakes: Your choice

9. Quarter-by-Quarter Predictions

Before each quarter, everyone predicts the score at the end of that quarter. Closest to actual score wins a point. Most points at the end wins. Ties? Closest total prediction breaks it.

Players: 2+ | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

10. The Over/Under Game

Take turns setting over/unders on random things:

  • Over/under 3.5 touchdowns in the first half?
  • Over/under 100 rushing yards for [player]?
  • Over/under 2 challenged plays?

Everyone bets over or under. Minority side drinks when resolved.

Players: 3+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺

11. Celebrity Sighting Pool

Everyone draws a celebrity name from a hat before the game. First person whose celebrity gets shown on camera wins. No celebrities drawn? Closest to halftime show runtime wins.

Players: 5+ | Competition Level: 💪💪

12. Play Type Roulette

Before each offensive play, everyone calls out a prediction: run, short pass, long pass, penalty. Correct prediction = immunity. Wrong = drink when the play resolves. Gets intense in crunch time.

Players: 3+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

13. First and Last Pool

Bet on "firsts" and "lasts":

  • First touchdown scorer
  • Last touchdown scorer
  • First penalty
  • First turnover
  • Last person to score any points

$1-5 per bet, winner takes the category pot.

Players: 4+ | Stakes: Low

📺 Commercial Break Games

Super Bowl commercials are half the show. These games make the ad breaks as competitive as the game itself.

14. Commercial Bingo

Create bingo cards with predictable commercial elements: beer ad, car ad, talking animal, celebrity cameo, movie trailer, emotional piano music, tech company, insurance mascot, food delivery app, "game day" reference. First to bingo wins.

Players: Any | Competition Level: 💪💪

15. Rate the Commercials

Everyone rates each commercial 1-10 on their phone. At the end, tally average scores. Crown the best and worst commercials democratically. Most controversial rating (farthest from average) drinks.

Players: 4+ | Engagement Level: 🗣️🗣️🗣️

16. Commercial Draft

Before the game, draft commercial categories (beer, cars, tech, food, etc.). Every time "your" category airs, you get a point. Most points at the end wins. Strategic—some categories air more than others!

Players: 4+ | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

17. Guess the Product

When a commercial starts, first person to correctly guess the product/brand wins a point. Wrong guesses = drink. Some commercials are intentionally misleading—that's the fun.

Players: 3+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺

18. Commercial Drinking Rules

Drink when:

  • A commercial makes you laugh (honor system)
  • A commercial uses a celebrity
  • A commercial has a talking animal
  • A commercial tries to make you cry
  • A commercial is a movie trailer
  • A commercial references the Super Bowl without saying "Super Bowl"

Players: Any | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

19. Commercial Charades

During long commercial breaks, act out past Super Bowl commercials (Budweiser Clydesdales, Apple 1984, Terry Tate Office Linebacker). First to guess gets a point. Great for commercial nerds.

Players: 4+ | Fun Level: 😂😂😂

🎤 Halftime Show Games

Twenty minutes of halftime? That's prime game time. Here's how to fill it.

20. Halftime Show Bingo

Create bingo cards with halftime show predictions: specific songs, costume changes, pyrotechnics, guest appearance, crowd shots, athlete reactions, wardrobe malfunction reference (it's been 20 years, we can joke), emotional ballad, dance break.

Players: Any | Competition Level: 💪💪

21. Song Prediction Pool

Everyone lists 5 songs they think the halftime performer will play. One point per correct song. Bonus point for correct opener and closer. Most points wins.

Players: 3+ | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

22. Karaoke Halftime

Mute the halftime show and have guests perform instead. Vote on best performance. Winner gets to pick the post-game activity (or pick the next song someone has to sing).

Players: 4+ (brave ones) | Entertainment Level: 🎤🎤🎤

23. Halftime Flip Cup Tournament

Use halftime for a proper flip cup tournament. Bracket-style elimination. Championship round during the two-minute warning. Losers do a halftime show dance.

Players: 8+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺🍺

24. Guest Artist Betting

Will there be a surprise guest? Everyone places bets on who might appear. Correct guess = winner's pick of leftovers. Wrong guess = kitchen cleanup crew.

Players: Any | Stakes: Chores

25. Dance-Along Challenge

During the halftime show, anyone can call "DANCE CHALLENGE"—everyone has to dance until the current song ends. Person who stops first takes a drink. Last one standing picks the next challenger.

Players: 4+ | Energy Level: 💃💃💃

📊 Super Bowl Squares Guide

The classic party game. Here's everything you need to run it properly.

26. How to Set Up Squares

  1. Draw a 10x10 grid (100 squares total)
  2. Label one team across the top, one down the side
  3. Have people buy/claim squares (put their name in boxes)
  4. Once all squares are filled, randomly assign numbers 0-9 to each row and column
  5. At the end of each quarter, check the last digit of each team's score
  6. The person whose square intersects those two numbers wins that quarter

Example: End of Q1, score is Chiefs 14, Eagles 7. The person with the square at row 4 (Chiefs) and column 7 (Eagles) wins.

27. Squares Variations

  • Reverse Squares: Losing score wins (least desirable numbers become good)
  • Halftime Double: Halftime winner gets double payout
  • Final Score Jackpot: Final score wins the biggest pot
  • Re-Draw: Redraw numbers at halftime for second half action

28. Stakes Ideas

  • Money: $1-10 per square, split pot by quarter (10%, 20%, 20%, 50%)
  • Chores: Quarterly winners assign chores to losers
  • Drinks: Winners make losers drink, or winners get drink tickets
  • Bragging Rights: Just for glory and trash talk

🏆 Team Competition Games

Divide your guests into teams and compete throughout the game.

29. Jersey Team Draft

Everyone wearing a team jersey is on that team. No jersey? Get drafted by team captains. Teams compete in mini-games during commercial breaks. Losing team does dishes.

Players: 6+ | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

30. Beer Pong: Championship Series

Tournament-style beer pong during commercial breaks. Name teams after NFL teams. Championship round during halftime. Trophy (empty bottle) goes to winners.

Players: 8+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺🍺

31. Tailgate Relay Race

During halftime: teams race to complete tailgate tasks—set up chairs, flip burgers (imaginary or real), shotgun a drink, throw a football through a target. First team done wins.

Players: 8+ | Energy Level: 🏃🏃🏃

32. Football Toss

Set up a target (trash can, laundry basket, through a tire). Each team gets 5 throws per commercial break. Most cumulative points at the end wins. Closest to game-winning team's final score gets bonus.

Players: 4+ | Competition Level: 💪💪

33. Offense vs. Defense

Teams alternate being "offense" and "defense." Offense tries to complete challenges (name 5 Super Bowl winners, throw a spiral, chug fastest). Defense tries to distract without touching. Failed offense = drink.

Players: 6+ | Chaos Level: 🔥🔥🔥

34. The MVP Vote

At the end of the game, vote for party MVP (not game MVP). Best trash talk, best snack contribution, funniest moment, best prediction. Winner gets the party trophy (can be anything ridiculous).

Players: Any | Glory Level: 🏆🏆🏆

🍕 Food Competition Games

Super Bowl parties are as much about food as football. Make it competitive!

35. Wing Eating Contest

Classic competition. Most wings in 2 minutes wins. Categories: most wings, cleanest bones, hottest sauce survived. Losers buy pizza for the next gathering.

Players: 3+ (brave ones) | Intensity: 🔥🔥🔥

36. Best Dip Competition

Everyone brings a dip. Blind tasting. Vote for the best (can't vote for your own). Winner gets a trophy (golden chip?). This alone can generate serious rivalry.

Players: 4+ dips | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

37. Chili Cook-Off

Multiple people bring chili. Numbered samples, blind voting. Best chili wins the ladle of honor. Host's chili gets automatic entry but no advantages.

Players: 3+ chilis | Competition Level: 💪💪💪

38. Guess the Food

Blindfolded taste test. Try to identify Super Bowl party foods: different chip flavors, dips, wings sauces, etc. Most correct guesses wins. Wrong guess = drink.

Players: 4+ | Fun Level: 😂😂😂

39. Hot Sauce Challenge

Line up hot sauces mild to extreme. Anyone can challenge anyone to the next level. Refuse = drink. Accept and fail (need milk) = drink. Complete the whole lineup = legend status.

Players: 2+ (brave) | Intensity: 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥

40. Food Prediction

Before the party, predict: first food to run out, most popular dish, most ignored dish. Correct predictions = points. Use for tiebreakers or bragging.

Players: Any | Meta Level: 🧠🧠

🧠 Football Trivia Games

Test everyone's NFL knowledge. Great during commercial breaks or timeouts.

41. Super Bowl History Trivia

Sample questions:

  • Who has won the most Super Bowls? (Tom Brady - 7)
  • What team has lost the most Super Bowls? (Bills & Patriots tied - 5)
  • What was the biggest blowout? (Super Bowl XXIV, 49ers 55-10 over Broncos)
  • Who scored the first Super Bowl touchdown? (Max McGee, Packers)
  • What Super Bowl had the famous "helmet catch"? (XLII)

Players: 2+ | Knowledge Level: 🧠🧠🧠

42. Name That Team

Give clues about NFL teams without saying the team name. "This team's fans call themselves 'The 12th Man'" (Seahawks). First to guess wins the point.

Players: 3+ | Knowledge Level: 🧠🧠

43. Jersey Number Challenge

Name famous players by their number. "Who wore 12 for the Patriots?" (Brady). "Who wore 85 for the Bengals?" (Chad Johnson/Ochocinco). Gets harder with older players.

Players: 2+ | Knowledge Level: 🧠🧠🧠

44. Mascot Madness

Name the mascot for each NFL team (harder than you think—some don't have official ones!). Bonus: Draw them from memory for extra points/laughs.

Players: 2+ | Knowledge Level: 🧠🧠

45. Commercial Trivia

Questions about famous Super Bowl commercials:

  • What year was Apple's "1984" commercial? (Super Bowl XVIII, 1984)
  • What company has the Clydesdale horses? (Budweiser)
  • What commercial featured Betty White playing football? (Snickers, 2010)
  • What was the "Whassup" commercial for? (Budweiser, 1999)

Players: 2+ | Knowledge Level: 🧠🧠

🎮 Casual Party Games

For when you want entertainment that doesn't require constant football attention.

46. Never Have I Ever: Football Edition

Football-themed prompts: "Never have I ever painted my face for a game," "Never have I ever cried after a loss," "Never have I ever worn a jersey of a team I don't follow," "Never have I ever pretended to understand football."

Players: 4+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺🍺

47. Most Likely To: Game Day Edition

"Most likely to start a fight over a call," "Most likely to fall asleep by halftime," "Most likely to care more about commercials than the game," "Most likely to eat all the wings." Point at who fits—most fingers pointed drinks.

Players: 4+ | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺

48. Two Truths and a Lie: Sports Edition

Share two true and one false sports fact or personal sports story. Everyone guesses the lie. Sports-clueless people can make up believable "facts"—that's half the fun.

Players: 4+ | Engagement Level: 🗣️🗣️🗣️

49. Would You Rather: NFL Edition

"Would you rather have courtside seats to every game of your team or one Super Bowl ticket?" "Would you rather be an NFL ref or a waterboy?" "Would you rather your team never win a Super Bowl or fold entirely?"

Players: 2+ | Engagement Level: 🗣️🗣️🗣️

50. The Jinx Game

Every time someone "jinxes" their team (says something overconfident), they have to knock on wood AND drink. If their jinx comes true (team fails right after), double drink. Gets superstitious fast.

Players: Any | Buzz Level: 🍺🍺

51. Truth or Dare: Timeout Edition

Play only during official timeouts. Quick rounds—when play resumes, the game pauses. Football-themed dares: "Do a touchdown celebration," "Impersonate a coach," "Give a halftime motivational speech."

Players: 4+ | Fun Level: 😂😂😂

🎯 Super Bowl Party Tips

Before the Party

  • Test your setup: Make sure everyone can see the TV—no one wants to watch the game in a mirror
  • Prep game materials: Print bingo cards, squares grids, and prop bet sheets ahead of time
  • Plan food timing: Apps and snacks for arrival, main food for halftime, dessert for 4th quarter
  • Set expectations: Let people know if it's a "serious watching" party or a "social with football on" party

During the Game

  • Announce commercial breaks: "Games are starting!" gets people back to the TV
  • Pace the drinking games: It's a 3+ hour event—marathon, not sprint
  • Keep non-fans engaged: Commercial games, food competitions, and halftime activities
  • Manage the volume: Loud for big plays, reasonable for conversation during slow moments

For Non-Football Fans

  • Commercial focus: The ads are genuinely entertaining—rate them, bet on them
  • Halftime show: Make it an event—it's basically a concert
  • Food competitions: Everyone can participate regardless of football knowledge
  • Prop bets on random stuff: Coin toss, Gatorade color—no football IQ required
  • Separate social area: Some people just want to hang out—that's okay!

After the Game

  • Award ceremony: Announce squares winners, prop bet winners, game champions
  • Clean-up assignments: Losers of various games get specific duties
  • Next year planning: Host? Location? Start the trash talk early

Win Your Super Bowl Party

The best Super Bowl parties have something for everyone: die-hard fans glued to every play, casual viewers watching for commercials, and people just there for the food and company. The right games bridge all those groups.

Start simple: Super Bowl squares and commercial bingo are easy wins. Add drinking games for the rowdy crowd. Keep halftime entertaining with competitions. And always, always have good food.

Whether your team's playing or you couldn't name a single player, you now have 50+ ways to make Super Bowl Sunday legendary. The game's just the backdrop—the party is what you remember. 🏈

Now go forth and host like a champion.
