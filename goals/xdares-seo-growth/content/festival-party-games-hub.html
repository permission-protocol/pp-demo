Coachella & Desert Party Games

The desert heat requires a specific kind of fun. These games are designed for the Indio heat, pool parties, and late-night tent hangs.

  • The Coachella Playbook: 15 specific games for the desert, including the "Heatwave Dare" and "Indio Influencer Bingo."
  • Pool Party Drinking Games: From "Marco Polo with a Twist" to "Inflatable Float Jousting."
  • Celebrity Spotting Roulette: Pick a list of likely attendees and set specific dares for the first person to spot them (or their double).

EDM Drinking Games & Rave Dares

High energy, neon lights, and heavy bass. These games are built for the EDM crowd where the drops are heavy and the dares are wilder.

  • EDM Drinking Games: Beats, drops, and neon dares. The "Drop-Shot" and "PLUR Dare" are rave essentials.
  • Glow Stick Roulette: Activate a glow stick in the dark—whoever it points to when it stops spinning takes a dare.
  • Bass Drop Challenges: Everyone must hold a specific pose or perform a task until the bass drops.

Campsite Icebreakers & Morning Starters

The campsite is where the real memories are made. These low-energy, high-social games are perfect for the morning recovery or the pre-show hype.

  • Icebreaker Games: Get to know your camp neighbors quickly with "Two Truths and a Lie" or "Most Likely To."
  • Hydration Dares: Turn drinking water into a game to keep the group safe (and hyped) throughout the day.
  • Tent-to-Tent Trivia: Challenge the neighboring camp to a quick round of music trivia for "campsite clout."

Fashion & Outfit Challenges

Festival fashion is a game in itself. Why not lean into it?

  • Outfit Swap Dares: Swap one accessory or item with a friend (or a willing stranger) for an hour.
  • Glitter Bomb Roulette: Whoever loses the dare gets "glittered" by the group.
  • Influencer Pose-Off: Compete for the best (or most ridiculous) "candid" photo at the most iconic festival spots.
