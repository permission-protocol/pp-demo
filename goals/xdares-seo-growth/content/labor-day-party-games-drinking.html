55+ Labor Day Party Games & End of Summer Drinking Games for Adults (2026)

Labor Day marks the unofficial end of summer—that bittersweet moment when we squeeze every last drop of warm-weather fun before autumn arrives. It's the last chance for backyard BBQs, pool parties, and outdoor games before everyone retreats indoors. So why waste it with a boring cookout when you could send summer off in style?

Whether you're hosting the neighborhood's final bash or just getting together with friends for one more grilling session, these games turn any Labor Day gathering into a legendary farewell to summer. From lawn game tournaments to end-of-summer drinking games, we've got 55+ ways to make September's first weekend unforgettable.

What you'll find: Classic BBQ drinking games, lawn game tournaments, pool and water games, end-of-summer celebration games, grilling competitions, outdoor Olympics, summer recap activities, team challenges, and chill sunset games. Everything you need for the ultimate Labor Day party.

🍖 BBQ & Grilling Drinking Games

Labor Day and BBQ go together like burgers and beer. These games turn the grill master into a competitor and give everyone something to do while waiting for food.

1. Grill Master Challenge

How to play: Multiple grill stations, each person cooks their signature dish. Blind taste test voting determines the winner. Losers drink, winner gets to create one party rule. Bonus: burned food = automatic shot.

2. Burger Building Race

How to play: All ingredients laid out. On "go," race to build and eat a complete burger. First to swallow their last bite wins and gives out drinks. Drop any ingredient = drink and restart that step.

3. BBQ Trivia

How to play: Questions about grilling, BBQ styles, meat cuts, and cookout traditions. Wrong answers = drink. Categories: Regional BBQ (Texas vs. Carolina vs. Kansas City), Meat Knowledge, Grill Skills, Side Dishes.

4. Condiment Roulette

How to play: Spin a wheel of condiments. Whatever it lands on, you must put on your next bite. Refuse = drink double. Successfully eat it = give out drinks. Hot sauce landing = shot for the bold.

5. "Is It Done Yet?"

How to play: Grill master asks guests to guess when the meat is done. Closest guess without going under wins. Furthest off drinks. If anyone guesses the exact time, grill master drinks.

6. Hot Dog Eating Mini-Contest

How to play: Not a full competitive eating contest—just 3 hot dogs, who finishes first. Winner gives out 3 drinks. Last place does a shot. Anyone who can't finish drinks the difference in dogs.

🎯 Lawn Game Tournaments

Labor Day is peak lawn game season. Set up tournament brackets and let the competition decide summer's champion.

7. Cornhole Championship

How to play: Double elimination bracket. Standard rules (3 points for bags in the hole, 1 point on the board). Drinking rules: First team to 21 wins, losers drink the point difference. Bag bounces off = drink.

Tournament tip: 8 teams makes a perfect bracket. Single games to 11 for preliminary rounds, finals to 21.

8. Ladder Golf Drinking Edition

How to play: Three rungs = 1, 2, 3 points. First to exactly 21 wins. Go over = reset to 15 and drink. Wrap around the top rung = opponent drinks. Miss all rungs = drink.

9. Horseshoes Classic

How to play: Traditional horseshoes with drinking stakes. Ringer = give out 3 drinks. Leaner = give out 2. Closest = give out 1. Completely miss the pit = drink. First to 21 wins, loser does a shot.

10. Kan Jam Tournament

How to play: Bucket shot (instant win) = opponent team chugs. Redirect in = give out 3. Direct hit = give out 2. Dinger = give out 1. Complete miss = thrower drinks.

11. Bocce Ball

How to play: Closest balls to the pallino score. Furthest from pallino each round = that team drinks. Knock the pallino = do a shot (good or bad). First to 12 wins; losers finish their drinks.

12. Giant Jenga Tower

How to play: Write rules on blocks or play standard with drinking stakes. Knock the tower = finish your drink plus a shot. Successfully pull = give out drinks equal to the current tower height (count visible rows).

🏊 Pool & Water Games

If you have access to a pool, make the most of it—this might be the last swim of the season. No pool? Sprinklers and water balloons work too.

13. Pool Pong

How to play: Floating cups (or cups on a raft). Same rules as beer pong but you're in the pool. Miss badly = swim a lap. Sink a cup = opponent drinks and swims to retrieve the ball.

14. Water Balloon Roulette

How to play: Pass water balloons in a circle while music plays. Music stops, whoever's holding it must pop it over their head. If it doesn't pop, drink. If it pops, you're safe but wet.

15. Floatie Flip Cup

How to play: Teams on opposite sides of the pool on floaties. Standard flip cup rules but you must stay on your floatie. Fall off = restart your cup. Winning team pushes losers off their floaties.

16. Marco Polo Drinking Edition

How to play: Classic Marco Polo. When caught, that person becomes "it" AND takes a drink. If "it" says "Marco" and no one is in the pool (fish out of water), everyone out of the pool drinks.

17. Diving Contest

How to play: Judged diving competition. Categories: Best Splash, Most Graceful, Most Creative, Biggest Belly Flop. Each category loser drinks. Belly flop winner (biggest splash) gives out shots.

18. Water Balloon Fight

How to play: Free-for-all or team battle. Last person dry wins and gives out drinks. Anyone who runs out of balloons and stays in the fight = brave but drinks if hit. Fill some balloons with other liquids (sports drinks, etc.) for surprise factor.

🏅 Backyard Olympics

Turn your Labor Day into a mini Olympics with multiple events, point tracking, and ultimate champions.

19. Lawn Olympics Format

Setup: Teams of 2-4. Multiple events. Gold = 3 points, Silver = 2, Bronze = 1. Track total points. Winning team gets medals (or at least bragging rights). Losing team serves drinks for the next hour.

20. Egg & Spoon Race

How to play: Balance an egg on a spoon, race to the finish line. Drop the egg = start over from where you dropped it. Last place drinks. Use hardboiled eggs unless you want chaos.

21. Three-Legged Race

How to play: Partners tied at the ankle, race to finish. Falling = get up and keep going. Last team drinks. First team picks the next event's order.

22. Sack Race

How to play: Classic potato sack race. Fall down = 2 second penalty (count before you can restart). Last place drinks. First place assigns drinks based on finish order.

23. Tug of War

How to play: Team vs. team. Best of 3 pulls. Losing team drinks together. If one team wins 3-0, losing team does shots. Add a mud pit in the middle for extra stakes.

24. Relay Race

How to play: Design your own relay with stations: chug a drink, spin 5 times, do 10 jumping jacks, run to the end, tag the next person. Last team finishes their drinks.

25. Dizzy Bat

How to play: Spin around a bat 10 times, then try to kick a ball through goalposts. Miss = drink and go to back of line. Hit it = give out drinks. Falling over = automatic drink.

☀️ End of Summer Celebration Games

Games that acknowledge the bittersweet reality: summer's ending. Celebrate what was and prepare for what's coming.

26. Summer Bucket List Check

How to play: Everyone shares their summer bucket list items. Drink for each item you DIDN'T accomplish. Give out drinks for each one you did. Most unfinished = do a shot. Most complete = give out a shot.

27. "Best of Summer" Awards

How to play: Categories: Best Beach Day, Best BBQ, Best Road Trip Moment, Worst Sunburn, Best Summer Romance, Best Concert/Festival. Vote on winners. Non-winners in each category drink.

28. Summer Photo Roast

How to play: Everyone shares their most embarrassing/funny summer photo. Group votes on categories: Best Tan Line, Worst Fashion Choice, Best Candid, Most "This Seemed Like a Good Idea." Drink based on votes received.

29. "Things I Won't Miss About Summer"

How to play: Go around sharing things you're glad are ending (humidity, mosquitoes, etc.). If others agree, they drink. If you're alone in your opinion, you drink.

30. Labor Day Predictions

How to play: Everyone predicts something for the next year: Who'll get promoted? Who'll move? Who'll get in a relationship? Write them down, seal them, open next Labor Day. Drink to your predictions.

31. Summer Superlatives

How to play: Vote on who in your group: Had the Best Vacation, Got the Best Tan, Complained Most About Heat, Had the Most Summer Flings, Ate the Most Ice Cream. Winners give out drinks, everyone else drinks.

🤝 Team Competitions

Split the party into teams and let the rivalry fuel the fun.

32. Flip Cup Championship

How to play: Best of 5 rounds. Standard flip cup rules. Winning team picks one person from losing team to chug. Ultimate winners don't have to help clean up.

See also: Flip Cup Drinking Game Rules

33. Relay Race Obstacle Course

How to play: Design an obstacle course: crawl under a net, jump over obstacles, balance on a beam, chug, spin, throw a ball in a bucket. Relay style. Losing team serves winners for 30 minutes.

34. Capture the Flag (Adult Edition)

How to play: Classic capture the flag with drinking stakes. Tagged in enemy territory = do a shot in "jail." Capture the flag = other team chugs. Game ends when one team captures both flags.

35. Balloon Stomp Battle

How to play: Everyone ties a balloon to their ankle. Last team with a balloon un-popped wins. Popped balloon = drink. Winning team gives out drinks equal to balloons they popped.

36. Human Hungry Hippos

How to play: One person lies on a skateboard/scooter, another holds their legs. "Hippo" scoops balls into a basket. Most balls collected wins. Losing team drinks the ball difference.

37. Water Bucket Relay

How to play: Race to fill a bucket by transferring water in small cups, over your head, person to person. Spill it? Too bad. First team to fill their bucket wins. Losers drink whatever water they lost.

🍺 Outdoor Drinking Game Classics

Proven drinking games that work perfectly in outdoor settings.

38. Beer Die

How to play: Throw a die high in the air over a table. Opponents try to catch it with one hand. Lands in cup = chug. Not caught = point. Drop = point for throwing team. First to 5 wins. Play for redemption.

39. Stump

How to play: Each player has a nail in a stump. Flip a hammer, catch it, and try to drive someone else's nail in one strike. Hit your own nail or miss = drink. Drive someone's nail fully in = they chug.

40. Polish Horseshoes (Beersbee)

How to play: Two poles with cups/bottles on top. Throw a frisbee to knock off the opponent's cup. Catch the cup = no points. Cup hits ground = 2 points. Frisbee not caught = 1 point. First to 21, losers drink the difference.

41. Giant Beer Pong

How to play: Buckets instead of cups, kickball or volleyball instead of ping pong balls. Same rules, bigger scale. Great for outdoors. Losing team cleans up the setup.

42. Spikeball Drinking Rules

How to play: Standard Spikeball but: hit the rim = drink. Miss the net = drink. Get aced = drink double. Win the game = assign shots to losing team.

43. Washers

How to play: Toss washers into boxes with holes. Different points for different sections. First to 21 wins. Miss the box entirely = drink. Hit the cup in the center = opponent chugs. Losers finish drinks.

🏃 Active & Sports Games

Get moving and work off some of those BBQ calories.

44. Kickball

How to play: Classic kickball with drinking rules. Strike out = drink. Get tagged out = drink. Score a run = give out drinks. Home run = give out 4 drinks. Winning team = losers do shots.

45. Volleyball

How to play: Rally scoring. Lose a point = rotate and that position drinks. Ace = acing team gives out drinks. Win by 2, play to 15. Losers serve drinks to winners.

46. Frisbee Golf

How to play: Set up targets around the yard. Lowest throws to complete the course wins. Each throw over par = drink. Ace a hole = everyone else drinks. Worst score does a shot.

47. Football Toss

How to play: Set up targets at different distances. Points based on difficulty. Take turns throwing. Lowest score each round drinks. Completely miss everything = drink double.

48. Badminton

How to play: First to 11 wins. Lose a point = drink. Get aced = drink twice. Win = assign drinks equal to the final score difference.

📸 Summer Recap & Memory Games

Reflect on the summer that was with these nostalgic games.

49. Summer Story Slam

How to play: Everyone shares their best summer story (2-minute max). Group votes on: Funniest, Most Adventurous, Most Romantic, Biggest Disaster. Each category winner gives out drinks. Worst storyteller drinks.

50. "Drink If You Did This Summer..."

How to play: Like Never Have I Ever but positive. "Drink if you went to the beach," "Drink if you tried a new restaurant," "Drink if you had a summer fling." Track who drinks most—they had the best summer and get to assign a shot.

51. Photo Memory Challenge

How to play: Show summer photos. Others try to guess: where it was, when it was, what you were celebrating. Closest guess gives out drinks. Way off = drink. Hilarious wrong guesses = everyone drinks.

52. Summer Playlist Battle

How to play: Everyone plays their "song of the summer." Vote on the best one. Non-winners drink. Ultimate winner gets aux control for the next hour.

53. Two Truths and a Lie: Summer Edition

How to play: Two true summer experiences and one made up. Guess the lie. Wrong guesses = drink. Successfully fool everyone = give out drinks to all guessers.

🌅 Sunset & Chill Games

As the sun sets on summer, wind down with these more relaxed games.

54. Sunset Toast Round

How to play: As the sun sets, everyone gives a toast to something from their summer. Clink glasses after each toast. Sip together. Final toast = to the summer that was.

55. Would You Rather: Fall Preview

How to play: Fall-themed would you rather. "Would you rather never rake leaves or never shovel snow?" "Would you rather have endless pumpkin spice or endless apple cider?" Minority opinion drinks.

See also: Would You Rather Drinking Game

56. Labor Day Confessions

How to play: Confess something from your summer you haven't told anyone. Others react. If you can top it with a bigger confession = they drink. If not = you drink.

57. Most Likely To: Fall Edition

How to play: Vote on autumn predictions. "Most likely to be obsessed with pumpkin spice," "Most likely to complain about cold," "Most likely to skip every fall activity." Most votes drinks.

Related: Most Likely To Drinking Game

58. Chill Trivia

How to play: Easy trivia while watching the sunset. Questions about summer movies, songs, pop culture. Wrong answers = gentle sip. No pressure, just vibes.

59. Gratitude Round

How to play: Go around sharing one thing you're grateful for from this summer. After each share, everyone drinks to celebrate it. Simple, warm, and connects the group before saying goodbye to the season.

☀️ Labor Day Party Safety Tips

  • Hydrate between drinks: Late summer heat + alcohol = dehydration risk. Have water stations everywhere.
  • Sunscreen: Even in September, UV is real. Especially if you're playing active games.
  • Pool safety: Drunk swimming is dangerous. Have sober spotters if people are in the water.
  • Food safety: Don't leave BBQ out too long in the heat. Refresh plates regularly.
  • Bug spray: Mosquitoes are still active. Don't let them ruin the party.
  • Plan for rides: If people drove, have a plan. Designated drivers, rideshares, or crash spots.
