Last Splash Pool Games

Labor Day often means the last pool party of the year. Make it memorable with these games that'll have everyone soaking up every final minute of summer.

1. Final Lap Relay Race

Split into teams and race across the pool in various silly styles: backward swimming, pool noodle only, one arm tied behind your back. First team to complete all laps wins bragging rights for the whole off-season.

Drinking twist: Losing team takes a shot of their choice. Winner picks the style for the next round.

2. Pool Closing Ceremony Chicken Fights

Classic shoulder-mounted battles, but with a Labor Day twist. The winner of each round gets to make a "summer decree" that everyone must follow for 10 minutes (within reason!).

3. Watermelon Football

Grease up a watermelon and play football in the pool. The slippery fruit makes catching and throwing hilarious. First team to score 5 touchdowns gets first dibs on dessert.

Pro tip: Use petroleum jelly for maximum slipperiness. The watermelon becomes nearly impossible to hold!

4. Pool Float Musical Chairs

Float pool toys in the middle of the pool—one less than the number of players. When the music stops, everyone swims for a float. No float? You're out (and you drink).

5. Diving Contest: Summer's Best Moments

Each diver has to act out their favorite summer memory mid-dive. Others guess what it was. Points for creativity, dramatic flair, and splash size. Best overall dive wins "Summer MVP."

6. Marco Polo Championship

The classic pool game, but tournament style. Keep eliminating until you have a champion. The final Marco gets to push anyone they want into the pool at any point during the rest of the party.

7. Floatie Flip Cup

Play Flip Cup on pool floaties. Teams balance on inflatable rafts while racing to flip their cups. When someone falls in, they have to get back on and continue. Chaos guaranteed.

8. Last Cannonball Contest

Everyone gets one shot at the biggest, best cannonball they can muster. Judge based on splash height, form, and style points. Winner gets crowned "Cannonball Champion" until next summer.

Worker Bee Drinking Games

Honor the spirit of Labor Day with these work-themed drinking games. After all, this holiday celebrates the workers—so let's raise a glass to the daily grind!

9. Clocking In / Clocking Out

Set specific times throughout the party as "clock in" (everyone starts a new drink) and "clock out" (everyone finishes their current drink). The host is the "boss" and can call impromptu overtime whenever they want.

Bonus: Anyone caught without a drink during work hours has to do a "performance review" (silly challenge chosen by the group).

10. Office Politics

Each player draws a job title from a hat (CEO, Intern, Middle Manager, HR, IT Guy, etc.). Throughout the night, everyone must act in character. Break character? Take a drink.

The CEO can delegate their drinks to the Intern. HR settles disputes. IT "fixes" problems by making others restart their drink.

11. The Union Break

Every 30 minutes, someone calls "Union Break!" Everyone must stop what they're doing, grab a drink, and toast to workers' rights. Anyone who misses the break owes a round.

12. Assembly Line Drinking

Set up stations around the yard. Each station has a mini-challenge (flip a coin, answer trivia, do a physical task). Complete a station, take a sip, move to the next. First to complete all stations wins!

13. The Overtime Challenge

A sudden-death style game. Two players face off with rapid-fire Never Have I Ever questions. First to drink three times loses and has to "work overtime" (fetch drinks for others for 10 minutes).

14. Promotion Pyramid

Build a pyramid of cups like Kings Cup. Each level represents a corporate ladder rung. Draw cards, follow rules, and work your way up. Reach the top cup? You're promoted to "Party VP" and get to make one rule everyone follows.

15. Time Card Trivia

Host asks work-themed trivia (famous CEOs, work movies, labor history). Get it right? "Clock" that answer. Get it wrong? Drink. Person with most correct answers by end of round earns "Employee of the Hour."

16. The Layoff

Musical chairs meets drinking. When the music stops, whoever doesn't find a seat gets "laid off" and has to finish their drink before they can rejoin the next round. Last person standing wins the "Golden Parachute" (a fancy drink of their choice).

Backyard BBQ Classic Games

No Labor Day is complete without classic backyard games. These timeless favorites are perfect for all ages and skill levels—with optional boozy upgrades for the adults.

17. Cornhole Championship

The backyard essential. Set up a tournament bracket and play until you have a champion. Three points for bags through the hole, one for bags on the board. First to 21 wins the round.

Labor Day twist: Decorate boards with summer vs. fall themes. Losing team has to wear "fall" colors (orange, brown) for the rest of the party.

18. Horseshoes Tournament

A Labor Day tradition that dates back generations. Set up stakes and let the clanging begin. Ringers are 3 points, leaners are 2, closest shoe is 1. Play to 21.

19. Ladder Golf (Bolo Toss)

Toss bolas at a three-rung ladder. Top rung scores 3, middle scores 2, bottom scores 1. First to exactly 21 wins—go over and you reset to 15!

20. Giant Yard Dice

Oversized dice for Yahtzee-style play. Roll for combinations like regular Yahtzee but with physical dice you can dramatically throw. Each losing round means a sip.

21. Bocce Ball

Roll your bocce balls closest to the pallino to score. A sophisticated lawn game that pairs perfectly with wine or beer. The team furthest away takes a drink.

22. KanJam

Partner up for this frisbee-into-can game. Direct hits through the slot are instant wins. The fast pace keeps energy high as summer winds down.

23. Spikeball

The trendy 2v2 game that's taken over backyards everywhere. Bounce the ball off the net and try to make opponents miss. Losers take a shot.

24. Croquet

Nothing says "end of summer sophistication" like croquet. Set up wickets and compete to navigate the course first. Drinking rule: Miss your ball entirely? Take a sip.

Summer Sendoff Games

Say goodbye to summer properly with these nostalgic games that celebrate the season's best moments.

25. Summer Superlatives

Everyone writes down nominees for categories like "Best Tan," "Best Beach Story," "Most Mosquito Bites," "Best Pool Cannonball," and "Worst Sunburn." Read aloud, vote, and drink to the winners!

26. Summer Bucket List Bingo

Create bingo cards with summer activities (beach trip, BBQ, outdoor concert, road trip, etc.). Guests mark off what they did this summer. First to bingo wins. Anyone who didn't complete at least 5 items drinks.

27. Photo Finish

Everyone shows their best summer photo on their phone. Group votes on categories: most adventurous, most relaxing, funniest, most "Instagram worthy." Winners in each category give out drinks.

28. Summer Song Roulette

Create a playlist of summer hits. When a song plays, first person to name the title and artist gets to give out a drink. Wrong guess? You drink instead.

29. "This Summer I..." Storytelling

Go around the circle sharing summer stories. After each story, the group votes: truth or exaggeration? If the group guesses wrong, they drink. If they guess right, the storyteller drinks.

30. Sunset Toast Ceremony

As the sun sets, everyone raises a glass and shares one thing they're grateful for from summer. After everyone shares, toast together. It's sentimental, but it sets the perfect end-of-summer mood.

31. Memory Lane Minute-to-Win-It

Quick challenges based on summer themes: stack the most sunscreen bottles, blow up a beach ball fastest, put on flip-flops blindfolded. Fastest time wins; slowest drinks.

Team Tournaments & Competitions

Nothing brings people together like healthy competition. Set up these tournaments and watch the rivalry heat up!

32. Labor Day Olympics

Create a mini-Olympics with multiple events: relay races, egg-and-spoon, three-legged race, balloon toss, and more. Keep score across all events. Winning team gets gold-colored plastic cups for the night.

33. Flip Cup Tournament

The ultimate team drinking game. Line up, chug, flip. First team to flip all cups wins. Check out our complete Flip Cup guide for tournament brackets and variations.

34. Beer Pong Bracket

Set up a Beer Pong tournament with full brackets. Play through until you have champions. Serious bragging rights at stake!

35. Relay Race Madness

Design a crazy relay with different legs: wheelbarrow race, sack race, balloon between knees, carrying a drink without spilling. First complete team wins; last place does a group punishment (like a polar plunge if there's a pool).

36. Tug of War

Classic, simple, and surprisingly intense. Winning team gets bragging rights and first crack at dessert. Losing team fetches drinks for 15 minutes.

37. Trivia Tournament

Teams compete across rounds: summer movies, Labor Day history, sports, and pop culture. Accumulate points; lowest-scoring team at the end handles cleanup duty.

38. Capture the Flag (Adult Edition)

Classic outdoor game, but if you're tagged, you have to take a sip before going to jail. Rescue your teammates and capture the enemy flag to win!

Fall Transition Party Games

Labor Day bridges summer and fall. These games lean into the transition with autumn-inspired twists!

39. Pumpkin Spice Taste Test

Blindfolded players taste various pumpkin spice items (lattes, cookies, beer, etc.) and guess the brand or product. Wrong guesses drink. Right guesses give out drinks. Welcome to fall!

40. Fantasy Football Draft Party

If you're in a fantasy league, host your live draft at the Labor Day party! Each pick comes with trash talk and drink assignments. Last pick of each round takes a shot.

41. Back to School Trivia

Questions about school subjects, famous teachers in movies, and education history. It's like going back to class—but with alcohol.

42. Summer vs. Fall Debate

Split into teams defending summer vs. fall. Each side presents arguments for why their season is better. Audience votes; losing team drinks. Topics: food, activities, weather, holidays.

43. Leaf Pile Jump (If Available)

If you have early-falling leaves, rake them up and take turns jumping in. Judge based on style, enthusiasm, and how many leaves stick to you after. Winner chooses the next game.

44. Bonfire Story Circle

As evening falls, gather around a fire pit. Take turns telling stories—spooky, funny, or nostalgic. The group votes on each story. Best story gets a special drink; worst storyteller tends the fire.

45. Seasonal Cocktail Competition

Teams create a "Goodbye Summer" or "Hello Fall" cocktail using provided ingredients. Blind taste test judging. Winning cocktail becomes the official party drink!

Chill Games for Lazy Days

Sometimes you just want to relax on Labor Day. These low-key games let you hang out without breaking a sweat.

46. Most Likely To

Everyone points to who they think is "most likely to" for various prompts. Whoever gets the most fingers pointed at them drinks. Perfect for lounging in lawn chairs.

47. Never Have I Ever (Summer Edition)

Focus questions on summer experiences: "Never have I ever gotten a speeding ticket on a road trip," "Never have I ever fallen asleep on the beach," etc.

48. Would You Rather

Pose dilemmas and see how people answer. Minority answer drinks. Keep it light or get philosophical—your choice.

49. Cards Against Humanity

The classic inappropriate card game. Perfect for a relaxed evening as the party winds down. Winner of each round gives out sips.

50. Hammock Conversations

Not exactly a "game," but sometimes the best Labor Day activity is just swinging in a hammock, chatting with friends, and sipping drinks. Enjoy the moment!

51. Truth or Dare Lite

Keep it chill with easy-mode truth or dare. Nothing too crazy—just enough to keep things interesting while you all relax.

After Dark Games

When the sun sets on your Labor Day party, these games keep the energy going into the night.

52. Glow Stick Ring Toss

Set up bottles with glow sticks inside. Use glow stick bracelets as rings. Points for successful tosses. The glowing effect makes it extra fun after dark.

53. Flashlight Tag

Classic tag but with flashlights. If you get caught in someone's beam, you're it. Add a drinking rule: if you're it for more than 3 minutes, take a shot.

54. Glow-in-the-Dark Beer Pong

Use glow cups and a glow ball for after-dark Beer Pong. The neon effect takes this classic game to another level.

55. Stargazing Drinking Game

Lay out blankets and stargaze. Every time someone spots a constellation, shooting star, or satellite, everyone else drinks. First to spot 5 things wins.

56. Bonfire Kings Cup

The classic card game played around a fire. The flickering flames add atmosphere to an already legendary drinking game.

57. Ghost Stories Drinking Game

Take turns telling scary stories. When the teller says certain keywords (ghost, dead, darkness, etc.), everyone drinks. Best story wins; lamest story teller fetches s'mores supplies.

58. Lawn Movie Night

Set up a projector and screen a summer classic. Play movie bingo or a drinking game based on the film. Perfect way to wind down the holiday weekend.

Labor Day Party Hosting Tips

Food & Drink

  • Grill variety: Burgers, dogs, brats—but also veggie options and chicken for lighter eaters
  • Potluck style: Ask guests to bring sides, desserts, or specialty drinks
  • Fall preview: Include some autumn-inspired items like apple cider, pumpkin beer, or cinnamon desserts
  • Stay hydrated: Have plenty of water and non-alcoholic options available

Setup

  • Multiple zones: Pool area, lawn games section, shaded seating, food/drink station
  • Comfortable seating: Mix of chairs, blankets, and hammocks
  • Bug control: Citronella candles, fans, and bug spray station
  • Weather backup: Have a tent or indoor backup plan—September weather can be unpredictable

Atmosphere

  • Summer playlist: All the hits from the season for one last play-through
  • Decorations: Mix summer (beach balls, leis) with fall (leaves, warm colors) for the transition vibe
  • Photo booth: Set up props for memorable pictures—last pool day selfies!
  • Sunset timing: Plan key activities around the golden hour for best vibes

Safety First

  • Designated drivers: Arrange rides home or encourage overnight guests
  • Pool supervision: Always have sober eyes on the water when drinking is involved
  • Food temperature: Keep hot foods hot and cold foods cold—foodborne illness is no way to end summer
  • Sun protection: Even late summer sun can burn—provide sunscreen

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Labor Day 2026?

Labor Day 2026 falls on Monday, September 7th. It's always the first Monday in September, giving Americans a three-day weekend to celebrate the end of summer.

What makes a good Labor Day party game?

The best Labor Day party games work for outdoor settings, accommodate various group sizes, and capture the end-of-summer vibe. Games like cornhole, pool activities, and BBQ-themed drinking games are perfect because they're easy to set up, fun for all skill levels, and work great in backyard settings.

What are some Labor Day drinking game ideas?

Worker-themed games like "Clocking In/Clocking Out," "Office Politics," and "Assembly Line Drinking" play on the holiday's labor theme. Classic outdoor drinking games like Flip Cup tournaments, Beer Pong brackets, and cornhole with drinking rules also work perfectly for Labor Day parties.

How do I plan a Labor Day party?

Plan around outdoor activities with a backup for weather. Set up multiple zones: games area, seating, food/drink station, and pool if available. Create a mix of active and relaxed games. Start earlier in the day to maximize daylight, and plan something special for sunset. Have plenty of food, drinks, and shade available.

What are good pool games for Labor Day?

Since it's the last pool party of the season, go all out! Try watermelon football, pool float musical chairs, floatie flip cup, chicken fights, Marco Polo tournaments, and a final cannonball contest. Make it memorable since the pool is closing soon!

Why is Labor Day celebrated?

Labor Day honors the American labor movement and the contributions workers have made to the strength and prosperity of the country. It became a federal holiday in 1894. Today, it also marks the unofficial end of summer and the transition into fall.
