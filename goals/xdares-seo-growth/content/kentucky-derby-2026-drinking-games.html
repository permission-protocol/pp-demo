🐴 Pre-Race Drinking Games

1. Post Position Shots

Each guest draws a number from 1–20 (this year's field size). Every time your horse's post position number is mentioned in the pre-race broadcast, you drink. If your horse scratches, you finish your drink.

2. Derby Bingo

Create 5x5 bingo cards with Derby staples: "mint julep mention," "celebrity sighting," "hat compliment," "rain delay," "crowd roar," "sponsor ad," "Triple Crown talk," "jockey interview," "historic stat," "underdog story." First to bingo takes a shot — or makes a dare.

3. Horse Name Alphabet Game

Go around the circle. Each person names a famous Kentucky Derby horse starting with the next letter of the alphabet. Miss one? Drink. Get stuck on Q or X? Everyone drinks in solidarity.

4. Churchill Downs Countdown

Set a timer for the last 2 minutes before post time. Every 30 seconds, the group must finish a sip. At the 1-minute mark, everyone stands. Anyone who sits before the race ends drinks double.

5. Derby Odds Board

Print the morning-line odds. Drink once for every 5-to-1 favorite you believe is overrated. Argue about it. That's the game.

🎯 Betting Pools & Bracket Games

6. $2 Exacta Pool

Everyone puts in $2 (or whatever feels right) and writes their exacta: the horse they think finishes 1st AND 2nd in order. Closest guess wins the pot. Tiebreaker: whoever guessed the fastest time wins.

7. Trifecta Fantasy Draft

Using a snake draft, guests pick 3 horses each. Points: 1st = 5 pts, 2nd = 3 pts, 3rd = 1 pt. Lowest point total buys the next round. Highest total chooses a dare for the loser.

8. Speed Board Squares

Like Super Bowl squares but for the winning time. Draw a 10x10 grid. Sell squares for $1 each. After filling the board, randomly assign seconds 0–9 to rows and columns. The winning horse's finish time (e.g., 2:01.3 → 1 and 3) determines the winning square.

9. Favorite vs. Field Bet

Guests split into two teams: Team Favorite (bets on the morning-line favorite) vs. Team Field (bets on the rest of the horses). Winning team picks a group dare for the losers.

10. Photo Finish Prediction

Before the race, everyone guesses the margin of victory (by length). Closest guess wins a mint julep made by the host. Farthest off? Drinks a mystery mix.

🏁 Race-Day Drinking Rules

These are the official @xdares_ Kentucky Derby Drink Rules. Read them before the race, or drink for not knowing them.

Drink Once When:

  • The announcer says "Churchill Downs"
  • The camera shows a big hat
  • A horse's name gets mispronounced
  • Anyone asks "which one is yours?"
  • A horse breaks late in the stretch
  • "My Old Kentucky Home" starts playing

Drink Twice When:

  • The favorite is NOT in the lead at the half-mile pole
  • A jockey falls (drink in their honor, they're okay usually)
  • The announcer says "Triple Crown"
  • A long shot (20-to-1 or higher) leads at any point

Finish Your Drink When:

  • Your horse finishes last
  • There's an official inquiry
  • The result is changed by stewards

Assign a Drink When:

  • Your horse wins — you pick who drinks (winner's privilege)
  • You correctly predicted the winner before morning line was posted

👒 Derby Hat Contest Games

The Kentucky Derby is the hat occasion. Make it competitive.

11. Blind Hat Vote

Guests score each hat 1–10 in four categories: Elegance, Creativity, Size, and Absurdity. Scores are tallied secretly. Top hat wins a prize; bottom hat drinks.

12. Hat Runway Challenge

Clear the living room for a 10-second runway walk. Guests vote on who wore their hat best. Losers do a dare from the group's dare deck.

13. Hat Trade Game

Set a 5-minute window before the race. Guests can trade hats with each other — but trades must be agreed on voluntarily. Anyone refusing a reasonable trade drinks. The person with the best-traded hat at race time gets a point bonus.

14. Hat Trivia Prize

Ask 5 Derby hat trivia questions (e.g., "What flower is used in the winning garland?" — roses). One point per correct answer; most points wins bragging rights and picks a truth or dare.

🍹 Mint Julep Challenges

15. Blind Mint Julep Taste-Off

Prepare 3–4 versions: classic bourbon, rye, non-alcoholic, and a "secret" version (add a splash of peach schnapps or elderflower). Guests rank them blind. Whoever guesses all 4 correctly wins.

16. Speed Julep Challenge

First one to correctly muddle, pour, and garnish a mint julep wins the right to sit in the "best seat" for the race. Judge on technique, not just speed.

17. Julep-Off

Everyone makes their own mint julep recipe. Group votes on best, most creative, and "what were you thinking." Winners and losers both assigned dares accordingly.

18. Non-Drinker Mocktail Sprint

Inclusive version: non-drinkers compete to make the best non-alcoholic mint julep (mint lemonade, sparkling water, fresh mint). They judge the alcoholic versions. Fair's fair.

🎲 Derby Truth or Dare

Use these with our full Truth or Dare questions for adults deck, or play standalone:

Derby Truths:

  • "What's the most money you've ever bet on anything — and did you win?"
  • "If you were a racehorse, what would your name be and why?"
  • "Have you ever cried at a sporting event? Don't lie."
  • "What's your secret to picking winners — or is it just vibes?"
  • "What's the weirdest thing you'd do for $10,000?"

Derby Dares:

  • Do your best horse impression for 30 seconds
  • Make up a fake race commentary for the next 60 seconds, narrating the room
  • Wear your hat backwards for the rest of the party
  • Call someone who isn't at the party and explain the Kentucky Derby rules to them
  • Do a victory gallop around the room if your horse wins

🧠 Horse Racing Trivia Game

Easy (1 pt each):

  1. What state hosts the Kentucky Derby? (Kentucky)
  2. What flowers make up the winner's garland? (Roses — it's called the "Run for the Roses")
  3. How long is the Kentucky Derby race? (1¼ miles)

Medium (2 pts each):

  1. What is the official cocktail of the Kentucky Derby? (Mint Julep)
  2. What horse won the last Triple Crown before Justify (2018)? (American Pharoah, 2015)
  3. What year was the first Kentucky Derby? (1875)

Hard (3 pts each):

  1. Name the three races of the Triple Crown in order. (Kentucky Derby, Preakness Stakes, Belmont Stakes)
  2. How many horses have won the Triple Crown? (13)
  3. What's the track record time for the Kentucky Derby? (1:59⅖ — Secretariat, 1973)

🎉 After-Party Games

Post-Race Recap Roast

Each person roasts their own horse pick. Best self-roast wins. "I picked a 50-to-1 shot because his name sounded lucky" earns maximum respect.

Derby Photo Challenge

Best party photo (judged by group vote) wins a prize. Categories: best hat photo, best "victory" reaction, best "devastated loser" reaction.

Loser's Dare Roulette

Everyone whose horse finished outside the top 5 spins a dare wheel (or draws from a dare jar). Standard adult party dares — keep it fun, consensual, and at the group's comfort level.

Double-Down Table Game

For the true degens: set up a card game (blackjack, poker, or even Go Fish for laughs) where the entry fee is finishing your drink. Winner takes the "house" (a pre-agreed prize or dare assignment rights).
