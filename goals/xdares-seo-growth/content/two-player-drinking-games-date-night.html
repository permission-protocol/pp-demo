🃏 Card Games for Two

A standard deck of cards is the ultimate two-player drinking game toolkit. Here are the best card-based drinking games for couples:

1. Higher or Lower

The classic: Simple, fast, and surprisingly addictive.

  • Dealer flips a card face-up
  • Guesser predicts if the next card will be higher or lower
  • Wrong guess = drink
  • Same number = drink twice
  • Switch roles every 5 cards or after 3 correct guesses

Pro tip: Make it spicier by adding "dare" penalties for three wrong guesses in a row.

2. Ride the Bus (Two Player Version)

The gauntlet: Answer four questions, or drink your way through.

  1. Red or Black? Guess the color
  2. Higher or Lower? Than your first card
  3. Inside or Outside? Will the third card fall between or outside the first two?
  4. Suit? Guess the suit (hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades)

Wrong answer at any stage = drink and start over. Take turns being the rider.

4. Pyramid

Memory meets bluffing: Perfect for couples who know each other well.

  • Build a pyramid: 4 cards on bottom, then 3, 2, 1
  • Each player gets 4 cards face-down to memorize
  • Flip pyramid cards one by one, starting from the bottom
  • If you have a matching card (or claim to), assign drinks: 1 for row 1, 2 for row 2, etc.
  • Can bluff! If called out on a bluff, drink double

5. F*ck the Dealer

Beat the odds: One person deals, the other guesses.

  • Dealer holds a card face-down
  • Guesser has two chances to guess the number (A-K)
  • First guess correct: Dealer drinks 4
  • First guess wrong: Dealer says higher or lower
  • Second guess correct: Dealer drinks 2
  • Second guess wrong: Guesser drinks the difference
  • After 3 wrong guesses, dealer changes

6. Indian Poker

Read your partner: Hilarious game of reading reactions.

  • Each player holds a card to their forehead (can see opponent's, not their own)
  • Take turns betting drinks on who has the higher card
  • Can raise, call, or fold
  • Loser drinks the bet amount

The fun is watching your partner's face when they see what you're holding!

7. Speed

Fast reflexes: The card game Speed, but drink when you lose.

  • Play standard Speed rules (race to empty your hand)
  • Loser of each round drinks for 5 seconds
  • First to win 3 rounds picks a "punishment dare" for the loser

8. War

Pure luck: The childhood classic with adult consequences.

  • Split deck evenly
  • Flip cards simultaneously
  • Lower card drinks
  • Tie = "War" — each player puts 3 cards face-down, flip the 4th
  • War loser takes a shot

🎲 Dice Games

All you need is one or two dice for these simple but effective games:

9. Sevens

Lucky seven: Roll two dice and see what happens.

  • Roll 7: Both drink
  • Doubles: Give out drinks equal to the number
  • Even total: You drink that many seconds
  • Odd total: Partner drinks that many seconds

10. Three Man (Modified)

Simplified rules: Works great with just two.

  • Roll to see who's "Three Man" first (lowest roll)
  • Three Man drinks whenever a 3 is rolled or dice equal 3
  • 7: Person to left of roller drinks (your partner)
  • 9: Social, both drink
  • 10: Social, both drink
  • 11: Person to right of roller drinks (your partner)
  • Doubles: Give both dice to partner who rolls and drinks that amount
  • Roll doubles again to pass Three Man title

11. Chō-Han

Japanese casino game: Simple and elegant.

  • One person shakes two dice in a cup, slams it down
  • Other person guesses: Chō (even) or Han (odd)
  • Wrong guess drinks
  • Alternate shaking and guessing

12. Ship, Captain, Crew

Three dice challenge: Strategic rolling.

  • Roll 5 dice, trying to get 6 (Ship), 5 (Captain), 4 (Crew) in order
  • Remaining dice are your "cargo" (points)
  • 3 rolls per turn to get the highest cargo
  • Lower cargo drinks the difference

💬 Verbal & Question Games

No equipment needed—just your voices, your drinks, and your connection:

13. Never Have I Ever (Two Player)

The classic confessional: Learn everything about each other.

  • Take turns saying "Never have I ever..."
  • If you HAVE done it, drink
  • Best with personal, targeted statements
  • Go deeper as the night goes on

Two-player tip: Make statements you think only one of you has done for maximum reveals!

14. Truth or Drink

No dares, just honesty: Answer truthfully or drink.

  • Take turns asking questions
  • Must answer honestly OR take a drink to pass
  • Start light, gradually go deeper
  • No judgment zone

Starter questions:

  • What's the most embarrassing thing you've Googled?
  • What's a secret you've never told me?
  • What's your hottest celebrity crush?
  • What's the weirdest thing you find attractive?
  • Have you ever lied to me? About what?

15. Two Truths and a Lie

Detective drinking: Guess the lie!

  • One person shares two truths and one lie
  • Other person guesses which is the lie
  • Wrong guess = drink
  • Correct guess = storyteller drinks

16. Most Likely To

Point and drink: Gets surprisingly revealing.

  • Ask "Who's most likely to..." questions
  • Both point at who you think
  • Whoever gets pointed at drinks
  • If you both point at the same person, they drink twice

17. Would You Rather

Impossible choices: Drink when you disagree.

  • Take turns asking "Would you rather..."
  • Both answer simultaneously
  • If you choose differently, both drink
  • Same answer = no drinks

18. Name Game / Categories

Quick thinking: Beat the clock.

  • Pick a category (actors, cities, songs, etc.)
  • Take turns naming things in that category
  • 5-second limit per answer
  • Repeat or hesitate = drink
  • First to 3 drinks loses

19. Don't Say It

Banned words: Catch your partner slipping.

  • Each person picks a common word the other can't say (drink, yes, no, I, etc.)
  • Continue normal conversation
  • Every time you catch them saying their word, they drink
  • Game lasts until bottle is empty

20. Story Builder

Improv drinking: Create absurd stories together.

  • One person starts a story with one sentence
  • Other person continues with the next sentence
  • Must make grammatical sense and continue logically
  • Hesitation, contradiction, or "um" = drink
  • Story must include certain trigger words that cause drinks

🎮 Board Game Conversions

Turn your favorite two-player games into drinking games:

24. Drunk Jenga

The tower of drinks: Write rules on the blocks.

  • Pull a block, do what it says
  • Tower falls = finish your drink
  • Write rules like: "Take 2 drinks," "Give 3 drinks," "Truth or drink," "Dare or drink"

25. Chess Boxing (Drinking Edition)

Battle of wits: Strategy meets drinks.

  • Every captured pawn = opponent drinks 1
  • Captured knight/bishop = drink 2
  • Captured rook = drink 3
  • Captured queen = drink 5
  • Check = drink
  • Checkmate = finish drink + winner picks dare

26. Connect Four

Simple but effective: Childhood game, adult stakes.

  • Loser drinks for 10 seconds
  • If you get a Connect 4 in <10 moves, opponent drinks 20 seconds
  • Best of 5 series, overall loser does a dare

27. Battleship

Naval warfare: Drink when your ships sink.

  • Hit = opponent drinks 1
  • Ship sunk = drink equal to ship size
  • Carrier sunk (5) = take a shot
  • All ships sunk = finish drink

28. Scrabble

Word nerds: High-scoring game.

  • Challenged word that's wrong = drink 3
  • Challenged word that's right = challenger drinks 3
  • 50+ point word = opponent takes a shot
  • Use all 7 letters = opponent finishes drink

📱 Phone & App Games

Use your phones for instant drinking game fun:

29. Heads Up!

The party app: Works perfectly with two.

  • One person holds phone to forehead
  • Other person describes the word/phrase
  • Wrong guesses = drink
  • Pass = drink twice
  • Loser of round takes a shot

30. Trivia Apps

Test your knowledge: Any trivia app works.

  • Take turns answering
  • Wrong answer = drink
  • Use lifeline = drink
  • Category you should know but miss = drink twice

31. Drink-If App

Purpose-built: Apps designed for couples drinking games.

Popular apps: Picolo, Drink!, iPuke, Party & Drinking Games

  • Select "couples mode" for intimate questions
  • Follow app prompts
  • Great for when you don't want to think of questions

32. Music Roulette

Spotify shuffle: Test your music knowledge.

  • Shuffle a shared playlist
  • First to name the song = opponent drinks
  • Name the artist too = opponent drinks twice
  • Neither knows = both drink

💕 Romantic & Spicy Games

For couples looking to heat things up—these games blend drinking with intimacy:

33. Strip + Drink

The hybrid: Lose a game, choose your penalty.

  • Play any two-player game from this list
  • Loser chooses: remove clothing item OR take a drink
  • Strategic choices as the game progresses
  • First person fully clothed with empty drink loses

34. Body Shots Challenge

Getting closer: Location-based shots.

  • Take turns flipping a coin
  • Heads: You choose where on your body they take a shot from
  • Tails: They choose where on your body they take a shot from
  • Gradually escalate locations

35. Truth, Drink, or Dare

Three options: The ultimate honesty game.

  • Ask increasingly personal/spicy questions
  • Partner must: Answer truthfully, Take 2 drinks, OR do a dare of your choosing
  • Great for learning new things about each other

36. Dirty Minds

Not what you think: Clean answers, dirty-sounding clues.

  • Give clues that sound dirty but have innocent answers
  • Example: "I go in hard and come out soft" (Answer: Spaghetti)
  • Dirty guess = drink
  • Can't guess = drink twice

37. Couple's Quiz

How well do you know them?

  • Write down questions about each other
  • Take turns asking (What's my biggest fear? Favorite movie? etc.)
  • Wrong answer = drink
  • Touching wrong answers = finish drink

38. Kiss, Marry, Kill (Drinking Edition)

Celebrity edition: Reveal your types.

  • Name three celebrities
  • Partner says kiss, marry, kill for each
  • If you agree on any answer = both drink
  • Completely different answers = person who gave names drinks

🏆 Competitive Challenges

For the couples who turn everything into a competition:

39. Quarters

The classic bounce: Test your aim.

  • Bounce quarter off table into shot glass
  • Make it = opponent drinks
  • Miss = your turn is over
  • Make 3 in a row = make a rule

40. Flip Cup 1v1

Race yourself: Best of series.

  • Set up 3 cups each in a row
  • Race to drink and flip all 3
  • Loser of race drinks an extra cup
  • Best of 7 series

41. Beer Die (Simplified)

Toss and catch: Requires table and die.

  • Toss die across table, must go higher than head height
  • If it hits table and bounces off, opponent tries to catch one-handed
  • Missed catch = drink
  • Die lands in cup = finish drink

42. Power Hour (Mini)

Endurance challenge: Mini-shot every minute.

  • Take a sip of beer every 60 seconds
  • Use a Power Hour playlist with audio cues
  • First to miss a sip drinks double next round
  • Go for 30-60 minutes

43. Video Game Shots

Gaming stakes: Add drinks to any game.

  • Mario Kart: Must finish drink before crossing finish line
  • Fighting games: Loser of round drinks
  • FIFA/Sports: Drink every goal scored against you
  • Any shooter: Drink every death

💡 Tips for Two Player Drinking

Pacing Yourselves

  • Use sips, not gulps — Games are more fun when you can actually play them
  • Keep water nearby — Staying hydrated is key
  • Eat beforehand — Never drink on an empty stomach
  • Know your limits — Tap out when you need to

Best Drinks for Two-Player Games

  • Wine: Perfect for Truth or Drink, movie games
  • Beer: Great for card games, competitive games
  • Cocktails: Ideal for romantic games (but count as double!)
  • Hard seltzer: Light enough for longer games

Setting the Mood

  • Lighting: Dim lights, candles, string lights
  • Music: Background playlist sets the vibe
  • Snacks: Keep munchies within reach
  • Phones away: Be present with each other

Making Memories

  • Take photos throughout the night
  • Keep score across multiple dates
  • Create inside jokes from game moments
  • Develop your own house rules

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What drinking games can you play with just 2 people?

The best two-player drinking games include Higher or Lower, Truth or Drink, Two-Player Kings, Never Have I Ever, Drunk Jenga, and movie/TV drinking games. Card games like Ride the Bus and Pyramid also work well with two players.

What are good drinking games for couples on date night?

Romantic two-player games include Truth or Drink, Couple's Quiz, Strip + Drink, Two Truths and a Lie, and custom movie drinking games. These games encourage conversation and intimacy while keeping the night fun. Check out our date night games guide for more ideas.

How do you pace drinking in two-player games?

Take sips instead of gulps, keep water nearby, eat beforehand, and use lower-alcohol drinks like beer or hard seltzer for longer games. Save shots for special rules or penalties only. Two-player games can get intense, so pace yourself!

What equipment do you need for two-player drinking games?

Most two-player drinking games need minimal equipment. A standard deck of cards covers most card games. Dice, a quarter, or just your voices are enough for many others. Some games use phone apps like Heads Up! or Picolo.

Can you play Kings with only 2 people?

Yes! Modify the rules for two players: 5s and 6s become Partner A drinks and Partner B drinks. Rules requiring groups become challenges between the two of you. The center cup rule for Kings still works perfectly with two—four Kings, one unlucky drinker!
