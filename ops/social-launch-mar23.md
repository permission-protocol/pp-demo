## 1. LinkedIn (Primary)
**When:** Monday 7:45–8:15am PT

**Post:**
KPMG says AI agents need kill switches.

That’s what you build for when things go wrong. A panic button.
But the real question for executives isn't "how do we stop it?" 
It's "who is liable for what it just did?"

If an AI agent can deploy code, move money, or access user data—there must be a Signer of Record and a tamper-evident receipt. 

Not log files. Not dashboards. An explicit, authorized decision.

Everyone is racing to orchestrate agents. We’re building the liability layer that dictates what they’re actually allowed to do. 

No high-risk AI action executes without an explicit human signer.
