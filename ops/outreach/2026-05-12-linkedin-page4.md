## Reply handling (carry over from page 3)

Same patterns as page 3, plus one new one for technical CTO buyers:

If a technical CTO says "we already handle approval in [our IAM / CI / runtime]":

> Fair. The piece I'm finding most stacks don't capture is the specific action authorization at the consequential boundary (the deploy, the spend, the data write), not the user authentication. IAM gates the user, PP gates the action. Worth pressure-testing if the framing lands.

If they say "interesting, send me a one-pager":

> Will do. One-pager covers the policy, blocking, and proof model. Most useful question I could answer in the same note: what's the consequential action your customers are most worried about right now?

That second one drives discovery without forcing a meeting.
