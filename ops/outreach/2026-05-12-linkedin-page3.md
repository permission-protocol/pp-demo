## Voice patterns to keep using

- "What keeps coming up..."
- "The pattern we keep running into..."
- "We keep hearing buyers ask..."
- "[Company] is closer to this than most..." (when sincere)
- "The regulator/auditor question is..."
- "Does this match what you're seeing?"

## Voice patterns to avoid

- "I'm betting..."
- "I think..." / "I believe..."
- "I'm curious..." (too tentative)
- "Worth a quick exchange?" (replaced with "Open to a 15 next week if the framing lands.")
- Any superlative without context
- Fake hook openers ("Loved your post...")
