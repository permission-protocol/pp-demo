## Two tracks

Each contact is one of:

- **Buyer track:** CFOs, finance operators, AI consulting leaders, fractional CFOs. Pitch is "approval and audit gap when agents start taking action".
- **Partner track:** Agent founders and agent platform builders. Pitch is "you build the gas, we build the brakes, and compliance-sensitive customers close faster when we plug in".

Partner messaging is a different posture. Not "are you feeling this pain", but "your buyers are going to ask this, and we're already the answer".

## Approach

Diagnostic question plus product line. Around 45 to 60 words. No links, no Calendly. Asks for a reaction, not a meeting.

Variation rules to avoid sounding AI-written:
- No em dashes anywhere
- Vary the opener (some "thanks for connecting", some lead with the hook, some reference the mutual or post first)
- Vary the question stem (some "quick yes/no", some "curious", some direct)
- One personalization token max per message
- Straight quotes, contractions, mixed sentence length
- Some opinion in the voice, not neutral reporting

## LinkedIn message titles

LinkedIn DMs require a subject line on the first touch. Rules:
- Under 50 characters
- Specific to them or to the question, not a generic sales line
- No "Hi" or "Hello"
- Treat it like an email subject from one founder to another

Send rate: 30 to 40 a day, not all 97 at once. LinkedIn throttles, and reply pipeline beats blast volume.

## Reply handling

If a buyer-track contact says "yes, this is real":

> Helpful. Which action is on your mind the most: approvals, payments, customer data access, deploys, or workflow triggers?

If a partner-track contact says "yes, we hear this from buyers":

> Helpful. Which buyer segment is pushing hardest, finance, security, compliance, or platform? Worth a 15-minute call to map where PP could plug in?

If they say "still too early":

> Fair. Are the agents in your stack still mostly read-only and drafting, or can any of them call tools that change state?

If they say "tell me more":

> Permission Protocol blocks high-risk AI actions until the right human or policy signs the exact action, then issues a tamper-evident receipt. Worth a 15-minute teardown this week? Sharp feedback over sales pitch.

## Do not include in first message

- Calendly or calendar links
- Demo offers
- "Cryptographically signed" (engineer-speak for CFOs)
- "Pilot cohort" framing (premature)
- Em dashes
- "Exactly that" or any phrase repeated across messages
