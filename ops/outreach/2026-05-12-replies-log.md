## Voice for the call with Art

When Art asks "so what is Permission Protocol exactly," 60-second answer:

> External signoff layer. When an AI agent tries to take a consequential action — a deploy, a financial approval, a customer data change — PP blocks it, routes the exact action to the right human, captures their explicit approval, and emits a tamper-evident receipt. The receipt is the audit trail that says exactly who signed off on what.
>
> Three layers: define the policy, block the action, prove the approval.
>
> Where it matters most for CFOs is the proof layer. When your auditor asks who authorized this $250k vendor change, you have a signed receipt, not a Slack screenshot.
